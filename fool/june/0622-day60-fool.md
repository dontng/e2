## 原句

### fool-01
> The whirlwind that swept through newsrooms harmed everybody, but much of the damage has been concentrated in areas where newspapers are least distinctive.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **whirlwind**：whirl（旋转）+ wind（风）→ 打着旋儿猛扫的风。核心意象：来得快、范围大；新闻语境比喻行业剧变，不是气象「台风」。
- **harm**：造成伤害、损害；比 hurt 书面，可接抽象对象。
- **damage**：损害的结果/破坏面，比 harm 更偏「造成的破坏量」。
- **distinctive**：dis-（分开）+ stinct（同 distinguish）→ 能区分开。核心意象：有特色；least distinctive = 最同质化。注意≠ distribute（分发）。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **swept through newsrooms**：swept through / swept across。中文感受：像风一样扫过整个新闻界，覆盖面广、没有幸免。
- **has been concentrated in areas**：concentrated in areas / concentrated in regions。中文感受：损害「落在」特定领域——被动，不是主动聚集，是「砸」到了那里。

**Step 3 扫句式**
主干一：The whirlwind harmed everybody——旋风伤了所有人；that swept through newsrooms 挂在 whirlwind 后，说明什么样的旋风。
but 换重心：much of the damage 作主语，has been concentrated in areas——大部分损害落在哪些领域。
where newspapers are least distinctive 限定 areas：报纸特色最不突出的那些领域。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：一股扫过新闻界的旋风伤了所有人，但大部分损害落在报纸最没特色的那些领域。
考场：这股席卷新闻界的旋风伤害了所有人，但大部分损害集中在报纸特色最不鲜明的领域。

## Vocab · whirlwind

### fool-02 [扣原句]
> The policy changes created a whirlwind of confusion across every department in the company.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **confusion**：混乱、晕头转向。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **a whirlwind of confusion**：a whirlwind of / a whirlwind of chaos。中文感受：「一阵混乱的风暴」——of 把 whirlwind 和它造成的混乱绑成一团。

**Step 3 扫句式**
主干 policy changes created a whirlwind of confusion；across every department 说明波及范围——横跨每个部门。政策一变，公司全员陷混乱。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：政策一变，公司每个部门都被卷进一团混乱。
考场：政策变动在公司各部门引发了一阵混乱。

### fool-03 [迁移]
> Investors faced a whirlwind week as three major banks announced mergers within five days.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **investor**：投资者。
- **merger**：合并、并购。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **a whirlwind week**：a whirlwind week / a whirlwind month。中文感受：whirlwind 修饰 week，说这周节奏飞快像旋风——hectic。

**Step 3 扫句式**

### fool-04 [防坑]
> He translated whirlwind as "typhoon" and missed that the sentence was about media upheaval, not weather.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **upheaval**：动荡、剧变——比 change 更猛烈，行业/社会级别的翻腾。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **translated … as "typhoon"**：translated whirlwind as / read whirlwind as。中文感受：as 表「当作、译成」。
首译典型错读：whirlwind 想到旋风/台风（气象义）就落笔；本句纠偏：新闻句里是行业动荡的比喻，不是天气灾害。

**Step 3 扫句式**
He translated whirlwind as "typhoon" 是第一错；and missed that 第二错——that 从句说出的是 media upheaval（媒体动荡），not weather，两者被明确对比。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：他把 whirlwind 翻成台风，没看出那句说的是媒体动荡不是天气。

## Vocab · sweep

### fool-05 [扣原句]
> New regulations swept through the industry and forced dozens of small firms to close within months.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **regulation**：法规、规章。
- **firm**：公司、企业（这里不是「牢固」的意思）。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **swept through the industry**：swept through / swept across。中文感受：法规像风一样扫过整个行业，覆盖面广、无一幸免。
- **forced … to close**：forced firms to close / forced them to close。中文感受：force + 人/机构 + to do，被迫关闭。

**Step 3 扫句式**
主语 New regulations，and 连接两个谓语：swept through the industry（席卷行业）+ forced dozens of small firms to close（逼退小公司）。within months 强调快。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：新法规席卷行业，几个月内逼退几十家小公司。
考场：新法规席卷行业，迫使数十家小企业在数月内关闭。

### fool-07 [防坑]
> She wrote "席过" instead of "席卷" and added "corners" that were not in swept through newsrooms.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **corner**：角落——注意原文 swept through newsrooms 里没有 corners 这个意象。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **instead of "席卷"**：instead of 席卷 / instead of 席扫。中文感受：instead of 说明本该写什么、实际写了什么。

**Step 3 扫句式**
两个错：① wrote "席过" instead of "席卷"——词译偏；② added "corners" that were not in swept through newsrooms——自行添加原文没有的意象。and 连接两件错事。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：她把 sweep 写成「席过」不是「席卷」，还加了原文没有的「边角」。

## Vocab · harm

### fool-08 [扣原句]
> The merger harmed employees at both companies, but the pain was worst in departments with overlapping roles.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **merger**：合并、并购。
- **overlapping**：重叠的——两个部门功能交叠、角色冗余。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **at both companies**：at both / at either。中文感受：at 强调所属，双方公司都受影响，没有哪边幸免。
- **with overlapping roles**：with overlapping roles / with redundant roles。中文感受：with 引出部门特征，岗位重叠的。

**Step 3 扫句式**
前半 The merger harmed employees at both companies——合并伤了双方员工；but 转：the pain was worst in departments with overlapping roles——最惨是岗位重叠部门。对比：都受伤，但重叠部门最重。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：合并伤了双方员工，但岗位重叠的部门最惨。
考场：合并对两家公司员工均造成伤害，但职责重叠的部门受创最重。

### fool-09 [迁移]
> Critics argued that the subsidy cuts would harm rural schools more than urban ones.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **subsidy cut**：补贴削减。
- **rural / urban**：农村 / 城市。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **harm … more than**：harm rural more than urban / harm X more than Y。中文感受：more than 连接比较的两端，对……伤害更大。

**Step 3 扫句式**
主句 Critics argued that；从句 the subsidy cuts would harm rural schools more than urban ones——削减补贴更伤农村学校；urban ones = urban schools，避免重复。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：批评者说砍补贴对农村学校伤害更大。
考场：批评者认为削减补贴对农村学校的伤害将超过城市学校。

### fool-10 [防坑]
> His translation described the "sweep" but left out harmed everybody entirely.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **entirely**：完全、彻底——强调漏得干干净净，没有一点保留。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **left out … entirely**：left out harmed everybody entirely / left out … completely。中文感受：leave out = 漏掉，entirely 强调整段消失。
首译典型错读：只抓住 sweep 的动感，harmed everybody 整段跳过；本句纠偏：sweep 和 harm everybody 是连续两件事，不能只译前半。

**Step 3 扫句式**
His translation described the "sweep" 是译了的部分；but left out harmed everybody entirely 是漏掉的——but 点明割裂，两件事各说一件。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：他译文写了「扫过」，harmed everybody 整段没有。

## Vocab · concentrate

### fool-11 [扣原句]
> Job losses were spread nationwide, but much of the pain has been concentrated in manufacturing towns.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **spread**：分散、蔓延——were spread 被动，失业面广。
- **manufacturing**：制造业的。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **much of the pain has been concentrated in**：much of the pain concentrated in / much of the damage concentrated in。中文感受：大部分痛楚「落在」某处——被动，和原句 much of the damage concentrated in areas 同骨架。

**Step 3 扫句式**
对比结构：Job losses were spread nationwide（分散，面广）but much of the pain has been concentrated in manufacturing towns（集中，点深）。在对比里感受 concentrate 的「收拢」vs spread 的「铺开」。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：失业全国都有，但大部分痛楚集中在制造业城镇。
考场：失业潮波及全国，但大部分冲击集中于制造业城镇。

### fool-12 [迁移]
> Public anger concentrated on the officials who had promised reform but delivered nothing.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **official**：官员。
- **reform**：改革。
- **deliver**：兑现、落实——这里不是「递送」，是「做到」。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **concentrated on**：concentrated on / focused on。中文感受：on 表落点，怒火「指向」某人/某处。
- **promised … but delivered nothing**：promised reform but delivered nothing。中文感受：承诺 + 什么都没做到，but 前后反差强烈。

**Step 3 扫句式**
主干 Public anger concentrated on the officials；who 从句修饰 officials：had promised reform but delivered nothing——光说不做。anger 的落点 = 「光说不做的官员」。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：公众怒火集中在那些承诺了改革却什么都没做到的官员身上。
考场：公众愤怒集中于承诺改革却毫无兑现的官员。

### fool-13 [防坑]
> He caught "集中" but tied it to "伤害最多" instead of damage concentrated in specific areas.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **specific**：特定的、具体的——指明是哪些 area，不是泛指。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **tied … to**：tied it to 伤害最多 / tied it to the wrong noun。中文感受：tie to = 挂在……上，绑定对象错了。
首译典型错读：concentrated 的主语是 damage，落点是 specific areas；本句纠偏：不是「伤害最多」，是「损害集中在特定领域」，主语和宾语都偏了。

**Step 3 扫句式**
He caught "集中" 是对的一半；but tied it to "伤害最多" 是误——主语（damage）和落点（areas）都偏了；instead of damage concentrated in specific areas 点明正确读法。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：他译出了「集中」，但挂在「伤害最多」上，不是「损害落在特定领域」。

## Vocab · distinctive

### fool-14 [扣原句]
> The paper survived because its local reporting remained distinctive in a market full of identical national feeds.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **survive**：存活、活下来。
- **identical**：一模一样的、完全相同的。
- **feed**：供稿、稿源——这里不是「喂食」，是新闻稿来源。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **remained distinctive**：remained distinctive / stayed distinctive。中文感受：remain 表「一直维持」，特色没有消失。
- **full of identical national feeds**：full of / packed with。中文感受：市场里到处是雷同的全国稿源，full of 强调充斥。

**Step 3 扫句式**
主句 The paper survived，because 引原因从句：local reporting remained distinctive——本地报道有特色。in a market full of identical national feeds 是背景——周围全是同质内容，反衬「有特色」的稀缺价值。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：市场上都是雷同全国稿，这份报纸靠本地报道有特色活下来了。
考场：该报得以存续，因为在同质化全国稿源充斥的市场中其本地报道仍保持鲜明特色。

### fool-15 [迁移]
> Wine labels with distinctive designs sell better even when the price is higher than plain bottles.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **label**：标签、酒标。
- **plain**：朴素的、无装饰的。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **with distinctive designs**：with distinctive / with unique。中文感受：with 引设计特征，有特色的设计。

主干 Wine labels with distinctive designs sell better；even when the price is higher than plain bottles 是让步从句——就算更贵也卖得好。对比：特色设计 vs 普通包装。

### fool-16 [防坑]
> He read distinctive as distribute and translated "areas where papers are least distinctive" as places to deliver newspapers.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **distribute**：分发、配送——和 distinctive 形近易混，词尾 -ute（分发）vs -ive（特色）。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **read … as distribute**：read distinctive as / translated distinctive as。中文感受：as 表「当成、读成」，认错了词。
首译典型错读：distinctive（-ive 特色）看成 distribute（-ute 分发），整个 where 从句滑向「配送地点」；本句纠偏：先看词尾 -ive/-ute 辨义，distinctive ≠ distribute。

**Step 3 扫句式**
两件错：① read distinctive as distribute——词认错；② translated "areas where papers are least distinctive" as places to deliver newspapers——整个从句滑向配送。因果：词混 → 从句全偏。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：他把 distinctive 看成 distribute，把「报纸最没特色的领域」译成「送报纸的地方」。

## Phrases · whirlwind that swept through…

### fool-17 [扣原句]
> The recession that swept through manufacturing left small suppliers with nowhere to turn for credit.

**Step 2 扫词块**

- **with nowhere to turn for credit**：with nowhere to turn / with no place to turn。中文感受：with 引困境，找不到地方求贷。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：席卷制造业的衰退让小供应商找不到地方贷款。
考场：席卷制造业的衰退使小型供应商无处寻求信贷。

## Phrases · has been concentrated in areas where…

### fool-20 [扣原句]
> Much of the funding has been concentrated in cities where universities are strongest, leaving rural labs underfunded.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **funding**：资金。
- **underfunded**：资金不足的——un- 前缀否定。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **Much of the funding has been concentrated in**：Much of the funding concentrated in / Much of the resources concentrated in。中文感受：被动，资金「落在」某些城市——和原句 much of the damage concentrated in areas 完全同骨架。
- **leaving rural labs underfunded**：leaving X underfunded / leaving X short。中文感受：leaving + 结果，伴随后果——资金去了强校城市，农村实验室就穷了。

**Step 3 扫句式**
主干 Much of the funding has been concentrated in cities where universities are strongest；where 从句限定城市类型；leaving rural labs underfunded 是伴随结果状语。整句模板：much of … has been concentrated in … where … , leaving … 。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：大部分钱集中在大学最强的城市，农村实验室就没了资金。
考场：大部分资金集中于大学实力最强的城市，导致农村实验室资金匮乏。

### fool-21 [迁移]
> Pollution has been concentrated in valleys where factories cluster and wind rarely clears the air.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **pollution**：污染。
- **valley**：山谷。
- **cluster**：聚集、扎堆。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **has been concentrated in valleys**：concentrated in valleys / concentrated in areas。中文感受：被动，污染「落在」山谷——主语是污染本身，不是人聚集它。
- **wind rarely clears the air**：wind rarely clears / wind seldom disperses。中文感受：rarely 强调风无力散污，clears the air = 净化/吹散空气。

**Step 3 扫句式**
主干 Pollution has been concentrated in valleys；where 从句限定哪种山谷：① factories cluster（工厂扎堆）and ② wind rarely clears the air（风散不走）。两个原因叠加，解释为什么 concentrated in this type of valley。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：污染集中在工厂扎堆、风又吹不散的山谷里。
考场：污染集中于工厂聚集且风难以净化空气的山谷地区。

### fool-22 [防坑]
> The translator missed everybody in the first half and only loosely kept "concentrated" in the second.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **loosely**：勉强地、松散地——译得不到位，没有牢牢抓住结构。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **missed … in the first half**：missed everybody in the first half / missed X in part one。中文感受：漏掉了某个关键成分，first half 对应原句 but 之前。
首译典型错读：前半只抓住 sweep，everybody 整段丢；后半只留「集中」二字，damage 和 areas 都没挂住；本句纠偏：前后两半都要完整——everybody 不能丢，damage concentrated in areas 要落实。
- **only loosely kept**：only loosely kept / barely kept。中文感受：勉强留着，结构散了。

**Step 3 扫句式**
两个问题 and 连接：① missed everybody in the first half——前半漏译；② only loosely kept "concentrated" in the second——后半也译不准。对应原句 but 前后两部分都出错。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：译者前半漏了 everybody，后半只勉强留了「集中」。

## Phrases · least distinctive

### fool-23 [扣原句]
> Chain stores are least distinctive in suburbs where every mall offers the same brands.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **suburb**：郊区。
- **brand**：品牌。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **least distinctive in suburbs**：least distinctive in / least distinctive among。中文感受：in 表「在某类地方」，least distinctive = 最没特色、最同质化的场合。
- **where every mall offers the same brands**：where every mall / where all shops。中文感受：where 从句解释为什么 least distinctive——品牌全一样，没什么能区分。

**Step 3 扫句式**
主干 Chain stores are least distinctive in suburbs；where every mall offers the same brands 修饰 suburbs，说明为什么郊区最没特色。因果在 where 里：品牌都一样 → 连锁店看不出差别。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：在每家商场品牌都雷同的郊区，连锁店最没特色。
考场：在各商场品牌相同的郊区，连锁店特色最为匮乏。

### fool-24 [迁移]
> Local papers are least distinctive when they rely entirely on syndicated national columns.

**Step 1 扫词**
- **syndicated**：联合供稿的、转载的——多家报纸共用同一来源的内容。
- **column**：专栏。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **rely entirely on**：rely entirely on / depend entirely on。中文感受：entirely 加重依赖程度，完全靠……一点自己的东西都没有。
- **syndicated national columns**：syndicated national / syndicated wire。中文感受：syndicated = 转载/联合供稿，national 表全国性而非本地原创。

**Step 3 扫句式**
主干 Local papers are least distinctive；when 引条件：they rely entirely on syndicated national columns——全靠转载全国专栏。没有自己的本地内容 → 最同质化。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：地方报要是全靠转载全国专栏，就最没特色。

### fool-25 [防坑]
> Distinctive ends with -ive (特色)；distribute ends with -ute (分发)—mixing them sends the clause to "delivery routes".

**Step 1 扫词**
- **clause**：从句——这里指 where newspapers are least distinctive 整个定语从句。
- **delivery route**：配送路线——误读的产物，distinctive 被看成 distribute 之后跑去的地方。

**Step 2 扫词块**
- **mixing them sends … to**：mixing them sends the clause to / confusing them leads to。中文感受：混淆两词的后果——整个从句跑偏到「配送路线」。
首译典型错读：distinctive（-ive 特色）认成 distribute（-ute 分发），整个 where 从句滑向「配送地点」；本句纠偏：先看词尾 -ive（特色）/-ute（分发），distinctive ≠ distribute。

**Step 3 扫句式**
分号前两个后缀对比；破折号后 mixing them sends the clause to "delivery routes"——说明混用后果。整句是辨词口诀 + 后果警告，简洁。

**Step 4 读句子**
直读：distinctive 词尾 -ive 是特色，distribute 词尾 -ute 是分发，混了整个从句就跑到「配送路线」去了。
