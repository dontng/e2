# Day 60 on 2026-06-22

## 原句

> The whirlwind that swept through newsrooms harmed everybody, but much of the damage has been concentrated in areas where newspapers are least distinctive.

## 我的理解和翻译

台风猛烈席扫过新闻室里的边边角角，但伤害最多的是集中在报纸要分发的地方

## 批改

```
台风猛烈席扫过新闻室里的边边角角，但伤害最多的是集中在报纸要分发的地方
```

**[1] whirlwind 译成"台风"**
断在词义被字典第一条锁死了。whirlwind 字面是旋风，但英语里极常用作比喻，指席卷一切的剧变或冲击。这句放在媒体行业语境里，"台风"变成了实体自然灾害，而原句讲的是某种行业冲击（数字化浪潮/媒体寒冬），整个语境就跑偏了。

**[2] "新闻室里的边边角角"**
断在对 swept through 的方向理解反了。swept through 是"席卷……整个范围"，强调波及之广、无一幸免。"边边角角"反而暗示只影响角落，与 harmed everybody 的意思完全矛盾。

**[3] distinctive 译成"分发"**
断在形近词混淆：把 distinctive（有特色的、有区别度的）读成了 distribute（分发）的某个变体。这是这次最致命的错误——核心语义完全丢失。least distinctive 是"最缺乏差异性/竞争力最弱"，整句的因果逻辑就在这里：受损最重的恰恰是那些自己没什么独特性的领域。

**[4] "伤害最多的是集中在"语序**
方向基本对，但 much of the damage has been concentrated 的重心是"大部分损害集中于……"，学生译文把"集中"变成了状语修饰，稍微弱化了原句强调"集中程度"的意图。这是小偏差，不是根本性错误。

**参考译文**

这场席卷各大新闻编辑室的旋风伤害了所有人，但大部分损害集中在报纸最缺乏差异性的领域。

## Vocab

**distinctive** *adj.*
词根 -distinct- 来自拉丁语 distinguere（刺开、分隔），加 -ive 后变形容词，指"能把自己和别人区分开来的"。核心意象：人群里一眼能认出来的那个——不是因为大，是因为不一样。

1. Her voice is so distinctive that you can recognize her on the radio after hearing just one sentence.
2. In a market saturated with generic content, a publication's distinctive editorial voice is the only reason readers return week after week.
3. The brand's *distinctive* logo sets it apart from its *distinct* rivals—one word describes character, the other marks separation.

---

**whirlwind** *n. / adj.*
whirl（旋转）+ wind（风），字面是旋风。英语里大量用作比喻，指速度极快、席卷一切、让人来不及反应的变化或事件。核心意象：站在龙卷风中心，四面都是碎片在飞。

1. The merger triggered a whirlwind of regulatory scrutiny, shareholder lawsuits, and executive departures all within a single quarter.
2. The whirlwind of automation that swept through manufacturing left millions of mid-skill workers without transferable options.
3. A *whirlwind* romance is thrilling precisely because it moves too fast to question—unlike a *hurricane*, which implies pure destruction, *whirlwind* keeps a sense of energy and momentum.

## Phrases

**sweep through** *phr. v.*
sweep（清扫、横扫）+ through（穿越整个范围）。合读之后：从一头彻底扫到另一头，没有死角，强调全面波及。核心意象：一把大扫帚从头扫到尾，经过的地方全被影响。

1. A wave of consolidation swept through the banking sector, leaving only a handful of mega-institutions standing.
2. The same anxiety that swept through print media a decade ago is now sweeping through local television.
3. The storm *swept through* the valley in minutes—compare this to *swept over*: "swept over" often implies a feeling washing across you, while "swept through" stresses moving across a physical or institutional space end to end.

---

**be concentrated in** *phr.*
concentrate 本义是"聚集到中心点"，被动式 be concentrated in 强调结果状态：本来分散的东西，最终聚集堆积在某个区域。核心意象：漏斗把所有东西导向同一个出口。

1. Wealth in that country is so concentrated in the top decile that the bottom half holds virtually no assets.
2. Much of the reputational damage from the scandal has been concentrated in the company's consumer-facing divisions, where trust is hardest to rebuild.
3. Unemployment is *concentrated in* coastal manufacturing towns—this is different from *centered on*, which suggests a focal point; *concentrated in* emphasizes density and accumulation.
