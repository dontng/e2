# Day 60 on 2026-06-22

## 原句

> The whirlwind that swept through newsrooms harmed everybody, but much of the damage has been concentrated in areas where newspapers are least distinctive.

## 我的理解和翻译

台风猛烈席扫过新闻室里的边边角角，但伤害最多的是集中在报纸要分发的地方

## 批改

```
台风[1]猛烈席扫过新闻室里的边边角角[2]，但伤害最多的是集中在报纸要分发[3]的地方
```

**[1] whirlwind** 这里是比喻「席卷行业的剧变/风暴」，不是气象意义上的「台风」。旋风意象是「转得快、扫得猛」，但落点是新闻业动荡，不是天气灾害。

**[2] swept through newsrooms harmed everybody** 三件事叠在一起：① sweep = **席卷**（「席」字错了），是快速扫过整个新闻界；② newsrooms 可译「新闻编辑室/新闻界」；③ **everybody 你整句漏掉了**——原句明确说伤了「所有人/各方」。「边边角角」是自行加的，原文没有 corners 这层意思。

**[3] areas where newspapers are least distinctive** 这是整句后半的主干错读。distinctive = **有特色的、与众不同的**；least distinctive = 特色**最不**突出、最同质化的领域。你译成「要分发」，是把 distinctive 和 distribute（分发）混了——形近/联想岔路，整段定语从句方向全偏。

**参考译文：** 这股席卷新闻界的旋风伤害了所有人，但大部分损害集中在报纸特色最不鲜明的领域。

## 评分

**2 / 10**

采分点（5点）：
- ✗ whirlwind（旋风/行业风暴） → 用户译「台风」，气象义取代比喻义
- △ swept through newsrooms（席卷新闻界） → 用户译「猛烈席扫过新闻室」，「袭」写错；加了「边边角角」
- ✗ harmed everybody（伤害了所有人） → 完全漏译，只译了 sweep 的「扫过」画面
- △ much of the damage has been concentrated（大部分损害集中在） → 用户译「伤害最多的是集中在」，damage/集中部分沾边，但主语换成「伤害」且 everybody 已丢
- ✗ least distinctive（特色最不鲜明） → 用户译「要分发的」，distinctive≠distribute，定语从句整段误读

## Vocab

### whirlwind *(n.)* · [🔊](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/whirlwind)

**词根**：whirl（旋转）+ wind（风）= 打着旋儿的风

**核心意象**：不是普通风，是「转着圈猛扫」——来得快、范围大、把人卷进去；新闻/政治里常比喻突然的行业剧变。

- *The policy changes created a whirlwind of confusion across every department in the company.* （公司各部门被一阵政策变动搞得晕头转向）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — whirlwind 比喻行业动荡，非天气
- *Investors faced a whirlwind week as three major banks announced mergers within five days.* （五天里三家大行宣布合并，投资者经历混乱一周）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 英二商业报道常见比喻
- *He translated whirlwind as "typhoon" and missed that the sentence was about media upheaval, not weather.* （他把 whirlwind 译成台风，没看出说的是媒体动荡而非天气）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[1]/✗ 气象义取代比喻义

### sweep *(v.)* · [🔊](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/sweep)

**词根**：与 swipe、swipe 同属「快速划过」意象

**核心意象**：像扫帚/旋风一样**一扫而过**，强调覆盖面广、速度快，不是轻轻碰一下。

- *New regulations swept through the industry and forced dozens of small firms to close within months.* （新法规席卷行业，数月内逼退数十家小公司）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — sweep through + 行业/场所
- *A wave of layoffs swept through the tech sector after the earnings reports disappointed Wall Street.* （财报不及预期后，裁员潮席卷科技业）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 经济报道
- *She wrote "席过" instead of "席卷" and added "corners" that were not in swept through newsrooms.* （她把 sweep 写成「席过」，还给 swept through newsrooms 加了原文没有的「边角」）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[2]/△ 袭字与漏 everybody

### harm *(v.)* · [🔊](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/harm)

**核心意象**：造成伤害、损害——可以是身体、利益、声誉；比 hurt 更书面，常接抽象对象。

- *The merger harmed employees at both companies, but the pain was worst in departments with overlapping roles.* （合并伤了双方员工，重叠岗位部门最惨）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — harmed everybody 的「伤及各方」
- *Critics argued that the subsidy cuts would harm rural schools more than urban ones.* （批评者称削减补贴对农村学校伤害更大）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 政策评论
- *His translation described the "sweep" but left out harmed everybody entirely.* （他的译文写了「扫过」，却把 harmed everybody 整段漏掉）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[2]/✗ 漏译 everybody

### concentrate *(v.)* · [🔊](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/concentrate)

**词根**：con-（一起）+ center（中心）= 聚到中心点

**核心意象**：分散的东西**收拢、集中**到一点或一片区域；concentrated in = 集中落在……

- *Job losses were spread nationwide, but much of the pain has been concentrated in manufacturing towns.* （失业全国都有，但大部分痛楚集中在制造业城镇）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — much of the damage concentrated in areas
- *Public anger concentrated on the officials who had promised reform but delivered nothing.* （公众怒火集中在承诺改革却毫无兑现的官员身上）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 社会评论
- *He caught "集中" but tied it to "伤害最多" instead of damage concentrated in specific areas.* （他抓到「集中」，却挂在「伤害最多」上，而非损害落在特定领域）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[3]/△ 集中对象偏了

### distinctive *(adj.)* · [🔊](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/distinctive)

**词根**：dis-（分开）+ stinct（刺/点，同 distinguish）= 能区分开的、有特色的

**核心意象**：**与众不同、有特色**——能一眼和其他东西区分开；least distinctive = 最没特色、最同质化。≠ distribute（分发）。

- *The paper survived because its local reporting remained distinctive in a market full of identical national feeds.* （这份报纸活下来，因为同质化的全国稿源里它的本地报道仍有特色）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — newspapers least distinctive 同质化领域
- *Wine labels with distinctive designs sell better even when the price is higher than plain bottles.* （设计有特色的酒标即使价高也更好卖）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 商业/消费
- *He read distinctive as distribute and translated "areas where papers are least distinctive" as places to deliver newspapers.* （他把 distinctive 看成 distribute，把 least distinctive 译成报纸要分发的地方）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[3]/✗ distinctive≠distribute

## Phrases

### whirlwind that swept through…（席卷…的旋风/风暴）

**讲解**：whirlwind 后接定语从句 that swept through newsrooms，整团「旋风」修饰语说明是**什么样的**旋风——扫过新闻界的。读的时候：先抓住 whirlwind harmed… 主干，再挂 that 从句。

- *The recession that swept through manufacturing left small suppliers with nowhere to turn for credit.* （席卷制造业的衰退让小供应商无处求贷）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — 名词 + that swept through + 领域
- *The scandal that swept through the ministry forced three senior officials to resign within a week.* （席卷该部的丑闻一周内逼三名高官辞职）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 政治报道
- *Do not turn whirlwind that swept through newsrooms into a weather report about typhoons.* （别把席卷新闻界的 whirlwind 翻成台风气象报道）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[1][2]/✗△

### has been concentrated in areas where…（损害/冲击集中在…领域）

**讲解**：much of the damage 是主语，has been concentrated in areas 是被动——损害**被集中**到某些 area；where 从句限定是**哪类** area：报纸 least distinctive 的那些。

- *Much of the funding has been concentrated in cities where universities are strongest, leaving rural labs underfunded.* （大部分资金集中在大学最强的城市，农村实验室缺钱）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — much of… concentrated in areas where
- *Pollution has been concentrated in valleys where factories cluster and wind rarely clears the air.* （污染集中在工厂扎堆、风又吹不散的山谷）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 环境报道
- *The translator missed everybody in the first half and only loosely kept "concentrated" in the second.* （译者前半漏 everybody，后半只勉强保留「集中」）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[2][3]/✗△ 半句译半句丢

### least distinctive（特色最不突出 / 最同质化）

**讲解**：distinctive 是「有区分度的」；least 拉到极致 = **最不**有特色。新闻业语境里常指内容同质化、和全国稿没差别的地方媒体。

- *Chain stores are least distinctive in suburbs where every mall offers the same brands.* （郊区商场品牌雷同，连锁店最没特色）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — least distinctive 领域
- *Local papers are least distinctive when they rely entirely on syndicated national columns.* （地方报全靠转载全国专栏时最同质化）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 媒体语境
- *Distinctive ends with -ive (特色)；distribute ends with -ute (分发)—mixing them sends the clause to "delivery routes".* （distinctive 以 -ive 表特色；distribute 以 -ute 表分发——混了就会译成配送路线）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[3]/✗ distinctive≠distribute
