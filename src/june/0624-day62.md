# Day 62 on 2026-06-24

## 原句

> In his Case Study House, Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life—few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers—but his belief that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable was widely shared.

## 我的理解和翻译

在他的案例研究屋里，RR可能误判了mechanical革命会如何影响每天生活，少量的美国家庭需要直升机飞行员，而大部分有的是衣服烘干机，但他相信，个人满足是同时有希望的和普遍共享的难以置信

## 批改

```
在他的案例研究屋里，RR可能误判了mechanical革命会如何影响每天生活，少量的美国家庭需要直升机飞行员[1]，而大部分有的是衣服烘干机，但他相信，个人满足[2]是同时有希望的[3]和普遍共享的难以置信[4]
```

**[1] `few American families acquired helicopters` 三处连环误读**："few" ≠ 少量，"few" = 极少有/几乎没有，是几近于零的量，语气比"少量"强得多；"acquired" ≠ 需要，"acquire" = 获得/买到手，强调主动取得；"helicopters" = 直升机（机器），你写成"直升机飞行员"多出了 -er/-man（飞行员），把设备变成了人。三个词叠错，把「极少有人买了直升机」读成了「少量家庭需要直升机飞行员」，句意方向完全走样。

**[2] `self-sufficiency` ≠ 个人满足**："self-sufficiency"是"自给自足"——自己能供应自己所需、不依赖外部。"个人满足"（personal satisfaction）是心理上的满足感，两者是不同的概念。原句语境：他相信，依靠自身就能维持生活（自给自足）这一理念既令人向往又不可避免。

**[3] `desirable` ≠ 有希望的**："desirable" = 令人向往的/可取的，指某件事是值得追求的好事；"有希望的"对应英文更接近"hopeful/promising"。原句 "both desirable and inevitable" = 「既令人向往，又不可避免」——一正向期待，一必然趋势，两者相辅相成。

**[4] `inevitable was widely shared` 完全断读**："inevitable"（不可避免的）被译成"难以置信"——字形上 inevitable 和 unbelievable 没有相似之处，但词长相近容易在疲倦时跳过。"was widely shared"的主语是前面的 "his belief that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable"，整句意思是「这一信念被广泛认同/分享」——不是「难以置信」，也不是「大家都在分享」，而是「这种看法在当时相当普遍」。

**参考译文：** 在他的案例研究住宅中，拉尔夫·拉普森或许误判了机械化革命将以怎样的方式影响日常生活——极少有美国家庭购置了直升机，不过大多数家庭最终买到了烘干机——但他所持的"自给自足既令人向往又不可避免"的信念，却得到了广泛认同。

## Vocab

### inevitable *(adj.)* · [🔊](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/inevitable)

**词根**：in-（否定）+ evit（避开，同 evade）+ -able → 无法避开的

**核心意象**：挡不住的、注定要来的——不管你喜不喜欢，它自己会走到你面前。画面：水往低处流，这是必然，不是愿望。

- *His belief that suburban self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable was widely shared among architects of his generation.* （他认为郊区自给自足既令人向往又不可避免的信念，在他那代建筑师中得到了广泛认同）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — inevitable 与 desirable 并列，表必然趋势
- *The committee acknowledged that some job displacement was inevitable as automation expanded into more sectors of the economy.* （委员会承认，随着自动化向更多经济领域扩展，部分岗位的消失是不可避免的）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 经济/社会变革语境中的必然性
- *"Inevitable" means something that cannot be avoided or prevented, not something that is "unbelievable" or hard to believe—the two words share no meaning despite their similar length.* （"inevitable"指无法避免或阻止的事，而非"难以置信"——尽管词长相近，两者含义毫无关联）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[4]/✗ inevitable 误混为 unbelievable

### acquire *(v.)* · [🔊](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/acquire)

**词根**：ad-（朝向）+ quaerere（寻求）→ 主动寻求并到手

**核心意象**：主动取得，把某样东西弄到自己手里——可以是买、学到、积累。强调已经拿到，而不是想要或正在寻找。

- *Few American families ever acquired helicopters, though the mechanical revolution had made personal aviation seem tantalizingly close.* （极少有美国家庭真正购置了直升机，尽管机械化革命曾让私人航空看起来触手可及）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — acquired helicopters 同语境，"取得/买到"
- *Over the decades, the university acquired an extensive collection of rare manuscripts and donated them to the national archive.* （几十年来，这所大学购置了大量珍贵手稿，并将其捐献给国家档案馆）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 机构积累资产场景
- *"Acquire" means to obtain or gain possession of something, not to need or require it—"few families acquired helicopters" means they didn't buy them, not that they didn't need them.* （"acquire"是取得/获得，不是"需要"——"few families acquired helicopters"说的是极少有人买到，而非极少有人需要）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[1]/✗ acquired 误读成"需要"

### self-sufficiency *(n.)* · [🔊](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/self-sufficient)

**词根**：self（自身）+ sufficient（足够的）→ 自身就够用

**核心意象**：不靠外部就能维持运转——一户人家种粮烧柴、一个国家不进口能源，都是自给自足。与"个人满足感"（personal satisfaction）是两回事：一个讲供给，一个讲心理。

- *Rapson's design reflected a faith that self-sufficiency—growing food, generating power, managing waste—was an achievable goal for the modern household.* （拉普森的设计折射出一种信念：自给自足——种粮、发电、处理废物——对现代家庭来说是可达成的目标）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — self-sufficiency 在住宅/生活方式语境中的用法
- *The report argued that energy self-sufficiency had become a strategic priority for countries seeking to reduce their exposure to global price shocks.* （报告认为，能源自给自足已成为各国降低全球价格冲击风险的战略重点）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 能源/国家政策语境
- *"Self-sufficiency" is the ability to provide for one's own needs without outside help, not personal emotional satisfaction—confusing the two changes the whole argument of the sentence.* （"self-sufficiency"是无需外部帮助便能自行满足需求的能力，而非个人情感满足——混淆两者会让整句论点偏转）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[2]/✗ self-sufficiency 误读成"个人满足"

### desirable *(adj.)* · [🔊](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/desirable)

**词根**：desire（渴望）+ -able → 值得渴望的

**核心意象**：值得追求的、令人向往的——别人都想要、或者理智上判断是好事的。不是"有希望的"（hopeful），不是"可行的"（feasible），是"人们愿意为之努力"。

- *His belief that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable gave him the confidence to design homes that most buyers were not yet ready to live in.* （他认为自给自足既令人向往又不可避免的信念，使他有信心设计出那些大多数买家还未准备好入住的住宅）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — desirable and inevitable 并列结构
- *A flexible work arrangement was seen as highly desirable by employees who had to balance long commutes with family responsibilities.* （对于那些需要兼顾长途通勤与家庭责任的员工来说，弹性工作安排被视为极为可取的选择）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 职场/政策评价语境
- *"Desirable" means worth wanting or pursuing, not "hopeful" or "promising"—"a desirable outcome" is one people actively want, not one they are merely optimistic about.* （"desirable"指值得追求的，而非"有希望的"或"前景好的"——"a desirable outcome"是人们主动想要的结果，而非只是对其保持乐观）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[3]/✗ desirable 误读成"有希望的"

## Phrases

### `—...—` 插入说明（破折号嵌套例子，主干不断）

**讲解**：原句用两个破折号（em dash）把一段具体例子"夹"在主干里——主干是"Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted…but his belief…was widely shared"，中间插入的"—few families acquired helicopters, though most got dryers—"是对"日常生活受影响"的具体举例，读完后主干继续。读的时候如果看到破折号就停下来找"结尾破折号"，把中间当括号跳过，先读主干再回来读例子。

> …may have mispredicted just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life—few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers—but his belief…was widely shared.

- *The new policy—which exempted households earning below the median income, though it applied to renters as well as homeowners—was passed with broad legislative support.* （新政策——它豁免了收入低于中位数的家庭，尽管也适用于租户和房主——以广泛的立法支持获得通过）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — 破折号嵌套说明，主干跨越插入段
- *The committee's report—long delayed by internal disagreements, though eventually endorsed by all members—called for immediate reforms to the pension system.* （委员会报告——因内部分歧而一再推迟，尽管最终获全体成员背书——呼吁立即对养老金制度进行改革）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 新闻/学术语境中的破折号插入结构
- *If you read "—few families acquired helicopters, though most got dryers—" as a continuation of the main clause rather than a parenthetical, you lose track of the subject and predicate that frame the whole sentence.* （如果把"—极少有人买了直升机，尽管大多数买了烘干机—"当成主干而非插入段来读，就会失去把握整句主谓框架的线索）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对插入结构读成主干、丢失主干方向的误读

### `widely shared`（信念/观点被广泛认同）

**讲解**：`widely shared` 的主语是"信念、观点、立场"类名词，shared 在此是"被众多人持有/认同"——不是把信息发出去（share a link），而是这个想法被很多人共同持有。原句末：his belief…was widely shared = 他的这个信念，在当时很多人身上都找得到。

> …his belief that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable was widely shared.

- *The conviction that economic growth required energy independence was widely shared among policymakers on both sides of the aisle.* （经济增长需要能源独立这一信念，在两党的政策制定者中得到广泛认同）
  **例句意图：** 扣原句 — widely shared belief 结构，观点被普遍持有
- *Skepticism about the long-term viability of the new framework was widely shared, even among those who had initially voted for it.* （对新框架长期可行性的怀疑情绪，即便在最初投票支持者中也颇为普遍）
  **例句意图：** 考纲迁移 — 政策/学术评价语境中情绪/立场被广泛持有
- *"Widely shared" here does not mean "commonly distributed" or "passed around"—it means a belief held by many people, closer to "widely held" than to "shared on social media."* （此处"widely shared"不是「广泛传播」或「到处分享」，而是指被许多人共同持有的信念，更接近"widely held"而非"在社交媒体上分享"）
  **例句意图：** 防坑 — 针对[4]/✗ widely shared 误解为"信息传播式的分享"
