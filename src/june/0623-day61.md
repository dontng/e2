# Day 61 on 2026-06-23

## 原句

> The phrase “less is more” was actually first popularized by a German, the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, who like other people associated with the Bauhaus, a school of design, emigrated to the United States before World War Ⅱ and took up posts at American architecture schools.

## 我的理解和翻译

术语少就是多，实际上是由一个德国人第一个流行开来的，是设计师Ludwig Mies van der Rohe，其他被组织的人倾向于称之为the Bauhaus，这是一种学院派的设计，二战前迁徙到美国，并在美国建造学校拿下很多支持

## 批改

```
术语少就是多[1]，实际上是由一个德国人[2]第一个流行开来的，是设计师Ludwig Mies
van der Rohe，其他被组织的人倾向于称之为the Bauhaus[3]，这是一种学院派的设计[3]，
二战前迁徙到美国，并在美国建造学校[5]拿下很多支持[4]
```

**[1] "less is more" → 术语少就是多**
断在词义锁死。"less is more" 是一句广为人知的设计格言，核心意思是"简约即美"/"少即是多"。硬译字面只得到一个没有意义的短语。这里是作为一个具名短语（加引号）出现的，翻译时保留引号、给出约定俗成的译法即可。

**[2] was first popularized by → 第一个流行开来的（主语偏移）**
断在被动语态的主语。原句主语是 the phrase "less is more"，不是人。被动结构 was popularized by a German 意思是"由一个德国人推广开来"，学生把 a German 挪成了主语，导致后面 the architect 的同位语关系也断掉了。

**[3] associated with → 被组织的人倾向于称之为**
断在词义极性。associate with = 与……有关联/联系，是中性的归属关系，不含"倾向于称之为"的意思。people associated with the Bauhaus 就是"与包豪斯有关联的人/包豪斯相关人士"。插入语 a school of design 是对 the Bauhaus 的解释性同位语，学生把它译成了"学院派的设计"，丢掉了"设计学院"这个定性。

**[4] took up posts → 拿下很多支持**
断在词义完全丢失。take up posts = 就任（某些）职位，是一个固定词块，posts 此处是"职位/教职"。学生读到 posts 可能联想到 post 的其他义项（支柱/帖子），再跟 architecture 组合后猜成了"建造支持"，路径是字形触发联想，不是语义推断。

**[5] architecture schools → 建造学校**
断在词义。architecture = 建筑学（作为学科），不是"建造"这个动作。American architecture schools = 美国的建筑学院。

---

**参考译文：**

"少即是多"这句话实际上是由一位德国人——建筑师路德维希·密斯·凡德罗——最先推广开来的。他和其他与包豪斯（一所设计学院）有关联的人一样，在二战前移居美国，并在美国多所建筑学院任职。

## Vocab

**`popularize`** *v.*

词根 popular（来自拉丁语 populus，人民）加 -ize 使动后缀，整体意思是"使……在大众中流行/推广开来"。

- Darwin's theories were first popularized in the late nineteenth century by writers who saw in evolution a justification for social inequality.
- The architect who popularized open-plan living spaces argued that walls were psychological barriers as much as physical ones.
- To *popularize* a concept is different from simply making it *popular*—the former implies an active agent doing the spreading, the latter only describes the end state.

这次断在把 was popularized by 的主语搞错了——原句主语是 the phrase，不是人；popularize 本身是"主动推广"，被动式就是"被某人推广开来"。

---

**`emigrate`** *v.*

词根 e-（向外）+ migrare（迁移），指"从本国/本地离开，移居他处"，强调离开的一端。

- Millions of Eastern Europeans emigrated to the industrial cities of North America in the decades before and after 1900, fundamentally reshaping the demographics of both regions.
- When the political climate turned hostile, many of the country's most talented scientists emigrated, taking with them not just expertise but the networks that sustained it.
- She *emigrated* from Poland to France, where she eventually *immigrated* and settled permanently—the difference is only about which end of the journey you're naming.

emigrate 强调"从哪里离开"，immigrate 强调"到哪里落脚"；这句用 emigrated to the United States，是从德国视角说"离开"。

---

**`architecture`** *n.*

来自希腊语 arkhitekton（arkhi- 首席 + tekton 建造者），-ure 是名词后缀表学科/技艺。

- The architecture of the new terminal was praised not only for its aesthetic boldness but for the way it managed the movement of thousands of passengers without friction.
- A course in the history of architecture is, in effect, a course in the history of how societies imagine the relationship between people and built space.
- *Architecture* (建筑学) is distinct from *construction* (建造/施工)—the former is a discipline concerned with design and theory, the latter with the physical act of building.

这次错在把 architecture（建筑学这门学科）读成了"建造"这个动作；architecture schools = 建筑学院，不是"建造学校"。

## Phrases

**`associated with`** *phr.*

结构：past participle 作后置定语，意思是"与……有关联的/隶属于……的"，常用来表示归属、关联、协作关系，不含评价色彩。

- Many of the scientists associated with the Manhattan Project later became vocal advocates for nuclear arms control, haunted by the consequences of their own work.
- Critics associated with the Frankfurt School argued that mass culture did not liberate people but instead shaped their desires in ways that served the existing social order.
- Being *associated with* a controversial figure is not the same as *being responsible for* their actions—the phrase signals connection, not complicity.

这次把 associated with 译成"倾向于称之为"，是把关联关系读成了一个动作短语；associated with 只表示归属或联系，无主观倾向色彩。

---

**`a school of design`** *appositive phr.*

同位语结构：名词短语紧跟在专有名词后面，用逗号隔开，功能是解释前面那个读者可能不熟悉的名词。

- The Situationist International, a loose collective of artists and theorists, developed the concept of *détournement*—repurposing existing cultural materials to subvert their original meaning.
- Her mentor, a professor of computational linguistics, had spent forty years studying how children acquire the grammar of their mother tongue without explicit instruction.
- When a text inserts *an appositive phrase* directly after a proper noun, read it as the author's own gloss; when it inserts *a relative clause*, the author is adding new information rather than defining.

同位语的识别信号是：专有名词后面紧接逗号 + 名词短语，翻译时直接加破折号或括号做注解，不要把它当动词宾语处理。
