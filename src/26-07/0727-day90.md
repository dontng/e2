# Day 90 · 2026-07-27

## 原句（Input）

> But the Nobel Foundation’s limit of three recipients per prize, each of whom must still be living, has long been outgrown by the collaborative nature of modern research—as will be demonstrated by the inevitable row over who is ignored when it comes to acknowledging the discovery of the Higgs boson.

你保住了每奖三人且须健在的限制，但 `outgrown` 的被动方向和最后的争议没有接成完整判断。先让“限制已不合时宜”成立，再读后面如何体现这个问题。

<details open>
<summary><strong>主语是 limit，has been outgrown 给被超越的对象</strong></summary>

> the Nobel Foundation’s **limit of three recipients per prize**, each of whom must still be living, **has long been outgrown by the collaborative nature of modern research**

`limit` 是名词中心，`of three recipients per prize` 说明每个奖项限制三名获奖者；`each of whom` 插入说明这些获奖者须健在。读到 `has long been`，外层谓语才开始，不能把 by 后的现代研究当作被改变的对象。

> Modern research **has outgrown the three-person limit**.  
> The three-person limit **has been outgrown by modern research**.

主动与被动描述同一个方向：现代研究的合作规模已超出这条限制所能适应的程度。`outgrow` 也可用于 `The child has outgrown these shoes.`，孩子长大后鞋不适用；不是孩子被鞋发展出来。

</details>

<details open>
<summary><strong>collaborative nature 是协作性质，非合作的国家</strong></summary>

`nature` 此处是性质：`the collaborative nature of research = the fact that research depends on collaboration`。`recipient` 是接受奖项的人，即获奖者，不等于每一个贡献者都获得了奖。`each of whom` 从获奖者集合逐个取出，不能接回单数 limit。

</details>

<details open>
<summary><strong>as 后面将出现证明，row 是争执</strong></summary>

> as **will be demonstrated by the inevitable row over who is ignored**...

`as` 把前面的判断带入补充说明，意为这一点将由争议体现；`will be demonstrated` 不必另造一个实体主语。`row` 在这里是争执：`a row over the prize`，非排成一行或趋势；`inevitable` 是不可避免的。

> There was **a row over who deserved credit**.  
> The seats were arranged **in a row**.

后句才是排列。`over` 给争论议题，`who is ignored` 询问谁被遗漏，不能把 ignored 当修饰趋势的形容词。

</details>

<details open>
<summary><strong>when it comes to 接评奖议题，非发现发生日期</strong></summary>

> when it comes to **acknowledging the discovery of the Higgs boson**

这是“谈到认可希格斯玻色子的发现、给予发现功劳时”，不是刚发现粒子的那一刻。`acknowledge` 在此涉及认可贡献；`When it comes to assigning credit, opinions differ.` 也在讨论功劳分配。把这块接到争议：认可发现功劳时，谁被遗漏会引发争执。

</details>

## 准确译文

> 但是，诺贝尔基金会规定每个奖项至多有三名获奖者，且每人都须健在；现代研究的协作性质早已超出了这项限制所能适应的程度。在认可希格斯玻色子发现的功劳时，围绕谁遭到遗漏必然出现的争议，将体现这一点。
