2020 英语（二）· Text 1 原题与原位复盘

Text 1

Rats and other animals need to be highly attuned to social signals from others so they can identify friends to cooperate with and enemies to avoid. To find out if this extends to non-living beings, Laleh Quinn at the University of California, San Diego, and her colleagues tested whether rats can detect social signals from robotic rats.

P1为什么研究者要做这个实验

第一句先交代背景：动物需要对来自同类的社会信号保持高度敏感，才能认出可以合作的朋友和需要避开的敌人。so 后面不是另一件事，而是 need to be highly attuned 的目的。

第二句才是实验目的。To find out if this extends to non-living beings 是“为了查明这种能力能否延伸到非生命体”；主干是 Quinn and her colleagues tested whether rats can detect ...。这里的 this 接前句整件事，if 和 whether 都要等后面的完整命题，不能读到小词就丢掉等待位。

词与关系：be attuned to 对……敏感；signals from others 来自其他个体的信号；cooperate with 与……合作；extend to 延伸到；detect 识别、察觉。

第21题问“实验是为了看什么”，所以只认目的句。friends / enemies 虽然醒目，却只是背景；B 项正是拿背景冒充实验目的。

They housed eight adult rats with two types of robotic rat — one social and one asocial — for four days. The robot rats were quite minimalist, resembling a chunkier version of a computer mouse with wheels to move around and colourful markings.

P2两类机器人长什么样，如何被放进实验

They housed eight adult rats with two types of robotic rat ... for four days：研究者把八只成年老鼠与两类机器鼠共同安置了四天。这里的 house 是动词“安置、饲养”，不是“房子”；with 说明老鼠与机器鼠被放在同一环境中。

破折号把 two types 填满：一种有社会性，一种没有。第二句的主干是 The robot rats were quite minimalist，后面的 resembling ... 补充外形：像更厚实的电脑鼠标，带有用于移动的轮子和彩色标记。

容易卡住的词：social / asocial 有社会性／非社会性；minimalist 设计极简；chunkier 更厚、更敦实；markings 外观上的标记。不要把 robotic 只理解成“动作机械”，这里就是“机器的”。

During the experiment, the social robot rat followed the living rats around, played with the same toys, and opened cage doors to let trapped rats escape. Meanwhile, the asocial robot simply moved forwards and backwards and side to side.

P3 · Q22动作都是真的，关键是谁做的

social robot 连做三件并列的事：followed ... around 跟着活老鼠四处活动，played with the same toys 玩相同的玩具，opened cage doors to let trapped rats escape 打开笼门让被困的老鼠逃出。to let 表目的，trapped 修饰 rats。

Meanwhile 把镜头切到 asocial robot；它只会前后、左右移动。第22题的 A、B、C 并非原文没出现，而是把 social robot 的动作挂到了 asocial robot 身上。你这题选 D，说明这次没有丢掉“谁做什么”。

词与小关系：follow ... around 跟着……到处走；let + 宾语 + 动词原形 让某人做某事；side to side 左右来回。

Next, the researchers trapped the robots in cages and gave the rats the opportunity to release them by pressing a lever. Across 18 trials each, the living rats were 52 per cent more likely on average to set the social robot free than the asocial one. This suggests that the rats perceived the social robot as a genuine social being. They may have bonded more with the social robot because it displayed behaviors like communal exploring and playing. This could lead to the rats better remembering having freed it earlier, and wanting the robot to return the favour when they get trapped, says Quinn.

P4 · Q23实验结果、研究者推测和回报动机

研究者把机器人关进笼子，让活老鼠可以通过按压杠杆把它们放出来。18轮实验中，活老鼠释放 social robot 的可能性平均高出 52%。the asocial one 里的 one 代替 robot，比较的是两类机器人，不是两类活老鼠。

接下来三句的确定程度不同：This suggests 是“结果表明”，may have bonded 是可能已经建立联系，could lead to 是可能进一步导致。阅读时若把 suggests / may / could 全抹掉，推测就会被误读成已证实事实。

最后一句先保住骨架：This could lead to A, and B。A 是 the rats better remembering having freed it earlier，老鼠更容易记得自己先前救过它；B 是 wanting the robot to return the favour，希望机器人日后回报。having freed it 是 remembering 的内容，want + the robot + to return 是“希望机器人去回报”。

词与关系：release / set free 释放；by pressing 通过按压；perceive A as B 把 A 看作 B；bond with 与……建立联系；communal 共同参与的；return the favour 回报人情。

第23题把 wanting ... return the favour 压缩成 expected it to do the same in return。正确项保留了“我先帮它—希望它以后帮我”的方向，其余动机都没有原文证据。

“Rats have been shown to engage in multiple forms of reciprocal help and cooperation, including what is referred to as direct reciprocity — where a rat will help another rat that has previously helped them,” says Quinn.

P5what 给概念命名，where 给概念补定义

主干是 Rats have been shown to engage in multiple forms of ...：已有研究表明，老鼠会参与多种互助与合作。have been shown to 是“已被证明会……”，不要读成“老鼠被展示去做”。

including what is referred to as direct reciprocity：其中包括一种被称为“直接互惠”的行为。what is referred to as X 整体占一个名词位置。破折号后的 where 不指真实地点，而是展开这种情形：一只老鼠会帮助先前帮助过自己的另一只老鼠。

词与关系：engage in 参与；reciprocal 相互的；reciprocity 互惠；previously 先前。这里也在为第23题的“回报”提供概念解释。

The readiness of the rats to befriend the social robot was surprising given its minimal design. The robot was the same size as a regular rat but resembled a simple plastic box on wheels. “We’d assumed we’d have to give it a moving head and tail, facial features, and put a scent on it to make it smell like a real rat, but that wasn’t necessary,” says Janet Wiles at the University of Queensland in Australia, who helped with the research.

P6 · Q24出现过的词，不等于实验支持的结论

第一句容易在长主语里提前迷路。主干是 The readiness ... was surprising；of the rats 说明是谁的准备意愿，to befriend the social robot 说明准备做什么。given its minimal design 是“考虑到它的设计如此简陋”，不是“被给予设计”。

第二句用 the same size as ... but resembled ... 把尺寸和外形分开：大小与普通老鼠相同，但看上去只是带轮子的塑料盒。resemble 后面直接接对象，不加 with。

引语的骨架是 We’d assumed [A], but A wasn’t necessary。A 包含两件并列动作：给机器鼠装上会动的头尾和面部特征；在它身上加气味，使它闻起来像真老鼠。that 回指整套设想，but 给出实验后的否定结果。

词与关系：readiness 准备意愿；befriend 与……建立友好关系；given 考虑到；facial features 面部特征；put a scent on 给……加上气味；smell like 闻起来像。

你在第24题划到 facial features 后选 A，位置没有找偏，但把“研究者原先以为需要”误当成“实验已经证明老鼠能记住”。A 还凭空加入了 remember。目前先记为一次“证据身份”失误，等后续真题再验证是否稳定复发。

The finding shows how sensitive rats are to social cues, even when they come from basic robots. Similarly, children tend to treat robots as if they are fellow beings, even when they display only simple social signals. “We humans seem to be fascinated by robots, and it turns out other animals are too,” says Wiles.

P7 · Q25全文结论和 children 类比怎样收束

The finding shows how sensitive rats are to social cues 不是问“怎样敏感”，而是感叹程度：这一发现说明老鼠对社会线索有多敏感。even when they come from basic robots 中的 they 回指 social cues——即使这些线索来自很简陋的机器人。

Similarly 明确说明 children 是相似例证，不是拿儿童与老鼠比较谁更强。儿童会 treat robots as if they are fellow beings，把机器人当作同类生命看待；as if 是“仿佛、就像”。末句的 it turns out 是“结果发现”。

词与关系：sensitive to 对……敏感；cue 可供判断的线索；fellow beings 同类生命；be fascinated by 被……吸引。

第25题 D 保留了全文结论。B 凭空增加“比其他动物更活跃”的比较；C 把 Similarly 表示的相似改成不同。你选 D，说明这次没有被无依据比较带偏。

2020年考研英语（二）试题　 第 3 页 共 14 页

21. Quinn and her colleagues conducted a test to see if rats can .

[A] pick up social signals from non-living rats

[B] distinguish a friendly rat from a hostile one

[C] attain sociable traits through special training

[D] send out warning messages to their fellows

Q21你的答案 A｜目的定位与同义替换成立

conducted a test to see if 对应原文 To find out ... tested whether ...；pick up 对应 detect，non-living rats 对应 non-living beings，因此选 A。

B 的 friendly/hostile 来自首句 friends/enemies，但那只是为什么要研究社会信号，不是这次实验具体要验证什么。你选 A，说明这次区分了背景与目的；以后仍应先找目的框架，再看框架里装的内容。

22.What did the asocial robot do during the experiment?

[A] It followed the social robot.

[B] It played with some toys.

[C] It set the trapped rats free.

[D] It moved around alone.

Q22你的答案 D｜对象与动作没有串位

Meanwhile 后面的主语是 asocial robot，谓语是 simply moved，D 的 moved around alone 是压缩改写。

A、B、C 的动作在原文里都真实发生过，但执行者是 social robot。此类干扰不靠编造信息，而靠更换主语；判断时要把“动作存在”和“动作属于谁”分开核对。

23. According to Quinn, the rats released the social robot because they.

[A] tried to practice a means of escape

[B] expected it to do the same in return

[C] wanted to display their intelligence

[D] considered that an interesting game

Q23你的答案 B｜因果链被正确压缩成选项

wanting the robot to return the favour 就是希望机器人以后也帮助自己，B 用 do the same in return 重写“回报同样的帮助”。这里的 return 不是单独的“返回”，要和 favour 连成“回报人情”。

A、C、D 都是读者可以想象出来的动机，但原文没有相应因果链。你选 B，说明这次抓住的是关系，而不是逐词复现。

24. Janet Wiles notes that rats .

[A] can remember other rats’ facial features

[B] differentiate smells better than sizes

[C] respond more to actions than to looks

[D] can be scared by a plastic box on wheels

Q24你的答案 A｜答案 C｜同词命中，但命中了被推翻的设想

你划到 facial features 后选 A，定位没有偏离原段，但这个词属于 We’d assumed ... 中的旧设想，随后被 but that wasn’t necessary 推翻。A 还额外加入了原文没有的 remember。

C 把整段压缩为：外观很简陋，老鼠仍会根据社会性动作作出回应。以后遇到原词复现，先判断它在原文中是结果、推测、他人观点还是被否定的设想，再谈同义替换。目前只记录一次证据身份失误，不据此定性。

25. It can be learned from the text that rats .

[A] appear to be adaptable to new surroundings

[B] are more socially active than other animals

[C] behave differently from children in socializing

[D] are more sensitive to social cues than expected

Q25你的答案 D｜跨句结论没有被比较项带偏

末段直接用 The finding shows how sensitive ... 收束全文，D 是结论改写。than expected 来自“机器人如此简陋，老鼠仍愿意与它互动”这一反预期结果。

B 凭空增加 rats 与 other animals 的高低比较；C 把 Similarly 表示的相似改成不同。你选 D，说明这次保住了类比方向和全文结论。
