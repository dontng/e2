# 2016 阅读理解 Text 1｜在原文上做题

> 首次作答：`B / B / A / C / D`；正确答案：`B / B / A / C / A`。
>
> 本页只解决一件事：原文把答案写在哪里，又怎样让人读偏。词义见 [16-text1-word](16-text1-word.md)，句子训练见 [Day 93](../src/august/0805-day93.md)。

## 标记

- <mark>答案证据</mark>：足以决定或直接支持答案。
- **转向 / 范围**：读原文时必须及时处理的路标。
- `〔Qxx〕`：此处应完成的考场动作，不翻译整段。

---

It's true that high-school coding classes **aren't essential** for learning computer science in college. Students without experience can **catch up** after a few introductory courses, said Tom Cortina, the assistant dean at Carnegie Mellon's School of Computer Science.

> **先别封口**：`It's true that ...` 先承认“并非必不可少”；它没有说早学“没用”。下一段 `However` 才把作者要讲的方向转回来。

**However**, Cortina said, early exposure is beneficial. When younger kids learn computer science, they learn that it's not just a confusing, endless string of letters and numbers - but a tool to build apps, or create artwork, or test hypotheses. <mark>It's not as hard for them to transform their thought processes as it is for older students.</mark> Breaking down problems into bite-sized chunks and using code to solve them becomes normal. Giving more children this training could increase the number of people interested in the field and help fill the jobs gap, Cortina said.

> `〔Q21〕` 题干的 `makes it easier` 到原文中找比较关系：`not as hard ... as ...`。真正变容易的是 `transform their thought processes`；apps、artwork、hypotheses 只是前一句列举的用途，不能从醒目的实词直接抄成答案。

Students also benefit from learning something about coding before they get to college, where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim, which can drive the less-experienced or -determined students away.

The Flatiron School, where people pay to learn programming, started as one of the many coding bootcamps that's become popular for adults looking for a **career change**. The high-schoolers get the same curriculum, **but** <mark>“we try to gear lessons toward things they're interested in,”</mark> said Victoria Friedman, an instructor. For instance, one of the apps the students are developing suggests movies based on your mood.

> `〔Q22〕` 问 high-schoolers，证据在 `but` 后；`toward` 后面的目标就是课程考虑的因素。`career change` 很显眼，却属于前一句的 adults，是 C 项的就近诱饵。

The students in the Flatiron class probably won't drop out of high school and build the next Facebook. **Programming languages** have a quick turnover, so the “Ruby on Rails” language they learned may not even be relevant by the time they enter the job market. **But the skills they learn** - how to think logically through a problem and organize the results - <mark>apply to any coding language</mark>, said Deborah Seehorn, an education consultant for the state of North Carolina.

> `〔Q23〕` 先分对象，再读判断：可能过时的是某种 language；能迁移的是 skills。B 项的“需要升级”来自前半段的真实信息，但把属性从 language 偷接到了 skills。

Indeed, the Flatiron students **might not go into IT at all**. But creating a future army of coders is **not the sole purpose** of the classes. These kids are going to be <mark>surrounded by computers - in their pockets, in their offices, in their homes - for the rest of their lives</mark>. The younger they learn how computers think, how to <u>coax</u> the machine into producing what they want - the earlier they learn that they have the power to do that - the better.

> `〔Q24〕` 前两句先排除“进入 IT / 培养程序员”这条路；正面且确定的信息是孩子将终身处在计算机环境中，并应尽早学会理解、使用机器。选项要概括这条正向信息，不能把被否定的词做成答案。
>
> `〔Q25〕` 先保住关系：`coax + the machine + into producing ...`，机器最后产出人想要的结果。这能把词义缩到“施加影响使其做某事”，也说明 D `challenge` 与句内结果、搭配不合。**但结构本身不能唯一证明 A**：英语也能说 `frighten A into doing B`，`misguide A into doing/believing B`。最后还要看语境——这里是学习如何让机器按意图工作，没有恐吓或误导；`coax` 因而接近 `persuade`。

## 21—25：只核对证据怎样变成选项

### 21. Cortina holds that early exposure to computer science makes it easier to __

- [A] complete future job training
- [B] remodel the way of thinking
- [C] formulate logical hypotheses
- [D] perfect artwork production

**做对：B。** `not as hard to transform their thought processes` → `makes it easier to remodel the way of thinking`。

**容易偏到哪里：** C、D 复用了原文实词，却把“编程可用于做什么”冒充成“早接触使什么变容易”。先锁定题干的 `easier`，再找原文中的难易关系。

### 22. In delivering lessons for high-schoolers, Flatiron has considered their __

- [A] experience
- [B] interest
- [C] career prospects
- [D] academic backgrounds

**做对：B。** `gear lessons toward things they're interested in` → considered `their interest`。

**容易偏到哪里：** C 借用了同段 `career change`，但那是在交代成人训练营为何流行，不是高中课程朝什么调整。

### 23. Deborah Seehorn believes that the skills learned at Flatiron will __

- [A] help students learn other computer languages
- [B] have to be upgraded when new technologies come
- [C] need improving when students look for jobs
- [D] enable students to make big quick money

**做对：A。** `skills ... apply to any coding language` → 这些能力可用于其他编程语言。

**容易偏到哪里：** B 把“编程语言更新快”挂给了 `skills`。原文信息是真的，主语换了，选项仍然是错的。

### 24. According to the last paragraph, Flatiron students are expected to __

- [A] bring forth innovative computer technologies
- [B] stay longer in the information technology industry
- [C] become better prepared for the digitalized world
- [D] compete with a future army of programmers

**做对：C。** `might not go into IT`、`not the sole purpose` 先挡住 B、D；`surrounded by computers ... for the rest of their lives` 加末句能力，合成 `better prepared for the digitalized world`。

**不全懂时：** 这题不必吃透末句全部结构，也能先凭两个否定排 B、D；但 A/C 的最终判断仍要靠后面的正面信息，不能把排除法说成完整证明。

### 25. The word “coax” is closest in meaning to __

- [A] persuade
- [B] frighten
- [C] misguide
- [D] challenge

**做对：A。** 结构给出“使机器产出结果”的大方向；学习控制机器的语境排除恐吓、误导和挑战。`coax` 是耐心、巧妙地劝动，四项中 `persuade` 最近。

**首次为什么可能选 D：** “人与机器”让 `challenge` 在题材上听起来像话，但把它放回 `challenge the machine into producing ...`，搭配和因果都接不上。选词题不能只看气氛，必须把选项放回原句。

**证据边界：** 目前只确认首次选了 D；究竟是不认识 `coax`，还是没有把选项放回句内，需由 [Day 93](../src/august/0805-day93.md) 的闭卷作答区分。

## 本篇怎样把错误选项藏进原文

| 题 | 原文里的诱饵 | 选项做了什么手脚 |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | apps / artwork / hypotheses | 把用途例子换成“变容易的事” |
| 22 | adults looking for a career change | 把成人背景换成高中生课程依据 |
| 23 | programming languages have a quick turnover | 把语言的属性换给通用 skills |
| 24 | IT / army of coders | 把明确否定的方向改写成肯定预期 |
| 25 | 人与机器的对抗感 | 用题材气氛掩盖搭配和结果不成立 |

这五题不是五种需要背名字的“题型”。它们共同要求一件更具体的事：**选项用了原文的词，不等于保留了原文中“谁—做什么—对谁成立—是否被否定”的关系。**
