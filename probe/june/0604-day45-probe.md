# day45 Probe

source: [src/june/0604-day45.md](../../src/june/0604-day45.md)

---

## 原句


> It tends to ignore, and thus eventually to eliminate, many elements in the land community that lack commercial value, but that are essential to its healthy functioning.

## 我的翻译


它倾向于无视，因此eventually限制，地方社区的很多元素是缺少商业价值，但这些元素也是它们健康功能的依存

## 评分


**5 / 10**

采分点（4点）：
- ✓ "tends to ignore" 倾向于无视
- ✗ "eventually eliminate" 最终彻底消除 → 「eventually限制」· eliminate=根除，限制≠消除；eventually留英文未译
- ✗ "land community" 土地共同体（利奥波德生态哲学概念）→ 「地方社区」· 生态整体概念≠人类聚居概念
- ✓ "lack commercial value" 缺乏商业价值
