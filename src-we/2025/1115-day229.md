## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 because/who和标点逗号，可以把句子断开为4段。**

**【句 1】宾语从句： Certain artifacts are especially vulnerable**

**【句 2】原因状语从句：because some pioneers in plastic art didn’t always know how to mix ingredients properly,**

**【句 3】主句：says Thea van Oosten, a polymer chemist**

**【句 4】定语从句：who, until retiring a few years ago, worked for decades at the Cultural Heritage Agency of the Netherlands.**

**✅ 分析主句**

**本句的主句是一个倒装句。主句中的谓语动词是 says，主语是Thea van Oosten，主语后面的a polymer chemist是同位语，解释说明Thea van Oosten。**

**✅ 分裂结构**

**句 4定语从句中插入了until介词短语作插入语，造成了句子的分裂。**

**who, until retiring a few years ago, worked for decades at the Cultural Heritage Agency of the Netherlands**
