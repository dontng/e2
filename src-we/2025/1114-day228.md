## 原句

> In one study, participants completed a battery of eight tasks, including four that tapped reflective thinking (discerning rules, comprehending vocabulary) and four that tapped intuition and creativity (generating new products or figures of speech).

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据两个连接词 that，可以把句子断开为3段。**

**【句 1】主句： In one study, participants completed a battery of eight tasks, including four 句 2 and four 句 3.**

**【句 2】定语从句： that tapped reflective thinking (discerning rules, comprehending vocabulary)**

**【句 3】定语从句： that tapped intuition and creativity (generating new products or figures of speech)**

**✅ 平行结构**

**如下两个 “four+that定语从句+插入语”，通过and连接，构成平行结构，一起补充说明前面的“八个任务包括什么（ eight tasks, including ） ”。其中four后面省略了名词tasks。**

1) **four that** **tapped reflective thinking (discerning rules, comprehending vocabulary)**

2) **four that tapped intuition and creativity (generating new products or figures of** **speech)**

**补充：如上第 2小点中还有一处小的平行结构， intuition and creativity，但结构比较简单，就不特别强调了。**

## 参考译文

**在一项研究中，参与者完成了一组八个任务，其中四个利用了反思性思维（辨别规则、理解词汇），（另外）四个利用了直觉和创造力（创造新产品或修辞手法）。**
