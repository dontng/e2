## 原句

> Grade forgiveness allows students to retake a course in which they received a low grade, and the most recent grade or the highest grade is the only one that counts in calculating a student’s overall GPA.

**思考题:**

in which 这种从句如何理解？

## 生词

✅ retake a course  重修一门课程  
✅ count in...  把……计算在内  
✅ calculate  v. 计算  
✅ overall  adj. 全面的，综合的，总体的  
✅ GPA (grade-point averages)  平均学分绩点

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句

1 Grade forgiveness allows students to retake a course

2 in which they received a low grade,

3 and the most recent grade or the highest grade is the only one

4 that counts in calculating a student's overall GPA.

✅
介词提前的定语从句

a course in which they received a low grade
，in
which是介词提前的定语从句，修饰前面的名词a course。在考研真题中遇到这种时，只需要采用代入法来理解，例如本句，从句是修饰前面的名词a course
，那么which
= a course
，则in which
= in a course，这样定语从句的意思就好理解了。

⚠️ 拓展：如何判断出这是“介词提前的定语从句”，而不是“介词+宾语从句”？

很简单，就看介词跟前后哪里是一体的。

I am interested in which team he will join.

如上，be interested in是一个词组，是一个整体；而从句which team he will join
也是一个完整的句子，则可以判断in属于前半部分的主句，而不属于从句，这里的in which就是“介词+宾语从句”。

Grade forgiveness allows students to retake a course in which they received a low grade.

如上，Grade forgiveness allows students to retake a course
是一个完整的句子，不需要再加上后面的in；而in
which they received a low grade = in a course they received a low grade
，则可以判断出in属于从句，这里的in which就是“介词提前的定语从句”
。
