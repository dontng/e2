## 原句

> The Internet—and pressure from funding agencies, who are questioning why commercial publishers are making money from government-funded research by restricting access to it—is making free access to scientific results a reality.

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句：

根据标点逗号/破折号和连接词who/why，可以把句子断开为3段。

【句1】主句：The Internet—and pressure from funding agencies, ...—is making free access to scientific results a reality.

【句2】非限定性定语从句：who are questioning ...

【句3】宾语从句：why commercial publishers are making money from government-funded research by restricting access to it

✅
分裂结构：

本句中两个破折号隔开的插入语，造成了主句的分裂。如果想要连贯地看懂句子，只需要把插入语先放在一边，看懂了主句之后，再来看插入语。

The Internet
— and pressure from funding agencies, who are questioning why commercial publishers are making money from government-funded research by restricting access to it —
is making free access to scientific results a reality.

✅
嵌套结构：

句1主句中嵌套结构了句2who非限定性定语从句，而句2中又嵌套了句3why宾语从句，层层嵌套。
