## 原句

> Meanwhile, the theft of information about some 40 million credit-card accounts in America, disclosed on June 17th, overshadowed a hugely important decision a day earlier by America’s Federal Trade Commission (FTC) that puts corporate America on notice that regulators will act if firms fail to provide adequate data security.

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句：

根据连接词that/that/if，可以把句子断开为4段。

【句1】主句：Meanwhile, the theft of information about some 40 million credit-card accounts in America, disclosed on June 17th, overshadowed a hugely important decision a day earlier by America’s Federal Trade Commission (FTC)

【句2】定语从句：that puts corporate America on notice

【句3】同位语从句：that regulators will act

【句4】条件状语从句：if firms fail to provide adequate data security.

✅
