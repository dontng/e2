## 原句

> Writing in The New Republic, Alice Lee notes that increasing the number of opportunities for board membership without increasing the pool of qualified women to serve on such boards has led to a “golden skirt” phenomenon, where the same elite women scoop up multiple seats on a variety of boards.

**思考题:**

that从句中主语是什么？

## 生词

✅ board membership 董事会成员  
✅ pool n. 贮备力量，备用人员；水池  
✅ qualified adj. 有资格的，适合的，胜任的  
✅ phenomenon n. 现象  
✅ scoop up 用勺舀，挖空（本文中指“包揽”）  
✅ multiple adj. 多样的，许多的  
✅ avariety of… 种种，各种各样的……

## 语法重点

✅ **断开长难句**

主句：Writing in The New Republic, Alice Lee notes

宾语从句：that increasing the number of opportunities for board membership without increasing the pool of qualified women to serve on such boards has led to a “golden skirt” phenomenon,

定语从句：where the same elite women scoop up multiple seats on a variety of boards.

✅
找主语

在that宾语从句中，主语非常难，结合了多种变化，不太好找，所以要先定位谓语动词，然后再去看它之前的部分就是主语，

如下：

increasing the number of opportunities for board membership without increasing the pool of qualified women to serve on such boards

记得有需要就随时看看静姐的《句句真研》查缺补漏～加油～

## 参考译文

在《新共和》杂志中，Alice Lee指出，增加女性在董事会中任职的机会而不增加可以胜任这些席位的女性的后备力量，已导致一种“金裙”现象，在这一范围中精英女性包揽了董事会的多个席位。
