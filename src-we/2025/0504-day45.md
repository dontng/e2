## 原句

> “Their capacity for tolerating stress may even be greater than men’s,” she observes, “it’s just that they’re dealing with so many more things that they become worn out from it more visibly and sooner.”

<div align="center">【2008 Text 1】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中的两个that从句分别是什么从句？

## 生词

✅ capacity /kəˈpæsəti/ n. 能力；容量  
✅ tolerate stress 忍受压力，承受压力  
✅ tolerate /ˈtɒləreɪt/ v. 忍受；容忍；包容  
✅ observe /əbˈzɜːv/ v. 观察，注意到；说，评论  
✅ deal with… 处理；涉及  
✅ become worn out 变得筋疲力尽  
✅ visibly /ˈvɪzəbli/ adv. 明显地，显然；看得见地

## 分析句子

找谓语动词
：
may be/observes/’s(is)/’re(are) dealing/become

断开
：
引号 / 逗号；that/that

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句：

根据标点引号/逗号和连接词that/that，可以把句子断开为5段。

【句1】直接引语1：“Their capacity for tolerating stress may even be greater than men’s”

【句2】主句：“...,” she observes, “...”

【句3】直接引语2中的主句：“it’s just ...

【句4】直接引语2中的表语从句：that they’re dealing with so many more things

【句5】直接引语2中的结果状语从句：that they become worn out from it more visibly and sooner.”

✅
判断从句的类型：

判断从句的类型，
静姐的口诀就是三个字——“往前看”。

it
’s
just
that
they’re dealing with so many more things

通过前面的’s(is)系动词，可以判断出后面的that从句是表语从句。

so
many more things
that
they become worn out from it more visibly and sooner
.

通过前面的so…that，可以判断出后面的that从句是结果状语从句。
