## 原句

> Taxes on low-wage labor need to be cut, and wage subsidies such as the earned income tax credit should be expanded: This would boost incomes, encourage work, reward companies for job creation, and reduce inequality.

<div align="center">【2018英一 Text 1】</div>

**思考题:**

你能找到本句中的平行结构吗？

## 生词

✅ subsidy  n. 补贴，补助金  
✅ earned income tax credit 工作收入抵税，所得税 抵  
✅ expand  v. 扩大，增加；扩展  
✅ boost  v. 使增长，推动  
✅ inequality  n. 不平等

## 分析句子

找谓语动词
:
need/should be expanded/would boost/encourage/reward/reduce

断开
: 通过连接词and以及标点符号逗号/冒号断开

## 语法重点

✅ **断开长难句**

1 Taxes on low-wage labor need to be cut,

2 and wage subsidies such as the earned income tax credit should be expanded:

3 This would boost incomes, encourage work, reward companies for job creation, and reduce inequality.

✅
平行结构

动词词组的平行：

1
boost incomes,

2 encourage work,

3 reward companies for job creation,

4 reduce inequality
，

四个动词词组通过逗号和并列连词and，平行并列在一起，跟在would后作谓语动词和宾语。

句子的平行：

2 wage subsidies such as the earned income tax credit should be expanded
，

两个句子之间通过并列连词and连接，平行并列在一起。

⚠️ 拓展：

平行结构，在《句句真研》的语法书和课中给大家非常清晰地讲过。平行结构也就是并列结构，可以平行并列词、词组、句子，中间可以用
逗号
和并列连词连接起来。这是考研长难句必考的重点，记得复习哦！
