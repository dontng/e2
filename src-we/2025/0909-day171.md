## 原句

> In other words, at a time when the working class has turned the country on its political head, frustrated that the opportunity that once defined America is vanishing, one obvious solution is staring us in the face.

<div align="center">【2018英二 Text 1】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中有几个从句？分别开始和结束于哪里？

## 生词

✅ in other words 换句话说  
✅ turn... on its head 使...... 完全改变思路  
✅ frustrated adj. 挫败的，失意的，泄气的  
✅ vanish v. 消失  
✅ stare v. 凝视，盯着

## 分析句子

找谓语动词
:
has turned/defined/is vanishing/is staring

断开
: 通过标点符号逗号以及连接词when/that/that来断开

## 语法重点

✅ **断开长难句和嵌套结构**

**从句** 1：when the working class has turned the country on its political head

从句 2：that the opportunity (that…) is vanishing

从句 3：that once defined America

其中，从句 1 中嵌套了从句 2，从句 2 中嵌套了从句 3，层层嵌套。

✅ **找主句**

**In other words, at a time…, one obvious solution is staring us in the face.**

## 参考译文

换句话说，在一个“工人阶级已完全改变了这个国家的政治思路、因曾经定义美国的机会逐渐消失而受挫”的时代，一个明显的解决办法正摆在我们面前。
