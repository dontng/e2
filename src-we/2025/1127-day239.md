## 原句

> Businesses should engage in conversations with IT, compliance, risk, and legal teams to review their privacy policy, and include in the discussion the customer/user experience designers and coders responsible for the company’s user interface, as well as the marketers and advertisers responsible for sign-ups, checkout baskets, pricing, and promotions.

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**虽然本句中有两个谓语动词，但是不能断开为两件事，因为中间由并列连词 and连接，且and后面的句子不完整，要跟前半句共享相同的成分，所以不能断开。**

**✅ 平行结构**

**Businesses**

**should engage in conversations with IT, compliance, risk, and legal teams to review their privacy policy,**

**and include in the discussion**

**① the customer/user experience designers and coders responsible for the company’s user interface,**

**② as well as the marketers and advertisers responsible for sign-ups, checkout baskets, pricing, and promotions.**

**本句的难点就在于包含了 4处平行（并列）结构。**

**平行结构 1：如上，本句中有两个谓语动词 should engage和include，通过 and并列，构成平行（并列）结构，二者的主语都是Businesses，表示“企业应该做这两件事”。**

**平行结构 2：谓语动词 include后两个宾语 designers and coders和marketers and advertisers平行并列，通过as well as连接，表示“包括这两类人在讨论中”。**

**平行结构 3： IT, compliance, risk, and legal teams 这四个名词（词组）通过逗号和 and连接，跟在词组 engage in conversations with 之后，表示 “参与到与四个部门的谈话中”。**

**平行结构 4： sign-ups, checkout baskets, pricing, and promotions 这四个名词（词组）通过逗号和 and连接，跟在词组 responsible for之后，表示“负责四个方面”。**

**✅ 简化句子**

**should engage (in conversations) with IT, compliance, risk, and legal teams to review their privacy policy,**

**简化后：**

1. **should engage (in conversations)****…****,**

2. **and** **include****…**

**① … designers and coder s …,**

**② as well as … marketers and advertisers ….**

**注意： in介词短语原本是句子的非核心成分，所以应该被简化掉，但是由于 engage in 是一个词组，所以为了表意完整，因此可以保留 in介词短语。**
