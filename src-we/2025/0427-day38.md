## 原句

> In his article “How Intelligent Is Intelligence Testing?”, Sternberg notes that traditional tests best assess analytical and verbal skills but fail to measure creativity and practical knowledge, components also critical to problem solving and life success.

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句：

根据连接词that，可以把句子断开为2段。

【句1】主句：In his article “How Intelligent Is Intelligence Testing?”, Sternberg notes

【句2】宾语从句：that traditional tests best assess analytical and verbal skills but fail to measure creativity and practical knowledge, components also critical to problem solving and life success.

⚠️注意：

1）并列连词but后的句子不完整，则不能断开。

2）引号中的句子How Intelligent Is Intelligence Testing其实是同位语，解释说明前面的名词article，所以不用断开。

## 参考译文

在他的文章《智商测试有多智能？》中，斯腾伯格指出，传统的测试能够对分析能力和语言能力做出最佳评估，但不能评估创造能力和实践知识，这些因素对问题的解决和人生的成功（来说）也是至关重要的。
