## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 that/even if/that和标点逗号，可以把句子断开为4段。**

**【句 1】主句： Branch points out**

**【句 2】宾语从句：that, …, unofficial educational materials … are being distributed to teachers.**

**【句 3】让步状语从句：even if a growing number of official guidelines and textbooks reflect scientific consensus on climate change**

**【句 4】定语从句：that convey more biased perspectives**

**2 嵌套结构**

**句 2that宾语从句中嵌套了句3 even if让步状语从句和句4that定语从句。**
