## 原句

> On top of this, they produce value from their extensive educational programs, their positive impact on the climate through carbon sequestration, their contribution to our cultural and artistic life, and of course through tourism.

<div align="center">【2023 英语二 Text 2】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中的平行结构是什么与什么平行并列？

## 生词

✅ extensive /ɪkˈstensɪv/ adj. 广泛的；大量的  
✅ extensively /ɪkˈstensɪvli/ adv. 广泛地；大量地；广阔地  
✅ positive impact 积极的影响  
✅ carbon sequestration 碳固定；碳封存；碳回收  
✅ contribution /ˌkɒntrɪˈbjuːʃ(ə)n/ n. 贡献，促成作用；捐款，捐助

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**本句中只有一个谓语动词 produce，所以本句只表达了一件事，是个简单句，不需要断开。**

**✅ 简化句子**

**简化的过程中：**

**<s>On top of this,</s>** **they produce value** **<s>from their extensive educational programs, their positive impact on the climate through carbon sequestration, their contribution to our cultural and artistic life,</s>** **<s>and of course through tourism</s>****<s>.</s>**

**简化后的核心内容：**

**… they produce value …**

**✅ 平行结构**

**平行结构一：介词 from后跟了三个名词词组作宾语，通过逗号连接，构成平行并列结构。**

**1）their extensive educational programs,**

**2）their positive impact on the climate through carbon sequestration,**

**3）their contribution to our cultural and artistic life,**

**注意：如上第 3点中介词 to 的后面 our cultural和artistic life，通过并列连词and连接，也是构成了平行结构，但是由于比较简单，所以不用特别注意。**

**平行结构二：整个 from介词短语和through介词短语，通过并列连词and连接，否成平行并列结构。**

**1） from their extensive educational programs, their positive impact on the climate through carbon sequestration, their contribution to our cultural and artistic life,**

**2 ） and of course through tourism**

## 参考译文

**最重要的是，它们通过广泛的教育项目产生价值，通过固碳对气候产生积极影响，通过对我们的文化和艺术生活做出贡献，当然还包括通过旅游业创造的价值。**
