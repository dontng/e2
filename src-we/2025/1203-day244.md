## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 who/that，可以把句子断开为3段。**

**【句 1】主句： Now, with record-high home prices and historically low inventory, there’s an increased urgency in such regulation, particularly among those**

**【句 2】定语从句：who worry**

**【句 3】宾语从句：that developers will come in and buy up swaths of housing to flip for a fortune on the short-term rental market.**

**注意：句 3中并列连词 and 后的句子不完整，则不能断开。**

**✅ 简化主句**

**简化的过程中：**

**【句** **1】主句：****<s>Now</s>****<s>,</s>** **<s>with record-high home prices and historically low inventory</s>****,** **there** **’** **s** **<s>an</s>** **<s>increased</s>** **urgency** **<s>in such regulation</s>****<s>,</s>** **<s>particularly among those</s>**

**简化后的核心内容：**

**【句 1】主句： … there’s … urgency …**

**✅ 嵌套结构**

**句 2 who定语从句中嵌套了句3that宾语从句。**
