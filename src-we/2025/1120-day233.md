## 原句

> It has been estimated that this generation, due to the pressures of technology, the wish for personal fulfilment and desire for diversity, will work for 17 different employers over the course of their working life and have five different careers.

**思考题:**

本句如何断开和简化？

## 生词

✅ estimate /ˈestɪmeɪt/ v. 估计，估算  
✅ estimation /ˌestɪˈmeɪʃn/ n. 估计；判断；评价  
✅ fulfilment /fʊlˈfɪlmənt/ n. 实现， 履行； 满足感，成就感  
✅ personal fulfilment 自我实现  
✅ diversity /daɪˈvɜːsəti/ n. 多样性，多样化  
✅ diverse /daɪˈvɜːs/ adj. 不同的，多种多样的；形形色色的

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 that，可以把句子断开为2段。**

**【句 1】主句： It has been estimated**

**【句 2】主语从句：that this generation, due to the pressures of technology, the wish for personal fulfilment and desire for diversity, will work for 17 different employers over the course of their working life and have five different careers.**

**注意：第二个并列连词 and 后的句子不完整，则不能断开。**

**✅ 简化长难句**

**主句简单，不用简化。从句简化如下：**

**【句 2】主语从句： that this generatio n, due to the pressures of technology, the wish for personal fulfilment and desire for diversity, will work for 17 different employers over the course of their working life and (will) have five different careers.**

**从句简化后：**

**that … generation … will work … and (will) have … careers.**

**✅ 平行结构**

**平行结构一：**

**如上从句简化后，主语 generation 后，两个谓语动词 will work 和 (will) have 通过 and连接，构成平行并列结构。**

**平行结构二：**

**due to the pressures of technology, the wish for personal fulfilment and desire for diversity**

**如上， due to后接三个名词词组，通过逗号和and连接，构成平行结构：**

**1 ） the pressures of technology,**

**2 ） the wish for personal fulfilment**

**3 ） and desire for diversity**

## 参考译文

**据估计，由于技术的压力、自我实现的愿望和对多样性的渴望，这一代人将在他们的职业生涯中为 17 个不同的雇主工作，并且从事 5 种不同的职业。**
