## 原句

> Restrictions that keep highly polluting cars out of certain areas — city centres, “school streets”, even individual roads — are a response to the absence of a larger effort to properly enforce existing regulations and require auto companies to bring their vehicles into compliance.

## 语法重点

✅
分裂结构

插入语，解释说明前面的certain areas

— city centres, “school streets”, even individual roads —

✅
嵌套结构

主句中嵌套了定语从句that keep highly polluting cars out of certain areas，修饰限定Restrictions。

✅
平行结构

Restrictions… are a response to the absence of a larger effort to

properly enforce existing regulations

and require auto companies to bring their vehicles into compliance.

这是两个非谓语动词 to do 的词组并列（后一个省略了 to），通过 and 连接，构成平行结构，修饰名词 effort。

非谓语动词 to do 的词组作宾补，其中 into 介词短语修饰动词 bring，表示结果。

## 参考译文

“禁止高污染汽车进入特定区域（如市中心、学区街道、甚至一些特别道路）”的限制措施是回应了缺乏更大规模行动去正确执行现有法规和要求汽车公司生产合规机动车。
