## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 when/what和标点破折号，可以把句子断开为4段。**

**【句 1】主句1： It comes at a time**

**【句 2】定语从句： when authors are already worried about the consequences of the proposed merger between PRH and another big publisher, Simon & Schuster**

**【句 3】主句 2 （破折号后引出进一步的解释说明）： the reduction in the number of unaligned UK publishers is likely to lead to fewer bidding wars, lower advances, and more conformity in terms of**

**【句 4】宾语从句：what is published.**

**✅ when定语从句的简化**

**when authors** **are** **<s>already</s>** **worried** **<s>about the consequences of the proposed merger</s>** **<s>between PRH and another big publisher</s>****,** **<s>Simon & Schuster</s>**

**简化后的核心内容： when authors are … worried …**

**✅ 平行结构**

**the reduction in thenumber of unaligned UK publishers is likely to lead to**

**fewer bidding wars,**

**lower advances,**

**and more conformity in terms of what is published.**

**✅ 嵌套结构**

**句 1主句中嵌套了句2when定语从句；句3主句中嵌套了句 4 what宾语从句。**
