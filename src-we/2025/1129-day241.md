## 原句

> A study last year by the National Center for Science Education, a non-profit group of scientists and teachers, looking at how state public schools across the country address climate change in science classes, gave barely half of US states a grade B+ or higher.

✅ barely /ˈbeəli/ adv. 仅仅，刚刚，勉强可能

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 how和标点逗号，可以把句子断开为2段。**

**【句 1】主句： A study last year by the National Center for Science Education, a non-profit group of scientists and teachers, looking at …, gave barely half of US states a grade B+ or higher (grades).**

**【句 2】宾语从句：how state public schools across the country address climate change in science classes**

**✅ 简化长难句**

**简化的过程中：**

**【句** **1】主句：****<s>A</s>** **study** **<s>last year</s>** **<s>by the National Center for Science Education</s>****<s>,</s>** **<s>a non-profit group of scientists and teachers</s>****<s>,</s>** **<s>looking at</s>****<s>…</s>****,** **gave** **<s>barely</s>** **half** **(of US states)** **a** **grade B+ or** **higher** **<s>(grades)</s>****.**

**【句** **2】宾语从句：****how** **<s>state public</s>** **schools** **<s>across the country</s>** **address** **<s>climate</s>** **change** **<s>in science classes</s>**

**简化后的核心内容：**

**【句 1】主句： … study … gave … half ( of US states ) … grade B+ or higher …**

**【句 2】宾语从句： how … schools … address … change …**

**注意： of 介词短语是句子的非核心成分，但是由于和 half 构成常用搭配，为了表意完整，在理解时也可以把这部分保留。**
