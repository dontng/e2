## 原句

> Efforts in Canada and elsewhere have been arguably languid at best, and have given us a situation where we frequently hear of employers begging for workers, even at times and in regions where unemployment is high.

<div align="center">【2021 英语二 Text 1】</div>

**思考题:**

如何断开？每个从句分别修饰什么？

## 生词

✅ arguably  adv. 可论证地，可争辩地；可以说  
✅ languid  adj. 迟缓的， 无力的； 慵懒的， 倦怠的  
✅ at best 最多； 最好的情况下  
✅ frequently  adv. 经常地， 频繁地  
✅ unemployment  n. 失业人数， 失业率； 失业， 失业状态

## 分析句子

找谓语动词

have been/have given/hear/is

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句

Efforts in Canada and elsewhere have been arguably languid at best, and have given us a situation

where we frequently hear of employers begging for workers, even at times and in regions

where unemployment is high.

⚠️
and
前后连接的不是句子
，
不能用来断开
；
而且
and
后面的句子不完整
，
也不能断开。

✅
理清句子的意思

主句
：
Efforts in Canada and elsewhere have been arguably languid at best, and have given us a situation

定语从句（修饰名词a situation）：where we frequently hear of employers begging for workers, even at times and in regions

定语从句（修饰名词regions）where unemployment is high.
