## 原句

> They chose this particular behavior for three reasons, according to study co-author Eric Schwitzgebel, a philosopher at the University of California, Riverside: students’attitudes on the topic are variable and unstable, behavior is easily measurable, and ethics literature largely agrees that eating less meat is good because it reduces environmental harm and animal suffering.

**思考题:**

本句中包含了几件事？如何断开？

## 生词

✅ particular /pəˈtɪkjələ(r)/ adj. 特定的，特别的，特殊的  
✅ philosopher /fəˈlɒsəfə(r)/ n. 哲学家；善于思考的人  
✅ variable /ˈveəriəbl/ adj. 多变的，易变的 n. 可变因素，变量  
✅ unstable /ʌnˈsteɪbl/ adj. 不稳 定的，易变的  
✅ measurable /ˈmeʒərəbl/ adj. 可测量的，可衡量的  
✅ ethics literature 伦理学文献，理论方面的文献

## 分析句子

找谓语动词
：chose/are/is/agrees/is/reduces

断开
：冒号
/逗号；and/that/because

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 and/that/because和标点冒号/逗号，可以把句子断开为6段。**

**【句 1】主句1：They chose this particular behavior for three reasons, according to study co-author Eric Schwitzgebel, a philosopher at the University of California, Riverside:**

**【句 2】主句2：students’attitudes on the topic are variable and unstable,**

**【句 3】主句3：behavior is easily measurable,**

**【句 4】主句4：and ethics literature largely agrees**

**【句 5】宾语从句：that eating less meat is good**

**【句 6】原因状语从句：because it reduces environmental harm and animal suffering.**

**✅ 简化主句 1**

**【句** **1】主句1：****They chose** **<s>this particular</s>** **behavior** **<s>for three reasons, according to study co-author Eric Schwitzgebel, a philosopher at the University of California, Riverside</s>**

**简化后的核心内容：**

**【句 1】主句1： They chose … behavior …**

**✅ 平行结构**

**冒号之前说 “有三个原因”，冒号后三个原因并列，构成平行结构：**

**students’attitudes on the topic are variable and unstable,**

**behavior is easily measurable,**

**and ethics literature largely agrees that eating less meat is good because it reduces environmental harm and animal suffering.**

**注意：平行结构最易出细节题！！！这里就出题了～**

**✅ 嵌套结构**

**and后的并列句中嵌套了that宾语从句，然后又嵌套了because原因状语从句。**
