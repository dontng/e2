## 原句

> France’s planned tax is a clear warning: Unless a broad consensus can be reached on reforming the international tax system, other nations are likely to follow suit, and American companies will face a cascade of different taxes from dozens of nations that will prove burdensome and costly.

**思考题:**

本句中包含了几件事？

## 生词

✅ consensus n. (意见等)一致，一致同意，共识  
✅ follow suit 效仿，照着做  
✅ acascade of... 一连串的......  
✅ cascade n. 倾泻，大量；小瀑布  
✅ burdensome adj. 难以承担的，繁重的  
✅ costly adj. 花钱多的，昂贵的，代价高的

## 语法重点

✅ **断开长难句**

根据标点和连接词可以断开成5段：

France’s planned tax is a clear warning:

Unless a broad consensus can be reached on reforming the international tax system,

other nations are likely to follow suit,

and American companies will face a cascade of different taxes from dozens of nations

that will prove burdensome and costly.

✅
理清句子关系

句子1之后的冒号说明其后是进一步解释说明的内容；

句子2是条件状语从句，unless表示“除非，如果不”；

句子3和4通过and构成并列句；

句子5是that定语从句，修饰前面的名词taxes。
