## 原句

> Young and old converge on one key point: Overwhelming majorities of both groups said they believe it is harder for young people today to get started in life than it was for earlier generations.

<div align="center">【2016 英二 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中一共包含了几件事？从哪里断开？

## 生词

✅ converge v. ( 观点或目标 ) 趋同； ( 线条、运动的物体等 ) 汇于一点  
✅ key point 关键点，要点  
✅ overwhelming majorities of... 绝大多数的......  
✅ overwhelming adj. 势不可挡的，压倒一切的；巨大的  
✅ get started 开始  
✅ generation n. 一代，一代人

## 分析句子

找谓语动词:
converge/said/believe/is/was

## 语法重点

✅ 冒号的作用

Young and old converge on one key point:…

通过标点冒号把前后句子并列在一起，冒号之后的内容是对冒号之前的内容进一步解释说明。

✅ 形式主语 it 和真正的主语 to do

it is harder for young people today to get started in life
