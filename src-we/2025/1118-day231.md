## 原句

> As the latest crop of students pen their undergraduate application form and weigh up their options, it may be worth considering just how the point, purpose and value of a degree has changed and what Generation Z need to consider as they start the third stage of their educational journey.

<div align="center">【2022 英语一 Text 2】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中包含了几件事？如何断开？

## 生词

✅ crop /krɒp/ n. 一批人，一群人  
✅ pen /pen/ v. 写  
✅ undergraduate /ˌʌndəˈɡrædʒuət/ n. 大学本科生  
✅ application /ˌæplɪˈkeɪʃ(ə)n/ n. 申请； 应用，实施  
✅ application form 申请表  
✅ option /ˈɒpʃn/ n. 选择，可供选择的事物  
✅ degree /dɪˈɡriː/ n. 学位  
✅ generation /ˌdʒenəˈreɪʃ(ə)n/ n. 一代； 一代人；产生  
✅ educational /ˌedʒuˈkeɪʃən(ə)l/ adj. 教育的；有教育意义的

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**先找谓语动词： pen/weigh/may be/has changed/need/start；**

**再根据连接词 As/how/and/what/as和标点逗号，可以把句子断开为5段。**

**【句 1】时间状语从句： As the latest crop of students pen their undergraduate application form and weigh up their options,**

**【句 2】主句：it may be worth considering just … and …**

**【句 3】宾语从句：how the point, purpose and value of a degree has changed**

**【句 4】宾语从句：what Generation Z need to consider**

**【句 5】时间状语从句：as they start the third stage of their educational journey.**

**✅ 平行结构**

**如下两个宾语从句通过 and连接，构成平行结构，跟在及物动词considering后面：**

**1）how the point, purpose and value of a degree has changed**

**2）what Generation Z need to consider**

**✅ 嵌套结构**

**主句中嵌套了句 3和句4两个宾语从句，同时句4宾语从句中又嵌套了句5时间状语从句。**
