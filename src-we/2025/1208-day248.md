## 原句

> The industry also points out that real grass requires considerable amounts of water, weed killer or other treatments and that people who lay fake grass tend to use their garden more.

## 生词

✅ considerable amounts of... 大量的……  
✅ considerable /kənˈsɪdərəbl/ adj. 相当多的；相当大的，相当重要的  
✅ weed killer 除草剂  
✅ treatment /ˈtriːtmənt/ n. 处理，加工；治疗  
✅ lay /leɪ/ v. 铺设，铺放；放置；下（蛋），产（卵）

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 that/and/that/who，可以把句子断开为4段。**

**【句 1】主句：The industry also points out … and …**

**【句 2】宾语从句：that real grass requires considerable amounts of water, weed killer or other treatments**

**【句 3】宾语从句：that people … tend to use their garden more.**

**【句 4】定语从句：who lay fake grass**

**✅ 平行结构**

**两个 that宾语从句平行并列，通过and连接，跟在及物动词词组points out后。**

**Theindustry also points out**

**1 ） that real grass requires considerable amounts of water, weed killer or other treatments**

**2 ） and that people … tend to use their garden more.**

**3 嵌套结构**

**主句中嵌套了两个 that宾语从句，在第二个宾语从句中又嵌套了who定语从句。**

## 参考译文
