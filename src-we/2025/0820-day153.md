## 原句

> Rather than just focusing on GDP, over 40 different sets of criteria from health, education and civil society engagement have been measured to get a more rounded assessment of how countries are performing.

<div align="center">【2017 英一 Text 3】</div>

**思考题:**

本句如何断开？主句的核心内容是什么？

## 生词

✅ rather than… 而不是  
✅ focus on… 集中于，聚焦于 ……  
✅ criteria  n. 标准，条件（ criterion 的复数）  
✅ civil society engagement 公民社会参与度  
✅ measure v. 测量，估量，判断  
✅ rounded  adj. 完善的，全面的  
✅ assessment  n. 评价，评定，判定

## 分析句子

找谓语动词:
have been measured/are performing

简化:

## 语法重点

✅ **断开长难句**

**主语：Rather than just focusing on GDP, over 40 different sets of criteria from health, education and civil society engagement have been measured to get a more rounded assessment of**

**宾语从句：how countries are performing.**

**✅ 简化主句**

**Rather than just focusing on GDP, over 40 different sets of criteria from health, education and civil society engagement have been measured to get a more rounded assessment of...**

**去掉非核心的介词短语、限定词、形容词和非谓语动词词组，就找到了主句的核心：**

**… sets of criteria… have been measured…**

**⚠️ 拓展：**

**简化句子的目的是为了看懂句子，而不是纠结于具体的一两个单词是否要去掉。有时为了完整的表意，个别的词可以不用去掉，例如本句中的 of criteria，跟在 sets 后构成一个完整的词组 sets of criteria。**

**所以学习语法和长难句是为了能看懂句子，实用即可，记住静姐的话，不纠结～～**
