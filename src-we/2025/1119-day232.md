## 原句

> This is not to say that there is no point in getting a degree, but rather stress that a degree is not for everyone, that the switch from classroom to lecture hall is not an inevitable one and that other options are available.

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 that/that/that/and/that和标点逗号，可以把句子断开为5段。**

**【句 1】主句：This is not to say …, but rather stress …, … and …**

**【句 2】宾语从句：that there is no point in getting a degree**

**【句 3】宾语从句：that a degree is not for everyone**

**【句 4】宾语从句：that the switch from classroom to lecture hall is not an inevitable one**

**【句 5】宾语从句：that other options are available.**

**✅ 平行结构**

**本句的难点就在于两处平行结构，尤其注意连接平行结构的并列连词（词组）。**

**1） not to say that there is no point in getting a degree, but rather (to) stress…**

**2） that a degree is not for everyone, that the switch from classroom to lecture hall is not an inevitable one and that other options are available**

## 参考译文

**这并不是说获得学位毫无意义，而是强调学位并不适合每个人，从（高中）教室到（大学）讲堂的转换并非一件必然会发生的事，还有其他选项可以选择。**
