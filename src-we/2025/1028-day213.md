## 原句

> In a 2019 survey of University of Georgia students, meanwhile, the career office found the most desirable trait in a future employer was the ability to offer secure employment (followed by professional development and training, and then inspiring purpose).

<div align="center">【2020 英语二 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

本句去修饰之后，核心内容是什么？

## 生词

✅ meanwhile adv. 与此同时  
✅ the most desirable trait 最受欢迎的品质、特点  
✅ employer n. 雇主，老板  
✅ secure adj. 稳定的，稳固的  
✅ employment n. 雇用，就业  
✅ professional development 职业发展

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句

主句：In a 2019 survey of University of Georgia students, meanwhile, the career office found

宾语从句：the most desirable trait in a future employer was the ability to offer secure employment (followed by professional development and training, and then inspiring purpose).

✅
简化长难句

主句：… the career office found

宾语从句：… trait… was the ability…

## 参考译文

与此同时，Georgia大学就业办公室在2019年一项针对该校学生的调查中发现，未来雇主最受欢迎的品质是提供稳定工作的能力（其后分别是职业发展及培训、激励人心的目标）。
