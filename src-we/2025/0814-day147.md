## 原句

> While younger people are somewhat more optimistic than their elders about the prospects for those starting out today, big majorities in both groups believe those “just getting started in life” face a tougher climb than earlier generations in reaching such signpost achievements as securing a good-paying job, starting a family, managing debt, and finding affordable housing.

<div align="center">【2016 英二 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中的主句和从句在去修饰找核心之后，余下的核心内容是什么？

## 生词

✅ somewhat adv. 稍微，有点  
✅ optimistic adj. 乐观的，乐观主义的  
✅ prospect n. 可能性，机会；希望，前景；景色  
✅ climb n. ( 社会地位 ) 上升，提高； ( 职务 ) 晋升  
✅ signpost n. 路标，标志物，指示牌  
✅ secure  v. 获得，取得，实现；握紧，抓牢

## 分析句子

找谓语动词:
are/believe/face

断开:
通过标点符号逗号，连接词while和分析主谓（主谓主谓）来断开长难句

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句

通过逗号、连接词
while
和分析主谓，可以很容易找到主句和两个从句：

主句：
big majorities in both groups believe

从句
1
（让步状语从句）：
While younger people are somewhat more optimistic than their elders about the prospects for those starting out today

从句
2
（宾语从句）：
those
“
just getting started in life
”
face a tougher climb than earlier generations in reaching such signpost achievements as securing a good-paying job, starting a family, managing debt, and finding affordable housing

✅
简化长难句

本句之所以特别长和难的主要原因都在补充说明、修饰限定的非核心成分太多。可以先“去修饰找核心”，看懂核心之后，再把非核心的加回来一点点看懂。

简化后：

While… people are… more optimistic…, … majorities… believe those… face … climb…

✅
平行结构

4
个
doing
词组，通过逗号和并列连词
and
连接，构成平行结构：

1. securing a good-paying job, 2. starting a family, 3. managing debt, and 4. finding affordable housing
