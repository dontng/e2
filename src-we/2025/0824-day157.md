## 原句

> Infants are wired to look at parents’faces to try to understand their world, and if those faces are blank and unresponsive—as they often are when absorbed in a device—it can be extremely disconcerting for the children.

<div align="center">【2017 英语二 Text 2】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中包含了几件事？两个并列连词 and 前后分别并列了什么？

## 生词

✅ infant  n. 婴儿，幼儿  
✅ be wired to do  天生、自然就会做某事  
✅ blank  adj. 没有感情的，不感兴趣的；空白的  
✅ unresponsive  adj. 无反应的  
✅ be absorbed in...  全神贯注于……（被吸引住）  
✅ disconcerting  adj. 使人不安的，令人尴尬的，打扰人的

如上，一共有4个谓语动词，说明本句中包含了4件事。

✅ **断开长难句**

**1 Infants are wired to look at parents’faces to try to understand their world,**

**2 and … it can be extremely disconcerting for the children.**

**3 if those faces are blank and unresponsive**

**4 —as they often are when absorbed in a device—**

## 参考译文

婴幼儿天生会看父母的脸，来试图了解他们的世界，而且如果这些脸是面无表情而且没有反应的——就像他们沉浸在（数字）设备中时一样——这会让孩子们非常不安。
