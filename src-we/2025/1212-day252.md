## 原句

> The participants filled out questionnaires about how often they did things that were altruistic and positive, like sacrificing their own interests to help a friend, or rebellious and negative, like getting drunk or staying out late.

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 how/that，可以把句子断开为3段。**

**【句 1】主句：The participants filled out questionnaires about**

**【句 2】宾语从句：howoften they did things**

**【句 3】定语从句：that werealtruistic and positive, like sacrificing their own interests to help a friend, or rebellious and negative, like getting drunk or staying out late.**

**✅ 平行结构**

**平行结构一： 两个形容词词组并列，通过并列连词 or 连接，构成平行结构。**

**1 ） altruistic and positive, like sacrificing their own interests to help a friend,**

**2 ） or rebellious and negative, like getting drunk or staying out late**

**平行结构二：两个形容词并列，通过并列连词 and连接，构成平行结构。**

**altruistic and positive**

**平行结构三： 两个形容词并列，通过并列连词 and连接，构成平行结构。**

**rebellious and negative**

**平行结构四： 两个形容词并列，通过并列连词 or 连接，构成平行结构。**

**getting drunk or staying out late**

**✅ 嵌套结构**

**主句中嵌套了 how宾语从句，而how宾语从句中又嵌套了that定语从句，层层嵌套。**

## 参考译文

**参与者填写了问卷，内容为他们做利他和积极事情的频率，如牺牲自身利益来帮助朋友，或做叛逆、消极事情的频率，比如喝醉或在外面待到很晚。**
