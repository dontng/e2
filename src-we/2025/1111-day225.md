## 原句

> Between them, the five biggest tech companies have spent an average of only $3.4 billion a year on sub-$1 billion acquisitions over the past five years — a drop in the ocean compared with their massive financial reserves, and the more than $130 billion of venture capital that was invested in the U.S. last year.

**思考题:**

本句如何断开和简化？

## 生词

✅ acquisition  n. 购买， 获得  
✅ adrop in the ocean 沧海一粟  
✅ compare with… 与 …… 相比较  
✅ massive financial reserves 巨大的资金储备  
✅ venture capital 风险资本

## 语法重点

✅
找谓语动词

Between them, the five biggest tech companies
have spent
an average of only $3.4 billion a year on sub-$1 billion acquisitions over the past five years
—
a drop in the ocean compared with their massive financial reserves, and the more than $130 billion of venture capital that
was invested
in the U.S. last year.

✅
断开长难句

主句：
Between them, the five biggest tech companies have spent an average of only $3.4 billion a year on sub-$1 billion acquisitions over the past five years
—
a drop in the ocean compared with their massive financial reserves, and the more than $130 billion of venture capital

定语从句：that was invested in the U.S. last year.

✅
简化长难句

主句：
Between them, the five biggest
tech companies have spent an average of only $3.4 billion a year
on sub-$1 billion acquisitions over the past five years
—
a drop in the ocean compared with their massive financial reserves, and the more than $130 billion of venture capital

定语从句：
that was invested
in the U.S. last year
.
