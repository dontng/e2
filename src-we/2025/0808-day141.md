## 原句

> Under the plan, for example, the agency said it would not prosecute landowner or businesses that unintentionally kill, harm, or disturb the bird, as long as they had signed a range-wide management plan to restore prairie chicken habitat.

<div align="center">【2016 英语二 Text 2】</div>

**思考题:**

本句如何断开？包含了几个从句？分别是什么从句？

## 生词

✅ prosecute v. 起诉，控告，检举  
✅ unintentionally adv. 无意地，非故意地  
✅ as long as … 只要；和……一样长  
✅ sign a range-wide management plan 签署大范围的管理计划  
✅ prairie chicken habitat 草原榛鸡栖息地

## 分析句子

**找谓语动词：said/would not prosecute/kill/harm/disturb/had signed**

**断开：通过标点逗号以及连接词that/as long as和分析主谓来断开长难句**

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句

根据标点、连接词和分析主谓 3 种办法来断开长难句。

1 主句：Under the plan, for example, the agency said

2 宾语从句：it would not prosecute landowner or businesses

3 定语从句：that unintentionally kill, harm, or disturb the bird,

4 条件状语从句：as long as they had signed a range-wide management plan to restore prairie chicken habitat.

## 参考译文

在此项计划之下，例如，该机构表示它不会起诉非故意杀死、伤害或骚扰该鸟类的土地所有者或企业，只要他们已经签署一项为恢复草原榛鸡栖息地而制定的大范围管理计划。
