## 原句

> The cases the court overturned said that if a business was shipping a customer’s purchase to a state where the business didn’t have a physical presence such as a warehouse or office, the business didn’t have to collect sales tax for the state.

<div align="center">【2019 英语一 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中一共几句话？几句之间都是什么 关系？

## 生词

✅ overturn v. 推翻，撤销  
✅ ship v. 运送，以船运送；乘船  
✅ physical presence 实体存在  
✅ warehouse n. 仓库

## 分析句子

找谓语动词
：
overturned/said/was shipping/didn't have/didn't have to collect

断开
：
通过标点符号逗号以及连接词that/if/where和分析主谓来断开长难句

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句

主句：The cases… said…

从句：the court overturned

从句：if a business was shipping a customer’s purchase to a state

从句：where the business didn’t have a physical presence such as a warehouse or office,

从句：that… the business didn’t have to collect sales tax for the state.

✅ 嵌套结构

主句1中嵌套了从句2；

从句5中嵌套了从句3，从句3中又嵌套了从句4。

## 参考译文

最高法院所推翻的案例认为，如果企业将顾客购得的商品运往某个州，而该企业在本州并没有实体存在（比如仓库或办公室），那么该企业就不需要为本州代收销售税。
