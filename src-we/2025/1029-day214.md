## 原句

> “Millennials wanted more flexibility in their lives,” notes Tanya Michelsen, Associate Director of YouthSight, a UK-based brand manager that conducts regular 60-day surveys of British youth, in findings that might just as well apply to American youth.

<div align="center">【2020 英语二 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

如何断开长难句？

## 生词

✅ Millennials 千禧年一代，千禧一代（出生于1981-1995年间）  
✅ flexibility n. 灵活性，弹性  
✅ Associate Director 副总监  
✅ brand manager 品牌管理公司；品牌经理  
✅ conduct v. 进行，组织，实施；带领，引导  
✅ apply to sth. 适用于某事，应用于某事

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句

“Millennials wanted more flexibility in their lives,”

notes Tanya Michelsen… in findings…(4定语从句)

Associate Director of YouthSight, a UK-based brand manager that conducts regular 60-day surveys of British youth,

that might just as well apply to American youth.

✅
同位语层层深入解释

在句子2中，Tanya Michelsen后接同位语Associate Director of YouthSight来解释说明；YouthSight后接同位语a UK-based brand manager来解释说明。2个同位语，层层深入解释。

✅
倒装句

句2是倒装句，原本正常的语序是Tanya Michelsen notes...，但由于主语 Tanya Michelsen后面的同位语较长，为了避免头重脚轻，所以把句子倒装，将主语和较长的同位语一起往后放。

## 参考译文

“千禧一代希望生活有更多的灵活性，”YouthSight副总监Tanya Michelsen在可能同样适用于美国青年的调查发现中表示（YouthSight是英国一家品牌管理公司，每60天对英国青年展开定期调查）。
