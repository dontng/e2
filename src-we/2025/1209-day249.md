## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 while和标点逗号，可以把句子断开为2段。**

**句 1和句2之间通过while并列。**

**【句 1】However, the use of artificial grass must comply with the legal and policy safeguards in place to protect biodiversity and ensure sustainable drainage**

**【句 2】 while measures such as the strengthened biodiversity duty should serve to encourage public authorities to consider sustainable alternatives.**

**✅ 简化句子**

**简化的过程中：**

**【句** **1】** **<s>However,</s>** **<s>the</s>** **use** **<s>of artificial grass</s>** **must comply** **<s>with the legal and policy safeguards</s>** **<s>in place to protect biodiversity and ensure sustainable drainage</s>**

**【句** **2】并列句：****while measures** **<s>such as the strengthened biodiversity duty</s>** **should serve** **<s>to encourage public authorities</s>** **<s>to consider sustainable alternatives</s>****.**

**简化后的核心内容：**

**【句 1】 … use … must comply …**

**【句 2】 while measures … should serve …**
