## 原句

> These unilateral developments differ in their specifics, but they are all designed to tax multinationals on income and revenue that countries believe they should have a right to tax, even if international tax rules do not grant them that right.

<div align="center">【2020 英语一 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中包含了几件事？ 如何断开？

## 生词

✅ unilateral  adj. 单方面的，单边的；单方面做出的  
✅ differ  v. 相异，有区别  
✅ specifics  n. 详情，细节；特性  
✅ tax  v. 收税  n. 税款  
✅ multinational  n. 跨国公司  
✅ grant  v. 准予，允许，授予

## 分析句子

找谓语动词
：
differ/are designed/believe/should have/do not grant

断开
：
通过标点符号
逗号
连接词
but/that/even if以及分析主谓（主谓主谓）

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句

根据标点逗号和连接词but、that和even if，可以断开长难句，如下：

These unilateral developments differ in their specifics,

but they are all designed to tax multinationals on income and revenue

that (countries believe) they should have a right to tax,

even if international tax rules do not grant them that right.

✅
简短主谓作插入语

在句子中常会出现简短的主谓作插入语，例如sb. think/ sb. say等，这种在分析句子时可以直接作为插入语先不看。

例：You are a beautiful girl,
which (I think) is very obvious
.

例：…
they are all designed to tax multinationals on income and revenue
that (countries believe) they should have a right to tax
…

⚠️ 拓展：

考研真题中的定语从句如果较长，不一定非要翻译到所修饰的名词前，也可以另起一句，例如本句中的3定语从句，就可以翻译成独立的一句。
