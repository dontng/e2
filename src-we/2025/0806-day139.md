## 原句

> But we’re going to have questions like that where we have things we’re doing that don’t make sense when the market changes and the world changes.

<div align="center">【2016 英一 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中包含了几件事？如何断开？如何翻译？

## 生词

今日无生词

## 分析句子

找谓语动词:
are going to have/have/are doing/don't make/ changes/changes

断开:
通过连接词where/that/when/and和分析主谓来断开长难句

✅ 捋顺翻译
