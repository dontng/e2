## 原句

> These eggs, which are making their debut now on shelves for as much as $8 a dozen, are still labeled organic and animal-friendly, but they’re also from birds that live on farms using regenerative agriculture—special techniques to cultivate rich soils that can trap green-house gases.

**思考题:**

本句包含了几件事？如何断开？

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 which/but/that/that和标点逗号，可以把句子断开为5段。**

**【句 1】主句1： These eggs, …, are still labeled organic and animal-friendly,**

**【句 2】定语从句：which are making their debut now on shelves for as much as $8 a dozen**

**【句 3】主句2：but they’re also from birds**

**【句 4】定语从句：that live on farms using regenerative agriculture—special techniques to cultivate rich soils**

**【句 5】定语从句：that can trap green-house gases.**

**✅ 分裂结构**

**These eggs, which are making their debut now on shelves for as much as $8 a dozen, are still labeled organic and animal-friendly,**

**如上， which非限定性定语从句插入到句子中间，两端有成对的逗号隔开，造成了原本连贯的句子分裂。**
