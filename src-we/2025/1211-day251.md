## 原句

> Perhaps, she suggests, the trend will change our approach to learning from a focus on individual facts and memorization to an emphasis on more conceptual thinking—something that is not available on the Internet.

**思考题:**

本句如何断开和简化？

## 生词

✅ approach /əˈprəʊtʃ/ n. 方式，方法；道路  
✅ individual /ˌɪndɪˈvɪdʒuəl/ adj. 个别的；个人的  
✅ memorization /ˌmeməˈrɪzeɪʃən/ n. 记忆  
✅ emphasis /ˈemfəsɪs/ n. 强调  
✅ conceptual /kənˈseptʃuəl/ adj. 概念的，观念的

## 分析句子

：suggests/will change/is not

：去掉非核心的副词、插入语、限定词和介词短语

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 that，可以把句子断开为2段。**

**【句 1】主句： Perhaps, she suggests, the trend will change our approach to learning from a focus on individual facts and memorization to an emphasis on more conceptual thinking—something**

**【句 2】定语从句： that is not available on the Internet.**

**注意： 谓语动词 suggests 位于插入语中，插入语是句子的非核心成分，可以先去掉不看，所以不用断开句子。**

**✅ 简化句子**

**简化的过程中：**

**【句** **1】主句：****<s>Perhaps,</s>** **<s>she suggests,</s>** **<s>th</s>** **<s>e</s>** **trend will** **change** **<s>our</s>** **approach** **<s>to learning from a focus on individual facts and memorization to an emphasis on more conceptual</s>** **<s>thinking</s>** **<s>—</s>** **<s>something</s>**

**【句** **2】定语从句：****that is not available** **<s>on the Internet</s>****.**

**简化后的核心内容：**

**【句 1】主句：… trend will change … approach …**

**【句 2】定语从句： that is not available …**
