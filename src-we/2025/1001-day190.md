## 原句

> The strategy also aims to ensure that carbon in woody material removed from the forests is locked away in the form of solid lumber or burned as biofuel in vehicles that would otherwise run on fossil fuels.

<div align="center">【2019 英语二 Text 2】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中有几个谓语动词？包含了几件事？

## 生词

✅ woody adj. 木质的  
✅ lock away sth. 把某物锁起来、封存起来  
✅ lumber n. 木材  
✅ biofuel n. 生物燃料  
✅ fossil fuel 矿物燃料，化石燃料

## 语法重点

✅ **判断谓语动词**

句中有多个动词，但判断句子数量的时候只看谓语动词

✅
断开长难句

1 主句：The strategy also aims to ensure

2 宾语从句：that carbon in woody material removed from the forests is locked away in the form of solid lumber or （主语+is）burned as biofuel in vehicles

3 定语从句：that would otherwise run on fossil fuels.

✅
简化句子

宾语从句中的多个介词短语（in woody material / in the form / of solid lumber / as biofuel / in vehicles ）和非谓语动词的词组（removed from the forests）都是非核心成分

先不看，就找到了核心内容：

that carbon … is locked away… or （主语+is）burned…

## 参考译文
