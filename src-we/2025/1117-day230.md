## 原句

> Joana Lia Ferreira, an assistant professor of conservation and restoration at the NOVA School of Science and Technology, notes that archaeologists first defined the great material ages of human history—Stone Age, Iron Age, and so on—after examining artifacts in museums.

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据连接词 that，可以把句子断开为2段。**

**【句 1】主句： Joana Lia Ferreira, an assistant professor of conservation and restoration at the NOVA School of Science and Technology, notes**

**【句 2】宾语从句：that archaeologists first defined the great material ages of human history—Stone Age, Iron Age, and so on—after examining artifacts in museums.**

**✅ 简化长难句**

**去掉主句中的插入语（也是同位语），如下**

**去掉从句中的副词 /形容词/限定词/介词短语/插入语，如下**

**【句 2】宾语从句： that archaeologists first defined the great material ages of human history — Stone Age, Iron Age, and so on — after examining artifacts in museums.**

**简化后的核心内容：**

**【句 1】主句： Joana Lia Ferreira … notes**

**【句 2】宾语从句： that archaeologists … defined … ages …**
