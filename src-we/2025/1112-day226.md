## 原句

> However, critics say the big companies use such deals to buy their most threatening potential competitors before their businesses have a chance to gain momentum, in some cases as part of a “ buy and kill ” tactic to simply close them down.

**思考题:**

本句如何断开？

## 生词

✅ threatening /ˈθretnɪŋ/ adj. 威胁的，胁迫的  
✅ potential /pəˈtenʃl/ adj. 潜在的，可能的 n. 潜力，可能性  
✅ momentum /məˈmentəm/ n. 势头；动力  
✅ tactic /ˈtæktɪk/ n. 策略，手段

## 语法重点

✅ 断开长难句

**根据连接词 before 和 分析主谓（主谓主谓），可以把句子断开为 3段。**

**【句 1】主句： However, critics say**

**【句 2】省略了连接词的宾语从句： the big companies use such deals to buy their most threatening potential competitors …, in some cases as part of a “ buy and kill ” tactic to simply close them down.**

**【句 3】时间状语从句： before their businesses have a chance to gain momentum，**

**✅ 简化其中的宾语从句**

**the big companies use such deals to buy their most threatening potential competitors …, in some cases as part of a “ buy and kill ” tactic to simply close them down.**

**简化后： … companies use … deals …**

**注意：在分析句子时， 一定不要一个词一个词断开，而要把单词合并在一个意思群中 （由一个词组或者几个词组组成的）来看待。例如上面的 to buy their most threatening potential competitors，就是一个完整的 to do sth. 的意思群（或者说词组）。按照意思群去看句子，更能够帮助大家有效地看懂。**
