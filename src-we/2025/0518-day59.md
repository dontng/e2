## 原句

> To take this approach to the New Englanders normally means to start with the Puritans’theological innovations and their distinctive ideas about the church—important subjects that we may not neglect.

**思考题:**

本句中的主语和宾语分别是哪部分？什么来充当主语和宾语的？

## 语法重点

✅
断开长难句
：

根据连接词that，可以把句子断开为2段。

【句1】主句：To take this approach to the New Englanders normally means to start with the Puritans’ theological innovations and their distinctive ideas about the church—important subjects

【句2】定语从句：that we may not neglect.

✅
分析主句：

主句中的谓语动词是means，它前面的To take this approach to the New Englanders normally自然就是主语，而它后面的to start with the Puritans’ theological innovations and their distinctive ideas about the church是宾语。

⚠️ 本句的难点就在于主语和宾语都是较长较难的非谓语动词to do的词组，而不是普通的名词或代词作主语宾语。

✅ 简化主句：

简化的过程中：

【句1】主句：

this
approach
to the New Englanders
normally

with the Puritans’ theological innovations and their distinctive ideas
about the church
