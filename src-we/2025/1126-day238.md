## 原句

> Even more surprising is that more than half of “unretirees”—those who plan to work in retirement or went back to work after retiring—said they would be employed in their later years even if they had enough money to settle down, the survey showed.

## 语法重点

**✅ 断开长难句**

**根据标点破折号 /逗号、连接词that/who/even if和分析主谓（主谓主谓），可以把句子断开为5段。**

**【句 1】主句： Even more surprising is**

**【句 2】主语从句：that more than half of “unretirees”—those …—said …, ( the survey showed ).**

**【句 3】定语从句：who plan to work in retirement or went back to work after retiring**

**【句 4】宾语从句：they would be employed in their later years**

**【句 5】让步状语从句：even if they had enough money to settle down**

**✅ 主句是倒装句**

**主句： Even more surprising is that more than half of “unretirees” … said …**

**Even more surprising是形容词词组，通常不能作主语。**

**其实这句话的主语是 that主语从句，由于主句较长被后置，整个句子倒装。**

**主句还原为原本的正常语序：**

**That more than half of “unretirees ” … said… is even more surprising.**

**✅ 嵌套结构**

**句 1主句中嵌套了句2that主语从句；句2 that主语从句嵌套了句3who定语从句和句4宾语从句；而句4宾语从句中又嵌套了even if让步状语从句，层层嵌套。**
