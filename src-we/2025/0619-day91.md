## 原句

> Even though there is plenty of evidence that the quality of the teachers is the most important variable, teachers’unions have fought against getting rid of bad ones and promoting good ones.

<div align="center">【2012 英一 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

思考题： 此句中的嵌套结构和平行结构，你找到了吗？

## 生词

✅ evidence n. 证据  
✅ variable n. 变量，可变因素  
✅ teachers'union  教师工会  
✅ fight v. 吵架，打架；战斗，斗争 （过去式/过去分词fought）  
✅ fight against … 与 …… 打架  
✅ get rid of … 除掉, 去掉；摆脱  
✅ promote  v. 推动，促进；提拔；促销

## 分析句子

找谓语动词:
is / is / have fought

断开:
根据标点逗号和连接词Even though/that来断开句子
