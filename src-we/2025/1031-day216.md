## 原句

> Yes, more investment is needed, but passengers will not be willing to pay more indefinitely if they must also endure cramped, unreliable services, interrupted by regular chaos when timetables are changed, or planned maintenance is managed incompetently.

<div align="center">【2021 英语一 Text 1】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中包含了几件事？可以断成几段？

## 生词

✅ investment  n. 投资；投入  
✅ passenger  n. 乘客  
✅ be willing to do sth.  愿意做某事  
✅ indefinitely  adv. 无限期地；不确定地  
✅ endure  v. 忍受，忍耐；持续存在，持久  
✅ cramped  adj. 拥挤的，狭窄的  
✅ unreliable  adj. 不可靠的；靠不住的  
✅ interrupt  v. 打断，打扰  
✅ chaos  n. 混乱，无秩序状态  
✅ maintenance  n. 维护，保养；保持，维持  
✅ incompetently  adv. 无能力地，不胜任地

## 语法重点

✅
找谓语动词

Yes, more investment is needed, but passengers will not be willing to pay more indefinitely if they must also endure cramped, unreliable services, interrupted by regular chaos when timetables are changed, or planned maintenance is managed incompetently.

⚠️：to pay，interrupted和planned是非谓语动词。

✅ 断开长难句

Yes, more investment is needed,

but passengers will not be willing to pay more indefinitely

if they must also endure cramped, unreliable services, interrupted by regular chaos

when timetables are changed,

or planned maintenance is managed incompetently.

⚠️：句3中的逗号不能用来断开，因为隔开的不是句子。

✅ 理清句子之间的关系

句4+5合在一起修饰前面句3中的chaos，表示“在……时候造成混乱”。

…if they must also endure cramped, unreliable services, interrupted by regular chaos (when timetables are changed, or planned maintenance is managed incompetently).

## 参考译文

是的，需要更多投资，但如果乘客还必须忍受拥挤、不可靠的服务、和在时间表更改或者保养计划执行不力的时候造成混乱而中断的服务，他们将不会愿意一直接受涨价（无限期地支付更多费用）。
