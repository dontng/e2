## 原句

> Unhappy parents rarely are provoked to wonder if they shouldn’t have had kids, but unhappy childless folks are bothered with the message that children are the single most important thing in the world: obviously their misery must be a direct result of the gaping baby-size holes in their lives.

<div align="center">【2011年英一阅读 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

句子如何断开？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> are provoked/shouldn't have had/are bothered/are/must be

（5个谓语动词，意味着5件事，应该断开为5段）

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
非谓语动词to wonder不算数。</mark>

## 生词

✅ provoke /prəˈvəʊk/ v. 激怒，惹怒；刺激；煽动  
✅ wonder /ˈwʌndə(r)/ v. 想知道  
✅ be bothered with... 被......所困扰  
✅ misery /ˈmɪzəri/ n. 悲惨  
✅ gaping /ˈɡeɪpɪŋ/ adj. 裂开的，豁开的

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过标点逗号/冒号和连词if/but/that断开

- 【句1】主句：Unhappy parents rarely are provoked to wonder
- 【句2】宾语从句（跟在及物动词wonder后）：if they shouldn’t have had kids,
- 【句3】主句（与句1并列）：but unhappy childless folks are bothered with the message
- 【句4】同位语从句（解释说明前面的抽象名词message）：that children are the single most important thing in the world:
- 【句5】冒号后解释说明冒号前：obviously their misery must be a direct result of the gaping baby-size holes in their lives.

## 参考译文

不幸福的父母很少被激起对“自己是否本不该要孩子”的思考；但不幸福的无子女者会不断被“孩子是世界上唯一最重要的事情”这一信息所烦扰：显然他们的痛苦必定是他们生活中那些豁开的、孩子大小的窟窿所带来的直接后果。
