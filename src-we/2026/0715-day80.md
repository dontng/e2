## 原句

> In an essay entitled “Making It in America,” the author Adam Davidson relates a joke from cotton country about just how much a modern textile mill has been automated: The average mill has only two employees today, “a man and a dog. The man is there to feed the dog, and the dog is there to keep the man away from the machines.”

<div align="center">【2013年英二阅读 Text1】</div>

**思考题:**

冒号之前的句子核心内容是什么？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> relates / has been automated/ has / is / is

（5个谓语动词，意味着5件事，应该断开为5段）

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
非谓语动词entitled/Making/to feed/to keep不算数。</mark>

## 生词

✅ entitle /ɪnˈtaɪt(ə)l/ v. 称做……，起名为……  
✅ relate /rɪˈleɪt/ v. 讲述；把……联系起来  
✅ a modern textile mill 一个现代纺织厂  
✅ automate /ˈɔːtəmeɪt/ v. 自动化，自动操作  
✅ employee /ɪmˈplɔɪiː/ n. 雇员，员工

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过冒号/句号/逗号和连词how/and来断开句子。

- 【句1】主句1：In an essay entitled “Making It in America,” the author Adam Davidson relates a joke from cotton country about
- 【句2】宾语从句：just how much a modern textile mill has been automated:
- 【句3】主句2：The average mill has only two employees today, “a man and a dog.
- 【句4】与句5并列：The man is there to feed the dog,
- 【句5】与句4并列：and the dog is there to keep the man away from the machines.”

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">名词后的多个修饰</span>**

介词短语from cotton country 和介词加从句about just how much a modern textile mill has been automated都是修饰前面的名词a joke，这类多个修饰语（短的放前，长的在后）考研中经常考。

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">熟词僻意</span>**  
动词relate在此句中不表示“联系”，表示“讲述”。

形容词average在此句中不表示“平均的”，表示“普通的”。

## 参考译文

《在美国制造》一文中，作者亚当•戴维森提及一个有关棉花产地某现代纺织厂自动化程度的笑话：该普通纺织厂如今只有两名员工，“一个人和一只狗。人负责喂狗，而狗负责让人远离机器。”
