## 原句

> To encourage innovation and competition, the report calls for increased investment in research, the crafting of coherent curricula that improve students’ ability to solve problems and communicate effectively in the 21st century, increased funding for teachers and the encouragement of scholars to bring their learning to bear on the great challenges of the day.

**思考题:**

句中的多个平行结构出现在哪里？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> calls / improve

（2个谓语动词，意味着2件事，应该断开为2段）

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
非谓语动词To encourage/increased/crafting/to solve/increased/to bring/to bear不算数。communicate与前面的to solve并列，把相同的to省略掉了，所以也是非谓语动词。</mark>

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过逗号和连词that来断开句子。

- 【句1】主句：To encourage innovation and competition, the report calls for increased investment in research, the crafting of coherent curricula 句2, increased funding for teachers and the encouragement of scholars to bring their learning to bear on the great challenges of the day.
- 【句2】定语从句：that improve students’ ability to solve problems and communicate effectively in the 21st century

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">平行结构</span>**

之前我在《句句真研》语法书和课上专门给大家讲过—— <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">平行结构，也叫并列结构，就是把多个词、词组或句子并列在一起做一个成分，其中平行并列的内容必须词性相同、形式一致。平行并列结构的辨认主要是通过并列连词作为标志</span>。  
**注意：**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">三者或以上的并列，如果中间的并列连词都相同，只保留最后一处的并列连词，前面一律换成逗号</span>。所以，在本句中找到and就找到了平行结构结束的最后一部分。  
本句其实有三处平行结构：

innovation and competition

to solve problems and (to) communicate effectively
