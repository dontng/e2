## 原句

> Toyota Motor, for example, alleviated some of the damage from its recall crisis earlier this year with a relatively quick and well-orchestrated social-media response campaign, which included efforts to engage with consumers directly on sites such as Twitter and the social-news site Digg.

<div align="center">【2011年英一阅读 Text 3】</div>

**思考题:**

主句的核心内容是什么？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> alleviated/included

（2个谓语动词，意味着2件事，应该断开为2段）

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
-ed结尾的动词要先判断一下意思，如果意思表示主动，则是谓语动词，如上两个；反之，表示被动则是非谓语动词。</mark>

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
数谓语动词，判断句中包含了几件事，非谓语动词to engage不算数。</mark>

## 生词

✅ alleviate /əˈliːvieɪt/ v. 减轻；缓和，缓解  
✅ recall crisis 召回的危机  
✅ relatively /ˈrelətɪvli/ adv. 相对地  
✅ well-orchestrated adj. 精心策划的  
✅ orchestrate /ˈɔːkɪstreɪt/ v. 精心安排，密谋；编管弦乐曲  
✅ social-media n. 社交媒体  
✅ campaign /kæmˈpeɪn/ n. 活动；竞选活动  
✅ engage with sb. 与某人接洽；与某人交流

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过逗号和连词which断开句子。

- 【句1】主句：Toyota Motor, for example, alleviated some of the damage from its recall crisis earlier this year with a relatively quick and well-orchestrated social-media response campaign,
- 【句2】非限定性定语从句：which included efforts to engage with consumers directly on sites such as Twitter and the social-news site Digg.

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2
简化主句：</span></strong>去扩展找核心，去掉非核心的插入语/介词短语/名词词组表示时间

- 【句1】主句： Toyota Motor, for example, alleviated some of the damage from its recall crisis earlier this year with a relatively quick and well-orchestrated social-media response campaign,

简化后，主句的核心：
Toyota Motor... alleviated some of the damage...

注意
：简化的目的是为了帮助看懂句子，而不是为了精简到极致，所以为了表意完整，如上的介词短语of the damage就没有去掉。

## 参考译文

例如，丰田汽车公司通过发动相对迅速、协调有致的社交媒介回应活动，包括在微博Twitter和社会新闻网Digg等网站上与消费者直接互动，缓解了今年早些时候的召回危机所造成的部分损害。
