## 原句

> We are even farther removed from the unfocused newspaper reviews published in England between the turn of the 20th century and the eve of World War II, at a time when newsprint was dirt-cheap and stylish arts criticism was considered an ornament to the publications in which it appeared.

<div align="center">【2010年英一阅读 Text 1】</div>

**思考题:**

两个定语从句在哪里？其修饰的先行词分别是什么？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> are removed/was/was considered/appeared

（4个谓语动词，意味着4件事，应该断开为4段）

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
非谓语动词published不算数。</mark>

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">经过一个多月的每日一句练习，同学们已经熟悉了分析长难句的准备——“找谓语动词”，因此在接下来的视频讲解中就不再赘述，但会在推文中继续给大家展示这一环节（如上）。</mark>

## 生词

✅ unfocused adj. 不聚焦的，不集中的，无重点的  
✅ publish v. 出版，刊印，发行，发表  
✅ stylish adj. 高水平的；时尚的，现代风格的  
✅ ornament n. 装饰，装饰物；摆设，点缀  
✅ publication n. 出版物

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过连词when/and/(in) which 断开句子。

- 【句1】主句：We are even farther removed from the unfocused newspaper reviews published in England between the turn of the 20th century and the eve of World War II, at a time
- 【句2+3】定语从句（两个句子并列构成）： when newsprint was dirt-cheap and stylish arts criticism was considered an ornament to the publications
- 【句4】介词提前的定语从句：in which it appeared.

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2 简化主句：</span></strong>去掉非核心的副词、介词短语、非谓语动词

- 【句1】主句： We are even farther removed from the unfocused newspaper reviews published in England between the turn of the 20th century and the eve of World War II, at a time

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">定语从句中包含了并列句</span>**

when定语从句修饰先行词a time，表示“……的一段时间”，但要注意定语从句中还包含了and连接的并列句（即句2+句3）。

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">介词提前的定语从句</span>**  
in which是介词提前的定语从句，修饰先行词the publications，表示“……的出版物”。

介词原本在后：…the publications which it appeared in.

介词提前：…the publications in which it appeared.  
这部分用法详见《句句真研》语法书的定语从句
