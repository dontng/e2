## 原句

> In his Case Study House, Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life—few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers—but his belief that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable was widely shared.

<div align="center">【2011年英二阅读 Text 3】</div>

**思考题:**

句中有几个从句？分别在哪里呢？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> may have mispredicted / would impact / acquired / got / was / was shared

（6个谓语动词，意味着6件事，应该断开为6段）

## 生词

✅ mispredict /ˌmɪsprɪˈdɪkt/ v. 错误的预测，错误的预估  
✅ mechanical revolution 机械革命  
✅ acquire /əˈkwaɪə(r)/ v. 获得，得到  
✅ helicopter /ˈhelɪkɒptə(r)/ n. 直升飞机  
✅ clothes dryer 干衣机，烘干机  
✅ belief /bɪˈliːf/ n. 信念，信仰  
✅ self-sufficiency /ˌself səˈfɪʃnsi/ n. 自给自足  
✅ desirable /dɪˈzaɪərəb(ə)l/ adj. 令人满意的，值得拥有的  
✅ inevitable /ɪnˈevɪtəb(ə)l/ adj. 不可避免的；必然会发生的

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过破折号和连词how/though/but/that断开句子。

注意：that从句结束于其后的第二个谓语动词前。

- 【句1】主句1：In his Case Study House, Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted
- 【句2】宾语从句：just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life
- 【句3】两个破折号中的主句：—few American families acquired helicopters,
- 【句4】让步状语从句：though most eventually got clothes dryers—
- 【句5】主句2，与主句1并列：but his belief 句6 was widely shared.
- 【句6】同位语从句：that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable（解释说明前面的抽象名词belief）

## 参考译文

在罗夫•雷普森的案例研究住宅中，他可能错误预测了机械革命对日常生活可能产生的影响——尽管大多数美国家庭都拥有了干衣机，但很少有能拥有直升机的——但他认为自给自足既是理想的，也是必然的，这一信念却得到了广泛传播。
