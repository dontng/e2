## 原句

> Curbs on business-method claims would be a dramatic about-face, because it was the Federal Circuit itself that introduced such patents with its 1998 decision in the so-called State Street Bank case, approving a patent on a way of pooling mutual-fund assets.

<div align="center">【2010年英一阅读 Text 2】</div>

**思考题:**

句中的强调句在哪里？强调的内容是什么？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> would be/was/introduced

（3个谓语动词，意味着3件事，本来应该断开为3段；但由于强调句型it was… that…中包含了要强调的句子，两句合为一体，所以断开为2段）

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
非谓语动词approving/pooling不算数。</mark>

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">经过一个多月的每日一句练习，同学们已经熟悉了分析长难句的准备——“找谓语动词”，因此在接下来的视频讲解中就不再赘述，但会在推文中继续给大家展示这一环节（如上）。</mark>

## 生词

✅ curb n. 抑制  
✅ claim n. 声明；要求  
✅ dramatic adj. 突然的，巨大的  
✅ Federal Circuit  联邦巡回法院  
✅ pool v. 集中（金钱、资源等） n. 池塘，水池 （注意熟词僻义）  
✅ mutual-fund  共同基金

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>根据逗号和连词because断开句子。

- 【句1】主句：Curbs on business-method claims would be a dramatic about-face,
- 【句2】原因状语从句（其中包含了强调句）：because it was the Federal Circuit itself that introduced such patents with its 1998 decision in the so-called State Street Bank case, approving a patent on a way of pooling mutual-fund assets.

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2 简化从句：</span></strong>去掉非核心的介词短语和非谓语动词词组

- 【句2】原因状语从句（其中包含了强调句）： because it was the Federal Circuit itself that introduced such patents with its 1998 decision in the so-called State Street Bank case, approving a patent on a way of pooling mutual-fund assets.

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">强调句</span>**

It was… that…是强调句的基本结构，was表示强调过去，强调的内容是the Federal Circuit itself。

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">非谓语动词词组</span>**

非谓语动词doing的词组approving a patent on a way of pooling mutual-fund assets，是一个整体，表示主动，修饰整个句子作状语。
