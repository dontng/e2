## 原句

> Many Americans regard the jury system as a concrete expression of crucial democratic values, including the principles that all citizens who meet minimal qualifications of age and literacy are equally competent to serve on juries; that jurors should be selected randomly from a representative cross section of the community; that no citizen should be denied the right to serve on a jury on account of race, religion, sex, or national origin; that defendants are entitled to trial by their peers; and that verdicts should represent the conscience of the community and not just the letter of the law.

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过连接词that/who/that/that/that/and that断开句子。

- 【句1】主句：Many Americans regard the jury system as a concrete expression of crucial democratic values, including the principles
- 【句2+3】that同位语从句（内含who定语从句）： that all citizens ( who meet minimal qualifications of age and literacy) are equally competent to serve on juries;
- 【句4】that同位语从句： that jurors should be selected randomly from a representative cross section of the community;
- 【句5】that同位语从句： that no citizen should be denied the right to serve on a jury on account of race, religion, sex, or national origin;
- 【句6】that同位语从句： that defendants are entitled to trial by their peers;
- 【句7】that同位语从句： and that verdicts should represent the conscience of the community and not just the letter of the law.

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">平行结构</span>**

5个that同位语从句，通过分号和并列连词and连接，构成平行结构，解释说明前面的抽象名词principles，详细说明“五大原则的内容是什么”。

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">平行结构最易出细节题，平行并列的每个点都可以改编为一个选项，例如本句，就是阅读题的出题句。</span>

## 参考译文

许多美国人认为陪审团制度是至关重要的民主价值观的具体体现，其中包括以下原则：所有满足最低年龄和文化要求的公民都具备同等资格担任陪审员；陪审员应从社会各部门的典型代表中随机挑选；任何公民不得由于种族、宗教、性别和民族出身被剥夺担任陪审员的权利；被告人有权利接受由同等地位的人进行的审判；审判结果应该代表社会良知，而不仅仅是法律条文的字面意义。
