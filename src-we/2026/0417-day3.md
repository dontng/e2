## 原句

> The latest was a panel from the National Academy of Sciences, enlisted by the White House, to tell us that the Earth’s atmosphere is definitely warming and that the problem is largely man-made.

<div align="center">【2005年阅读 Text 2】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中的平行结构，分别是什么与什么平行并列？

## 生词

☑ panel /ˈpænl/ n. 专家组  
☑ enlist /ɪnˈlɪst/ v. 召集，招募  
☑ atmosphere /ˈætməsfɪə(r)/ n. 大气，大气层；气氛  
☑ definitely /ˈdefɪnətli/ adv. 毫无疑问地，明确地，确切地  
☑ man-made 人为造成的，人造的

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> was/is warming/is

（三个谓语动词，意味着三件事，应该断开为三段）

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>根据连词that/and/that，可以把句子断开为3段。

- 【句1】主句：The latest was a panel from the National Academy of Sciences, enlisted by the White House, to tell us... and...
- 【句2】宾语从句1：that the Earth’s atmosphere is definitely warming
- 【句3】宾语从句2：that the problem is largely man-made.

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">分裂结构</span>**

非谓语动词词组enlisted by the White House作插入语，通过两个逗号隔开，造成了主句的分裂。

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">平行结构</span>**

两个that宾语从句，通过并列连词and连接，构成平行并列结构。

to tell us <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">that</span> the Earth’s atmosphere is definitely warming <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">and</span> <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">that</span> the problem is largely man-made
