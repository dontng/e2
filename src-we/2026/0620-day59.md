## 原句

> But the researchers believe that outside directors have an easier time of avoiding a blow to their reputations if they leave a firm before bad news breaks, even if a review of history shows they were on the board at the time any wrongdoing occurred.

<div align="center">【2011年英二阅读 Text 1】</div>

**思考题:**

句中包含了几件事？断开为几段？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> believe / have / leave / breaks / shows / were / occurred

（7个谓语动词，意味着7件事，应该断开为7段）

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
非谓语动词avoiding不算数。</mark>

## 生词

✅ reputation /ˌrepjuˈteɪʃ(ə)n/ n. 名声，声誉  
✅ a blow to reputations 对声誉的打击  
✅ review /rɪˈvjuː/ n. 检查，审查；回顾  
✅ a review of history  历史记录  
✅ board /bɔːd/ n. 董事会；板子；甲板  
✅ be on the board 在董事会上；在板子上；在飞机/车/船上  
✅ wrongdoing /ˈrɒŋduːɪŋ/ n. 错事；坏事  
✅ occur /əˈkɜː(r)/ v. 发生

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过逗号和连词that/if/before/even if/at the time以及分析主谓来断开句子。

注意：
句首的But表示本句与上一句之间的关系，不用来断开本句。

- 【句1】主句：But the researchers believe
- 【句2】宾语从句：that outside directors have an easier time of avoiding a blow to their reputations
- 【句3】条件状语从句：if they leave a firm
- 【句4】时间状语从句：before bad news breaks,
- 【句5】让步状语从句：even if a review of history shows
- 【句6】省略了连词的宾语从句：they were on the board
- 【句7】时间状语从句：at the time any wrongdoing occurred.

## 参考译文

但研究者认为，如果外部董事在坏消息爆发前就已离开公司，那么他们会更容易避免声誉受损——即便历史记录显示“错误行为形成时，董事们尚在其职”（依然如此）。
