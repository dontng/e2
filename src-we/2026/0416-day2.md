## 原句

> However, whether such a sense of fairness evolved independently in capuchins and humans, or whether it stems from the common ancestor that the species had 35 million years ago, is, as yet, an unanswered question.

**思考题:**

主句的主语是哪部分？

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> evolved/stems/had/is

（四个谓语动词，意味着四件事，应该断开为四段）

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>根据标点逗号和连接词whether/or/whether/that，可以把句子断开为4段。

- 【句1】主句：However,..., or..., is, as yet, an unanswered question.
- 【句2】主语从句1：whether such a sense of fairness evolved independently in capuchins and humans
- 【句3】主语从句2：whether it stems from the common ancestor...
- 【句4】定语从句（修饰名词ancestor）：that the species had 35 million years ago

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">平行结构</span>**

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">也叫并列结构，就是多个词、词组或句子通过并列连词和逗号连接在一起。</span>

本句是两个whether主语从句通过并列连词or并列，构成平行并列结构。

1）whether such a sense of fairness evolved independently in capuchins and humans,

2）or whether it stems from the common ancestor that the species had 35 million years ago,

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">嵌套结构</span>**

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">大句子里面套小句子，层层嵌套，就是嵌套结构。</span>

句1主句中嵌套了句2和句3两个并列的whether主语从句，同时句3主语从句中还嵌套了句 4that定语从句。
