## 原句

> Davidson’s article is one of a number of pieces that have recently appeared making the point that the reason we have such stubbornly high unemployment and declining middle-class incomes today is largely because of the big drop in demand because of the Great Recession, but it is also because of the advances in both globalization and the information technology revolution, which are more rapidly than ever replacing labor with machines or foreign workers.

<div align="center">【2013年英二阅读 Text1】</div>

**思考题:**

本句一共表达了几件事？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> is / have appeared / have / is / is / are

（6个谓语动词，意味着6件事，应该断开为6段）

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
非谓语动词making/replacing不算数。</mark>

## 生词

✅ make the point 发表观点  
✅ stubbornly /ˈstʌbənli/ adv. 倔强地；顽固地  
✅ unemployment /ˌʌnɪmˈplɔɪmənt/ n. 失业，失业率  
✅ decline /dɪˈklaɪn/ v. 下降，减少，衰退；谢绝  
declining /dɪˈklaɪnɪŋ/ adj. 下降的，越来越少的  
✅ the Great Recession 大衰退; 大萧条  
✅ recession /rɪˈseʃ(ə)n/ n. 经济衰退，不景气；后退，撤回  
✅ advance /ədˈvɑːns/ n. 前进，发展；增长，提高  
✅ globalization /ˌɡləʊbəlaɪˈzeɪʃn/ n. 全球化，全球性  
✅ information technology revolution 信息技术革命  
✅ more… than ever 比以往任何时候都更……  
✅ replace A with B 用B来代替A

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过标点，连词和分析主谓来断开句子。

- 【句1】Davidson’s article is one of a number of pieces 句2 making the point 句3
- 【句2】that have recently appeared
- 【句3】that the reason 句4 is largely because of the big drop in demand because of the Great Recession,
- 【句4】we have such stubbornly high unemployment and declining middle-class incomes today
- 【句5】but it is also because of the advances in both globalization and the information technology revolution,
- 【句6】which are more rapidly than ever replacing labor with machines or foreign workers.

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">理清句子之间的关系</span>**

理清句子之间的关系，一定要搞清楚句子的类型和修饰补充什么，这样能更好的看懂句意。

【句1】主句：Davidson’s article is one of a number of pieces 句2 making the point 句3

【句2】定语从句：that have recently appeared （修饰前面的名词pieces）

【句3】同位语从句：that the reason 句4 is largely because of the big drop in demand because of the Great Recession, （解释说明前面的抽象名词point）

【句4】省略了关系词的定语从句：we have such stubbornly high unemployment and declining middle-class incomes today （修饰前面的名词reason）

【句5】并列句：but it is also because of the advances in both globalization and the information technology revolution, （与前面的句3同位语从句并列，一起说明两个原因分别是什么）

【句6】非限定性定语从句：which are more rapidly than ever replacing labor with machines or foreign workers. （补充说明前面的名词词组the advances in both globalization and the information technology revolution）

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">嵌套结构</span>**

句1主句中嵌套了句2定语从句和句3同位语从句，句3同位语从句中又嵌套了句4定语从句。

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">分析长难句与谓语动词</span>**

如果句子非常长，大家对于断开没有把握的情况下，可以先数一下句子中的谓语动词。有几个谓语动词就说明句子中讲了几件事，就可以将句子相应地断成几段。  
此外，还可以通过谓语动词的位置检验句子是否断开成功。

## 参考译文

戴维森的文章是最近出现的一系列文章中的一篇，这些文章提出这样一种观点：当前我们的失业率居高不下，中产阶级收入下降，很大程度上是因为大萧条造成的需求大幅下降，但它（这种情况）也是因为全球化和信息技术革命的进步，这种进步以前所未有的速度使得机器或外来雇工代替了（本土）劳动力。
