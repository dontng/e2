## 原句

> A century ago, Freud formulated his revolutionary theory that dreams were the disguised shadows of our unconscious desires and fears; by the late 1970s, neurologists had switched to thinking of them as just “mental noise”—the random byproducts of the neural-repair work that goes on during sleep.

<div align="center">【2005年阅读 Text 3】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中包含了几方不同的观点？分别是谁发出的观点？

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动：</span></strong> formulated/were/had switched/goes

（四个谓语动词，意味着四件事，应该断开为四段）

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>根据分号和连接词that/that，可以把句子断开为4段。

- 【句1】主句1：A century ago, Freud formulated his revolutionary theory
- 【句2】同位语从句（解释说明抽象名词theory）：that dreams were the disguised shadows of our unconscious desires and fears
- 【句3】主句2：by the late 1970s, neurologists had switched to thinking of them as just “mental noise”—the random byproducts of the neural-repair work
- 【句4】定语从句（修饰名词work）：that goes on during sleep

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">本句通过分号，并列了两方不同的观点</span>**：分别是来自于弗洛伊德和神经病学家们的观点。

1）A century ago, <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">Freud</span> formulated his revolutionary theory that dreams were the disguised shadows of our unconscious desires and fears;

2）by the late 1970s, <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">neurologists</span> had switched to thinking of them as just “mental noise”—the random byproducts of the neural-repair work that goes on during sleep.

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">考研阅读的观点题</span>**

多方观点汇集处，一定会出题！

注意审题，看看题目中考查的是哪一方的观点，其他方的观点经常会作为混淆项出现。
