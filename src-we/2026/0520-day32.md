## 原句

> In fact, the more new things we try—the more we step outside our comfort zone—the more inherently creative we become, both in the workplace and in our personal lives.

<div align="center">【2009年阅读 Text 1】</div>

**思考题:**

句中的几个比较级表达什么意思？

## 生词

☑ step outside our comfort zone 走出我们的舒适区  
☑ inherently /ɪnˈherəntli/ adv. 内在地，与生俱来地，固有地  
inherent /ɪnˈherənt/ adj. 内在的，与生俱来的，固有的  
☑ creative /kriˈeɪtɪv/ adj. 创造性的，创作的

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> try/step/become

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>根据破折号断开句子。

- 【句1】In fact, the more new things we try
- 【句2】—the more we step outside our comfort zone—
- 【句3】the more inherently creative we become, both in the workplace and in our personal lives.

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">比较级的重要句型</span>**

“the + 比较级……，the + 比较级……”表示“越……越……”，指一方随着另一方的变化而变化。在构成这一句型时会把原本两个包含比较级的句子调整语序，把比较级提前，前面再加上the，例如：

1) we try more new things变为the more new things we try

2) we step outside our comfort zone more变为the more we step outside our comfort zone

3) we become more inherently creative变为the more inherently creative we become

## 参考译文

事实上，我们尝试越多新事物——走出自己的舒适区越远——我们就会变得越有内在的创造力，在工作和我们的个人生活中都是如此。
