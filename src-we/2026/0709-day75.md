## 原句

> Priestly explains how the deep blue color of the assistant’s sweater descended over the years from fashion shows to department stores and to the bargain bin in which the poor girl doubtless found her garment.

<div align="center">【2013年英一阅读 Text1】</div>

**思考题:**

句中共包含几件事？第2件事的核心内容是什么？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> explains / descended / found

（3个谓语动词，意味着3件事，应该断开为3段）

## 生词

✅ descend /dɪˈsend/ v. 下降；下去  
✅ department store 百货商店  
✅ bargain bin 打折区  
✅ doubtless /ˈdaʊtləs/ adv. 毫无疑问地；不用怀疑地  
✅ garment /ˈɡɑːmənt/ n. （一件）衣服

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过连词how/(in) which断开句子。

- 【句1】主句：Priestly explains
- 【句2】宾语从句：how the deep blue color of the assistant’s sweater descended over the years from fashion shows to department stores and to the bargain bin
- 【句3】介词提前的定语从句：in which the poor girl doubtless found her garment.

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2 简化：</span></strong>去扩展，找核心。去掉非核心的介词短语。

- 【句2】宾语从句： how the deep blue color of the assistant’s sweater descended over the years from fashion shows to department stores and to the bargain bin

简化后how从句的核心：
how the deep blue color… descended…

## 参考译文

Priestly解释了助手身上毛衫的深蓝色如何在几年间从时装展屈尊至百货店，再沦落到打折区——这个可怜的女孩儿身上的衣衫无疑淘自最后一站。
