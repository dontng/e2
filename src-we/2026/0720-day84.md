## 原句

> Looking beyond the culture war logic of right or wrong means opening up the middle ground and understanding that managing immigration today requires multiple paths and multiple outcomes, including some that are not easy to accomplish legally in the existing system.

<div align="center">【2013年英二阅读 Text2】</div>

**思考题:**

主句主语是什么？两个that从句分别是什么从句？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> means / requires / are not

（3个谓语动词，意味着3件事，应该断开为3段）

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
非谓语动词Looking/opening/understanding/managing/to accomplish/existing不算数。</mark>

## 生词

✅ culture war 文化战争  
✅ middle ground 中间地带，中间立场  
✅ immigration /ˌɪmɪˈɡreɪʃ(ə)n/ n. 移民  
✅ multiple /ˈmʌltɪp(ə)l/ adj. 多样的；许多的；多重的  
✅ outcome /ˈaʊtkʌm/ n. 结果  
✅ accomplish /əˈkʌmplɪʃ/ v. 完成，实现；达到  
✅ the existing system 现有制度

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过连词that/that来断开句子。

- 【句1】主句：Looking beyond the culture war logic of right or wrong means opening up the middle ground and understanding
- 【句2】宾语从句：that managing immigration today requires multiple paths and multiple outcomes, including some
- 【句3】定语从句：that are not easy to accomplish legally in the existing system.

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">非谓语动词词组作主语和宾语</span>**

主语：Looking beyond the culture war logic of right or wrong

宾语：opening up the middle ground和understanding...，两个doing并列（即平行结构）  
**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">判断从句</span>**  
本句中有两个that从句

第一个that引导宾语从句，跟在及物动词understanding后。

第二个that引导定语从句，修饰代词some（代词相当于名词用）。

## 参考译文

超越这种非对即错文化战争的逻辑，意味着要打开中间地带，并知晓当今的移民管理需要多种途径及多样结果，包括一些现有制度下通过法律难以达成的途径和结果。
