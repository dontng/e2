## 原句

> Today, surrounded by promises of easy happiness, we need art to tell us, as religion once did, Memento mori: remember that you will die, that everything ends, and that happiness comes not in denying this but in living with it.

<div align="center">【2006年阅读 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

本句中出现了几处平行结构？分别是什么？

## 生词

☑ surround /səˈraʊnd/ v. 围绕，环绕；包围  
☑ easy happiness 唾手可得的快乐  
☑ religion /rɪˈlɪdʒən/ n. 宗教，教派；宗教信仰  
☑ memento mori /me'mentəu'məurai/ 死亡警告；使人想到死亡的物体（或象征）  
☑ live with… 接受，忍受

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> need/did/remember/will die/ends/comes

（非谓语动词surrounded/ to tell/ denying/ living不算）

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>根据标点逗号/冒号和连词as/that/that/and/that，可以把句子断开为6段。

- 【句1】主句：Today, surrounded by promises of easy happiness, we need art to tell us,..., Memento mori:...
- 【句2】方式状语从句：as religion once did
- 【句3】祈使句：remember...,..., and...
- 【句4】宾语从句：that you will die
- 【句5】宾语从句：that everything ends
- 【句6】宾语从句：(and) that happiness comes not in denying this but in living with it

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">分裂结构</span>**

as religion once did状语从句，通过两个逗号隔开，插入到句子中，造成了句子的分裂。

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">平行结构</span>**

1） 两个介词短语in doing，通过not… but…连接，构成平行（并列）结构。

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">not</span> in denying this <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">but</span> in living with it

2） 三个that宾语从句，通过逗号和and连接，构成平行（并列）结构。

that you will die<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">,</span> that everything ends<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">, and</span> that happiness comes not in denying this but in living with it

## 参考译文

如今，被唾手可得的快乐的承诺围绕着，我们需要艺术像宗教曾经做过的那样来告诉我们“死亡警示”：记得你终将死去，一切将会终结，快乐不在于否定这一点，而在于接受它。
