## 原句

> problem threatens to earn them—especially in America—the sort of nasty headlines that inevitably lead to heads rolling in the executive suite: data insecurity.

本句如何断开？

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> have sorted/improved/threatens/lead

注意：
四个谓语动词，意味着四件事，本来应该断开为四段；但是and并列句后面的句子不完整则不能断开，因此第一个和第二个谓语动词之间不断开。

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>据逗号和连词as/that断开句子

- 【句1】as时间状语从句：Just as bosses and boards have finally sorted out their worst accounting and compliance troubles, and improved their feeble corporation governance,
- 【句2】主句：a new problem threatens to earn them—especially in America—the sort of nasty headlines
- 【句3】that定语从句：that inevitably lead to heads rolling in the executive suite: data insecurity.

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">平行结构</span>**

1） bosses <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">and</span> boards

2） accounting <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">and</span> compliance

3） sorted out their worst accounting and compliance troubles, <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">and</span> improved their feeble corporation governance

## 参考译文

就在老板和董事会终于解决了最棘手的财务和合规问题，并改善了薄弱的公司管理的时候，一个新的问题可能会为他们招惹来——特别是在美国——那种会不可避免地导致管理层受到严惩的负面头条，即数据不安全。
