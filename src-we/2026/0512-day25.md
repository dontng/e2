## 原句

> “Their capacity for tolerating stress may even be greater than men’s,” she observes, “it’s just that they’re dealing with so many more things that they become worn out from it more visibly and sooner.”

<div align="center">【2008年阅读 Text 1】</div>

**思考题:**

句中的两个that从句分别是什么从句？

## 生词

☑ capacity /kəˈpæsəti/ n. 能力；容量  
☑ tolerate stress 忍受压力，承受压力  
☑ tolerate /ˈtɒləreɪt/ v. 忍受；容忍；包容  
☑ observe /əbˈzɜːv/ v. 观察，注意到；说，评论  
☑ deal with… 处理；涉及  
☑ become worn out 变得筋疲力尽  
☑ visibly /ˈvɪzəbli/ adv. 明显地，显然；看得见地

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> may be/observes/’s(is)/’re(are) dealing/become

（五个谓语动词，意味着五件事，应该断开为五段）

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>根据标点引号/逗号和连接词that/that，可以把句子断开为5段。

- 【句1】直接引语1：“Their capacity for tolerating stress may even be greater than men’s”
- 【句2】主句：“...,” she observes, “...”
- 【句3】直接引语2中的主句：“it’s just...
- 【句4】直接引语2中的表语从句：that they’re dealing with so many more things
- 【句5】直接引语2中的结果状语从句：(so)… that they become worn out from it more visibly and sooner.”

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">判断从句的类型</span>**

静姐的口诀就是三个字——“往前看”，看从句前挨着什么。

it <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">’s</span> just <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">that</span> they’re dealing with so many more things  
前面的’s(is)是系动词，则判断出后面的that从句是表语从句。

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">so</span> many more things <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">that</span> they become worn out from it more visibly and sooner.  
前面的so…that…搭配，则判断出后面的that从句是结果状语从句。
