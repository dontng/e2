## 原句

> Although the Supreme Court of the United States had prohibited intentional racial discrimination in jury selection as early as the 1880 case of Strauder v. West Virginia, the practice of selecting so-called elite or blue-ribbon juries provided a convenient way around this and other antidiscrimination laws.

<div align="center">【2010年英二阅读 Text 4】</div>

**思考题:**

简化后句子的核心内容是什么？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> had prohibited/provided

（2个谓语动词，意味着2件事，应该断开为2段）

## 生词

✅ the Supreme Court 最高法院  
✅ prohibit /prəˈhɪbɪt/ v. 禁止；阻止  
✅ intentional /ɪnˈtenʃən(ə)l/ adj. 故意的；有意的 intention n. 打算，计划；意图  
✅ discrimination /dɪˌskrɪmɪˈneɪʃn/ n. 歧视；区别对待  
✅ racial discrimination 种族歧视  
✅ antidiscrimination n. 反歧视  
✅ practice /ˈpræktɪs/ n. 惯例，做法；实践；练习

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过逗号和连词Although断开句子。

- 【句1】让步状语从句： Although the Supreme Court of the United States had prohibited intentional racial discrimination in jury selection as early as the 1880 case of Strauder v. West Virginia,
- 【句2】主句：the practice of selecting so-called elite or blue-ribbon juries provided a convenient way around this and other antidiscrimination laws.

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2 简化：</span></strong>去扩展找核心，去掉非核心的介词短语/形容词。

- 【句1】让步状语从句： Although the Supreme Court of the United States had prohibited intentional racial discrimination in jury selection as early as the 1880 case of Strauder v. West Virginia,
- 【句2】主句： the practice of selecting so-called elite or blue-ribbon juries provided a convenient way around this and other antidiscrimination laws.

简化后句子的核心内容：
Although the Supreme Court… had prohibited… discrimination…, the practice… provided a… way….

注意：
其实冠词the/a这些都可以简化掉，但是没必要，因为简化的目的是为了抓住句子的核心看懂，而不是为了简化到最精简。

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">让步状语从句</span>**

Although表示“尽管”，引出让步状语从句，表示“明让步，暗转折”，翻译从句和主句时，可以译成“尽管……，（但是）……”。

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">同级比较</span>**

as early as the 1880 case，直译为“和1880年的案件一样早”，意译调整为“早在1880年的案件”。

## 参考译文

尽管美国最高法院早在1880年Strauder诉西弗吉尼亚州一案中就已经禁止陪审团遴选中的蓄意种族歧视，但挑选所谓的精英陪审员或蓝丝带陪审员的做法，却为绕开这一反歧视及其它反歧视法案提供了一条捷径。
