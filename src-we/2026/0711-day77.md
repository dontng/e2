## 原句

> As so often, the past holds the key to the future: we have now identified enough of the long-term patterns shaping the history of the planet, and our species, to make evidence-based forecasts about the situations in which our descendants will find themselves.

<div align="center">【2013年英一阅读 Text3】</div>

**思考题:**

两个非谓语动词词组shaping...和to make...分别修饰什么？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> holds / have identified / will find

（3个谓语动词，意味着3件事，应该断开为3段）

<mark style="background-color: rgb(238, 245, 13);">注意：
非谓语动词shaping/to make不算数。</mark>

## 生词

✅ identify /aɪˈdentɪfaɪ/ v. 识别出，辨认出  
✅ long-term patterns 长期的模式  
✅ make evidence-based forecasts 做出有根据的预测  
✅ descendant /dɪˈsendənt/ n. 子孙，后裔

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过冒号和连词(in) which断开句子。

- 【句1】主句1：As so often, the past holds the key to the future:
- 【句2】主句2：we have now identified enough of the long-term patterns shaping the history of the planet, and our species, to make evidence-based forecasts about the situations
- 【句3】介词提前的定语从句：in which our descendants will find themselves.

## 语法重点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">判断非谓语动词所修饰的对象</span>**

一般非谓语动词的修饰原则都是“就近修饰”，所以shaping the history of the planet修饰前面的名词patterns。

通常to do前面有逗号，则修饰整个句子，所以to make evidence-based forecasts about the situations修饰前面整个句子。

## 参考译文

正如通常情况那样，过去掌握着通向未来的钥匙：我们现在已经确定了足够多的地球以及人类历史何以形成的长期模式，使我们能够对子孙后代的生存环境做出有根据的预测。
