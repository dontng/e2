## 真题

**【段 2 】**

① Death is normal; we are genetically programmed to disintegrate and perish, even under ideal conditions. ② We all understand that at some level, yet as medical consumers we treat death as a problem to be solved. ③ Shielded by third-party payers from the cost of our care, we demand everything that can possibly be done for us, even if it’s useless. ④ The most obvious example is late-stage cancer care. ⑤ Physicians —frustrated by their inability to cure the disease and fearing loss of hope in the patient —too often offer aggressive treatment far beyond what is scientifically justified.

## 解题方法

**写作目的题考查的是文章中的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">举例、引用或比喻等被使用的目的</span>是为了表达什么。**

**写作目的题的常见提问方式：**

***The example of ... is used to show ________.**

***The author uses the example of ... to show that ________.**

***... is mentioned in Paragraph XXX to present ________.**

***... is used to illustrate _______.**

***The quotation in Paragraph XXX explains that ________.**

***... is quoted to indicate _______**

***By citing the book The Tipping Point, the author intends to _______.**

**写作目的题的解题方法：<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">先找内容，再前后找目的</span>**

## 易错点

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">内容≠目的，这是写作目的题的解题关键。</span>

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">出题老师会故意把举例、引用、比喻的内容做成选项</span>，故意误导大家，而<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">同学们最容易错把“内容”当成“目的”，这是最易错的点</span>。例如本题的B和C选项，虽然选项内容在原文中“有”且“全”（全部都能与原文对应），但是它是属于例子内容的一部分，不是题干所提问的目的，不匹配题干问题，因此B和C选项错误。**
