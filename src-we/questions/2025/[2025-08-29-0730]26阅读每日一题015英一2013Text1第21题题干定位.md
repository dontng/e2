## 真题

**【段 1 】**

① In the 2006 film version of The Devil Wears Prada, Miranda Priestly, played by Meryl Streep, scolds her unattractive assistant for imagining that high fashion doesn’t affect her. ② Priestly explains how the deep blue color of the assistant’s sweater descended over the years from fashion shows to department stores and to the bargain bin in which the poor girl doubtless found her garment.

<div align="center">【每日一题 015 · 2013 英语一 Text 1 第 21 题 · 题干定位】</div>

**题目:**

21. Priestly criticizes her assistant for her _______

[A] lack of imagination.

[B] poor bargaining skill.

[C] obsession with high fashion.

[D] insensitivity to fashion.


## 生词

✅ personalised medicine 个性化医疗  
✅ version n. 版本  
✅ played by... 由 ……饰演  
✅ scold sb. for sth. 因某事斥责、责骂某人  
✅ descend v. 落到，堕落；下降，下去  
✅ over the years 这些年来，多年以来  
✅ the bargain bin 打折区  
✅ bargain n. 特价商品  
✅ doubtless adv. 无疑地，肯定地  
✅ adj. 无疑的，肯定的  
✅ garment n. 衣服，服装，外观

## 解题方法

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">题干定位分为两步：初步定位</span>**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">→精准定位</span>**

**1.初步定位：定位到某一两段，不能直接找到答案，还需要对比选项和原文，<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">题干个别关键词对应原文</span>**

**2.精准定位：定位到某一句，直接找到答案，<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">题干绝大部分单词逐一对应原文</span>**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">注意同义改写：题干和正确选项都可能被同义改写。</span>题干中Priestly criticizes her assistant for 是对原文Priestly... scolds her... assistant for的同义改写。 D选项insensitivity to fashion（对时尚不敏感）是对原文imagining that high fashion doesn’t affect her（认为高级时尚没有影响到她）的同义改写。**
