## 真题

**【段 2】**

①Mr. Koziatek is part of something pioneering. ②He is a teacher at a New Hampshire high school where learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical. ③When did it become accepted wisdom that students should be able to name the 13th president of the United States but be utterly overwhelmed by a broken bike chain?

<div align="center">【每日一题 044 · 2018 英语二 Text 1 第 21 题 · 写作目的题】</div>

**题目:**

21. A broken bike chain is mentioned to show students’lack of.

[A] practical ability

[B] academic training

[C] pioneering spirit

[D] mechanical memorization


## 生词

✅ pioneering adj. 开创性的，探索性的  
✅ mechanical memorization 机械记忆  
✅ practical adj. 实践的，实用的  
✅ accepted wisdom 公认的看法  
✅ utterly adv. 完全地，十足地，彻底地  
✅ overwhelm v. 使不知所措；压倒, 制服；覆盖, 淹没

## 解题方法

**写作目的题考查的是文章中的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">举例、引用或比喻等被使用的目的</span>是为了表达什么。**

**写作目的题的常见提问方式：**

***The example of ... is used to show ________.**

***... is used to illustrate _______.**

***The quotation in Paragraph XXX explains that ________.**

***... is quoted to indicate _______**

***The “empty bottles ” metaphor illustrates that people feel a pressure to.**

***The ants analogy is used to illustrate.**

**写作目的题的解题方法：<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">通常，先找内容，再前后找目的</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">这道题考查的很特别：两个例子和两个目的分别对应</span>**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">例子的对应：</span>句②指出， “学习并不关乎书本、考试和机械记忆，而（在于）实践”。而句③中的两个例子正好对应句②的这两个方面，其中“说出美国第13任总统的名字”对应“书本、考试和机械记忆”，而a broken bike chain（一根断了的自行车链条）对应practical（实践的） 。<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">这道题的特别之处在于其中举出了两个例子，分别对应两个目的，因此在解题的时候要注意例子的对应。</span>**
