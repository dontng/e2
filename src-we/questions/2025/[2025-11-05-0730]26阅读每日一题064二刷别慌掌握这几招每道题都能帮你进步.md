**【段 2】**

① However, Cortina said, early exposure is beneficial. ② When younger kids learn computer science, they learn that it’s not just a confusing, endless string of letters and numbers —but a tool to build apps, or create artwork, or test hypotheses. ③ It’s not as hard for them to transform their thought processes as it is for older students. ④ Breaking down problems into bitesized chunks and using code to solve them becomes normal. ⑤ Giving more children this training could increase the number of people interested in the field and help fill the jobs gap, Cortina said.

**题目:**

21. Cortina holds that early exposure to computer science makes it easier to.

[A] complete future job training

[B] remodel the way of thinking

[C] formulate logical hypotheses

[D] perfect artwork production


## 生词

✅ exposure n. 接触，体验(新事物)；暴露, 显露  
✅ expose v. 揭露，揭发; 使曝光; 显露  
✅ beneficial adj. 有益的，有利的  
✅ benefit v. 受益，得到好处 n. 利益，好处; 救济  
✅ confusing adj. 令人困惑的，难懂的；混乱的  
✅ confuse v. 使困惑，使混乱  
✅ a string of... 一连串的、一系列的...  
✅ hypothesis n. 假设，假说(复数形式 hypotheses)  
✅ transform v. (使)转换；(使)改变；(使)变形  
✅ break down 拆分，分解  
✅ bite-sized chunk 很小的块  
✅ jobs gap 职位空缺

## 解题方法

**观点题就是考查某人发出的观点内容。**

**识别观点题**

**Sb. think/believe/hold/suggest/argue that…**

**According to sb.,…**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">观点题的解题方法</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.人：作者不用找，其他人要明确找到</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2.观点细节：细节比对原文， “灵魂三问法”</span>**

## 出题点

**精准定位：当题干中绝大部分单词可以逐一对应原文时，就可以把定位范围缩小，定位到某一句，直接找到答案，例如本题。但<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">这道题的难点在于要找到题干中easier对应原文中的not as hard，找到这个同义改写，才能实现精准定位找到答案</span>。**

**同义改写：<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">题干和正确选项都是对原文的同义改写</span>，例如本题，这是考研阅读中最常使用的出题方式，来提高解题的难度。**
