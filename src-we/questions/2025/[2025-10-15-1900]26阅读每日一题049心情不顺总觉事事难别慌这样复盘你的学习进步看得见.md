## 真题

**【段 3】**

①The use of this little-known practice has accelerated in recent years, as colleges continue to do their utmost to keep students in school (and paying tuition) and improve their graduation rates. ②When this practice first started decades ago, it was usually limited to freshmen, to give them a second chance to take a class in their first year if they struggled in their transition to college-level courses. ③But now most colleges, save for many selective campuses, allow all undergraduates, and even graduate students, to get their low grades forgiven.

<div align="center">【每日一题 049 · 2019 英语一 Text 2 第 27 题 · 细节题】</div>

**题目:**

27. What was the original purpose of grade forgiveness?

[A] To maintain colleges’graduation rates.

[B] To increase universities’income from tuition.

[C] To prepare graduates for a challenging future.

[D] To help freshmen adapt to college learning.


## 生词

✅ practice n. 惯例；实践；练习  
✅ accelerate v. 加快，加速  
✅ acceleration n. 加速，促进  
✅ do one's utmost 尽某人全力，竭力  
✅ tuition n. 学费  
✅ graduation rate 毕业率  
✅ freshman n. 新生, 大学一年级学生；新手  
✅ struggle v. 艰难地行进，吃力地行进；挣扎，努力  
✅ n. 斗争；奋斗；努力  
✅ transition n. 过渡；转变，变迁  
✅ selective adj. 严格筛选的，精心选择的  
✅ undergraduate n. 大学本科生，大学肄业生（没毕业的学生）  
✅ graduate n. 研究生；大学毕业生

## 解题方法

**细节题就是考查对于文章细节内容的理解**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节题的解题方法：细节比对原文！！！</span>**

**判断细节是否正确用<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">“灵魂三问法”</span>：**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2.全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3.匹配否？</span>**

**（适用于细节题/推断题/观点题）**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">注意审题干，其中的形容词或副词有时是解题的关键。</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">注意现在和过去的对比，易出题。</span>**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">注意审题干：本题问的是original purpose （最初的目的），不是“所有的目的</span>”，也不是“现在的目的”，所以找到“original”（最初的）是解题的关键。**

**注意时间：注意原文中的时间提示词，句**

**①说的是in recent years，句③说的是now，指的是最近和现在，不是最初；而句②说的是first started decades ago （数十年前最初开始时），正好对应题干所问的 original purpose（最初的目的）。**
