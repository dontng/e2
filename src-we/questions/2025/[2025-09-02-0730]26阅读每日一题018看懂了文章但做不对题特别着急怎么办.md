## 真题

**【段 1 】**

① Some growth stems from a commitment by governments and farsighted businesses to fund cleaner energy sources. ② But increasingly the story is about the plummeting prices of renewables, especially wind and solar. ③ The cost of solar panels has dropped by 80 percent and the cost of wind turbines by close to one-third in the past eight years.

<div align="center">【每日一题 018 · 2018 英语二 Text 2 第 26 题 · 语义题】</div>

**题目:**

26. The word “ plummeting ” (Para. 2) is closest in meaning to _______.

[A] rising

[B] falling

[C] changing

[D] stabilizing


## 生词

✅ stem from... 出自 ……，来源于......  
✅ commitment n. 承诺  
✅ farsighted adj. 有远见的  
✅ cleaner energy sources 更清洁能源  
✅ solar panels 太阳能板, 太阳能电池板  
✅ wind turbines 风力涡轮机

## 解题方法

**语义题常见的提问形式：**

***The word/phrase/expression/sentence “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) most probably means/refers to/denotes/stands for/suggests _______**

***The word “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) is closest in meaning to _______**

***What does the author mean by “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) ?**

***By “…” (Line…, Paragraph…), the author means/implies ________.**

**判断语义题：一可以根据题干中的引号，二可以根据原文中的下划线。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">语义题考查的是文章中的单词、词组或者句子的含义：</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">生词、熟词僻意、指代。</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">语义题的解题关键——上下文！</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">根据上下文中的同义词/近义词、逻辑对应/逻辑相反、举例等来判断。</span>**

## 出题点

**本题是<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">根据上下文中的同义词</span>来猜测词义的。**

**句②中plummeting修饰的是prices of renewables, espe cially wind and solar（可再生能源价格，尤其是风能和太阳能）。句③提到， “太阳能电池板的价格下降了80% ”， “风力涡轮机的价格下降了近三分之一”，由此可知，句③是句②的具体例证，句②中的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">plummeting</span>和句③的谓语动词<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">dropped</span> （下降）相对应，含义应一致，因此B选项<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">falling</span>正确。**
