## 真题

**【段 5】**

①Knowing the results of Epley’s study, it makes sense that many people hate photographs of themselves viscerally—on one level, they don’t even recognise the person in the picture as themselves. ②Facebook, therefore, is a self-enhancer’s paradise, where people can share only the most flattering photos, the cream of their wit, style, beauty, intellect and lifestyles. ③It’s not that people’s profiles are dishonest, says Catalina Toma of Wisconsin-Madison University, “but they portray an idealised version of themselves.”

<div align="center">【每日一题 048 · 2014 英语二 Text 2 第 30 题 · 推断题】</div>

**题目:**

30. It can be inferred that Facebook is a self-enhancer’s paradise because people can.

[A] present their dishonest profiles

[B] define their traditional lifestyles

[C] share their intellectual pursuits

[D] withhold their unflattering sides


## 生词

✅ make sense 有意义，讲得通  
✅ on one level 在某种程度上  
✅ recognize... as... 认出……是......  
✅ the cream of... ……的精华部分  
✅ wit n. 风趣；才智，智慧  
✅ intellect n. 智力，理解力，非凡的才智  
✅ not... but... 不是......而是......  
✅ profile n. 印象，形象；外形，轮廓  
✅ portray v. 描绘，画像，描述  
✅ idealise/idealize v. 把……理想化  
✅ ideal n. 理想；典范 adj. 理想的，完美的；想象的

## 解题方法

**推断题的判断： be inferred/learned/indicated**

**不用纠结于区分细节题和推断题，因为<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题和细节题的解题方法一样</span>，都是考查对于文章细节内容的理解。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题千万不要推断！！！</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节比对原文，运用“灵魂三问法”：</span>**

**1)有否？**

**2)全否？**

**3)匹配否？**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题的正确答案往往是对原文信息换种方式表达，比如同义改写、正话反说、高度概括。</span>**

## 出题点

**正话反说：原文中说的是share only the most flattering photos（只分享最漂亮的照片），正话反说一下，这句话就可以理解为withhold their unflattering sides（隐瞒他们不讨喜的方面），因此D选项正确。**
