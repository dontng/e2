## 真题

**【段 4】**

①Viewed in this light, guilt is an opportunity. ②Work by Tina Malti, a psychology professor at the University of Toronto, suggests that guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency. ③In a number of studies, Malti and others have shown that guilt and sympathy may represent different pathways to cooperation and sharing. ④Some kids who are low in sympathy may make up for that shortfall by experiencing more guilt, which can rein in their nastier impulses. ⑤And vice versa: High sympathy can substitute for low guilt.

<div align="center">【每日一题 052 · 2019 英语二 Text 1 第 24 题 · 观点题】</div>

**题目:**

24. Malti and others have shown that cooperation and sharing.

[A] may be the outcome of impulsive acts

[B] may help correct emotional deficiencies

[C] can bring about emotional satisfaction

[D] can result from either sympathy or guilt

## 解题方法

**观点题就是考查某人发出的观点内容。**

**识别观点题**

**Sb. think/believe/hold/suggest/argue that…**

**According to sb.,…**

**观点题的解题方法**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.人：作者不用找，其他人要明确找到</span>**

**2.观点细节：细节比对原文， “灵魂三问法”**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">题干的精准定位，直接找到答案。</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">因果关系是非常重要的出题点，要注意不同的词组，避免因果混淆。</span>**

## 出题点

**在观点题中考查内容细节时，也经常会考查到因果关系。<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">因果关系的关键是分辨出不同的词或词组是后接原因还是后接结果的，厘清是前因后果还是前果后因。</span>如下两个词组虽然字面不一样，但是因果关系的意思一样。**

**原文： A represent different pathways to B. “A代表不同的路通向B”，即“A导致B”，A是原因，B是结果。**

**选项：B result from A. “B结果源自A”，即B是结果，A是原因。**
