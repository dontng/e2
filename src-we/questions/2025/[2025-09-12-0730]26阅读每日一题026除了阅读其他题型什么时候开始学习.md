## 真题

**【段 2 】**

① Snap decisions can be important defense mechanisms; if we are judging whether someone is dangerous, our brains and bodies are hard-wired to react very quickly, within milliseconds.

**【段 3 】**

① But snap decisions in reaction to rapid stimuli aren’t exclusive to the interpersonal realm. ② Psychologists at the University of Toronto found that viewing a fast-food logo for just a few milliseconds primes us to read 20 percent faster, even though reading has little to do with eating. ③ We unconsciously associate fast food with speed and impatience and carry those impulses into whatever else we’re doing. ④ Subjects exposed to fast-food flashes also tend to think a musical piece lasts too long.

<div align="center">【每日一题 026 · 2013 英语二 Text 3 第 32 题 · 细节题】</div>

**题目:**

32. Our reaction to a fast-food logo shows that snap decisions _______.

[A] can be associative

[B] are not unconscious

[C] can be dangerous

[D] are not impulsive


## 生词

✅ snap decision 快速决策，仓促决定  
✅ important defense mechanism 重要防御机制  
✅ be hard-wired to do sth. 天生会做某事  
✅ millisecond n. 毫秒  
✅ in reaction to... 作为对……的反应，应对……  
✅ stimuli n. 刺激，刺激物；促进因素（ stimulus 的复数）  
✅ exclusive adj. 专有的, 独有的, 排他的  
✅ realm n. 领域，范围  
✅ prime sb. to do sth. 使某人准备好做某事、应付某事  
✅ prime v. 使准备好  
✅ have little to do with... 与...... 关系不大  
✅ unconsciously adv. 无意识地，不知不觉地；失去知觉地  
✅ unconscious adj. 无意识的，不知不觉的；失去知觉的  
✅ associate... with... 将...... 和...... 联系起来  
✅ associate v. 联系；联想  
✅ associative adj. 关联的；联想的  
✅ impatience n. 不耐烦, 没有耐心, 急躁、  
✅ impulse n. 冲动，心血来潮；推动力  
✅ impulsive adj. 冲动的  
✅ subject n. 被试者; 主题; 学科, 科目、  
✅ exposed to … 被暴露到，接触到……

## 解题方法

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节比对原文！</span>所有涉及到考查细节的题目，都要<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">拿着选项仔细地去比对原文</span>，逐词比对，因为考研阅读在考查细节时会考得非常细。绝对不要凭借脑子里的印象去想，非常易错。**

**判断细节是否正确的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">“灵魂三问法”</span>：**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1)有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2)全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3)匹配否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">（如上适用于细节题/推断题/观点题）</span>**

**同义改写是考研阅读最经典的出题套路，尤其注意换词性的同义改写。**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">同义改写</span>： A选项的associative、 B选项的unconscious、 C选项的impulsive分别是对原文中的associate、 unconsciously、 impulses这些词的同义改写，都是通过<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">换词性</span>的方式，<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">这是考研阅读中经典的出题套路</span>。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">反向干扰</span>：选项内容与原文不符，有时甚至相反，例如“原文说是，选项说不是”、“原文说可以，选项说不可以”，这是<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">考研阅读中经典的错误选项的挖坑陷阱</span>。例如本题的B和D选项，“原文说是无意识的，选项说不是无意识的”、“原文说是冲动的，选项说不是冲动的”。这种反向干扰选项可以直接根据文中的细节比对进行排除。**
