## 真题

**【段 1】**

① Just how much does the Constitution protect your digital data? ② The Supreme Court will now consider whether police can search the contents of a mobile phone without a warrant if the phone is on or around a person during an arrest.

<div align="center">【每日一题 071 · 2015 英语一 Text 2 第 26 题 · 细节题】</div>

**题目:**

26. The Supreme Court will work out whether, during an arrest, it is legitimate to

[A] search for suspects’mobile phones without a warrant.

[B] check suspects’phone contents without being authorized.

[C] prevent suspects from deleting their phone contents.

[D] prohibit suspects from using their mobile phones.


## 生词

✅ Constitution n. 宪法  
✅ digital data 数字数据  
✅ Supreme Court 最高法院  
✅ the contents of... ……的内容  
✅ content n. 内容；含量；目录  
✅ warrant n. 授权证；许可证；执行令  
✅ arrest n. 逮捕，拘捕

## 解题方法

**细节题就是考查对于文章细节内容的理解**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节题的解题方法：细节比对原文！！！</span>**

**判断细节是否正确用<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">“灵魂三问法”</span>：**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2.全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3.匹配否？</span>**

**题干的精准定位可以直接找到答案，但要注意同义改写。题干和正确选项都是对原文的同义改写。**

## 易错点

**A 选项最易错：仔细比对AB选项和原文，发现最关键的信息就是“搜查的到底是手机还是手机里的内容”，搞清楚这个细节，题目就解决了。**
