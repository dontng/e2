## 真题

**【段 1 】**

① The weather in Texas may have cooled since the recent extreme heat, but the temperature will be high at the State Board of Education meeting in Austin this month as officials debate how climate change is taught in Texas schools.

**题目:**

21. In Paragraph 1, the weather in Texas is mentioned to _______

[A] forecast a policy shift in Texas schools.

[B] stress the consequences of climate change.

[C] indicate the atmosphere at the board meeting.

[D] draw the public’s attention to energy shortages.


## 生词

✅ cool v. 使变凉，冷却；冷静下来 adj. 凉爽的；冷静的  
✅ temperature n.（特定场合的）氛围；温度，气温  
✅ debate v. 讨论，辩论；仔细考虑 n. 讨论，辩论  
✅ climate change 气候变化

## 解题方法

**写作目的题考查的是文章中的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">举例、引用或比喻等被使用的目的</span>是为了表达什么。**

**写作目的题的常见提问方式：**

***The example of ... is used to show ________.**

***... is used to illustrate _______.**

***The quotation in Paragraph XXX explains that ________.**

***... is quoted to indicate _______**

***The “empty bottles ” metaphor illustrates that people feel a pressure to.**

***The ants analogy is used to illustrate.**

**写作目的题的解题方法：<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">先找内容，再前后找目的（优先看本句或本段）</span>**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">写作目的题的解题思路，区分“内容”和“目的</span>”：举例、引用或比喻是写作内容，通过题干定位可以找到，但这并不是题目要问的；在内容的前后找其服务的目的，那才是答案所在。**
