我建议你二刷做过的真题。因为真题的意义就在于查缺补漏、巩固提升，与其做更多的真题，不如把做过的真题中的错题好好复习，提分的效果更好。复习错题的时候要注意：

看文章，生词要扫清不会的，定位句或命题句一定要看懂。

看题，要知道每一道题的题目类型和解题方法。

看自己的易错，要知道自己最容易陷入的思维误区，并努力避免。

做到以上，才算是彻底吃透了真题。在吃透的基础上，可以再去做更多的新题。加油！

英语二2018年Text3第35题-写作目的题

【段4】 ①The product they’re selling is data, and we, the users, convert our lives to data for the benefit of the digital giants. ②Just as some ants farm the bugs called aphids for the honeydew they produce when they feed, so Google farms us for the data that our digital lives yield. ③Ants keep predatory insects away from where their aphids feed; Gmail keeps the spammers out of our inboxes. ④It doesn’t feel like a human or democratic relationship, even if both sides benefit.

35. The ants analogy is used to illustrate.

[A] a typical competition pattern among digital giants

[B] a win-win business model between digital giants

[C] the benefits provided for digital giants’ customers

[D] the relationship between digital giants and their users

**答案见视频下方**

## 再看讲解

**正确选项：D**

**解决“做不对”的问题**

## 解题方法

**写作目的题考查的是文章中的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">举例、引用或比喻等被使用的目的</span>是为了表达什么。**

**写作目的题的常见提问方式：**

***The example of ... is used to show ________.**

***... is used to illustrate _______.**

***The quotation in Paragraph XXX explains that ________.**

***... is quoted to indicate _______**

***The “empty bottles ” metaphor illustrates that people feel a pressure to.**

***The ants analogy is used to illustrate.**

**写作目的题的解题方法：<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">先找内容，再前后找目的</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">选项分析也是解题中很关键的技巧：找相同，比较不同</span>**

## 出题点

**这道题的特别之处在于选项的分析。<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">在解题时，如果发现选项有相同点，那么可以找到相同再比较不同，或者是把选项分类。</span>本题的A、B两个选项范围都是在“数字巨头之间”，C选项针对的是“数字巨头的顾客”，而D选项指的是“数字巨头和它们的用户之间”。厘清四个选项的范围，再结合例子，能够更清晰准确地找到答案。**

farm v. 养殖，耕种；承包出去

bug n. 小虫子，昆虫；缺陷，故障

aphid n. 蚜虫

honeydew n. 蜜露，蜜汁

yield v. 出产；屈服，放弃n. 产量

predatory adj. 捕食性的，食肉的

spammer n. 垃圾邮件发送者

democratic adj. 民主的，民主精神的

**英语一2006年Text 2第26题-细节/推断题**

**【段1】 ①Stratford-on-Avon, as we all know, has only one industry—William Shakespeare—but there are two distinctly separate and increasingly hostile branches. ②There is the Royal Shakespeare Company (RSC), which presents superb productions of the plays at the Shakespeare Memorial Theatre on the Avon. ③And there are the townsfolk who largely live off the tourists who come, not to see the plays, but to look at Anne Hathaway’s Cottage, Shakespeare’s birthplace and the other sights.**

**【段2 】 ①The worthy residents of Stratford doubt that the theatre adds a penny to their revenue. ②They frankly dislike the RSC’s actors, them with their long hair and beards and sandals and noisiness. ③It’s all deliciously ironic when you consider that Shakespeare, who earns their living, was himself an actor (with a beard) and did his share of noise-making.**

**26. From the first two paragraphs, we learn that**

**[A] the townsfolk deny the RSC’s contribution to the town’s revenue.**

**[B] the actors of the RSC imitate Shakespeare on and off stage.**

**[C] the two branches of the RSC are not on good terms.**

**[D] the townsfolk earn little from tourism.**
