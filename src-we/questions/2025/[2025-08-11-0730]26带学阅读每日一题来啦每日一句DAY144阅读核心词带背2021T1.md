**英语一2010年Text 1第21题**

【段1 】① Of all the changes that have taken place in English-language newspapers during the past quarter-century, perhaps the most far-reaching has been the inexorable decline in the scope and seriousness of their arts coverage.

【段2 】① It is difficult to the point of impossibility for the average reader under the age of forty to imagine a time when high-quality arts criticism could be found in most big-city newspapers. ② Yet a considerable number of the most significant collections of criticism published in the 20th century consisted in large part of newspaper reviews. ③ To read such books today is to marvel at the fact that their learned contents were once deemed suitable for publication in general-circulation dailies.

**句句真研**

DAY144

词汇 | 语法 | 解析 | 答疑

Young people who are still getting started in life were more likely than older adults to prioritize personal fulfillment in their work, to believe they will advance their careers most by regularly changing jobs, to favor communities with more public services and a faster pace of life, to agree that couples should be financially secure before getting married or having children, and to maintain that children are best served by two parents working outside the home, the survey found.

【2016 英二 Text 4】

思考题：

本句中的平行结构，是什么与什么平行并列？

本句中有几个从句？分别是什么从句？

**视频讲解**

**内容详解**

## 生词

✅ get started 开始

✅ prioritize v. 按优先顺序列出，确定优先顺序；优先处理，优先考虑

✅ personal fulfillment 个人满足感

✅ advance v. 改进，进步；增长, 提高；前进，发展

✅ pace of life 生活节奏

✅ be financially secure 有经济保障

✅ maintain v. 坚持，主张；保持，继续

**分析**

**找谓语动词: are getting/were/will advance/should be/are served/found**

**断开:通过标点符号逗号，以及连接词who/that/that和分析主谓来断开长难句**

**翻译**

调查发现，相比稍微年长一些的成年人来讲，仍处于人生起步阶段的年轻人更可能在工作中优先考虑个人满足感，他们觉得定期更换工作最能推动职业发展，他们更喜欢公共服务更多、生活节奏更快的社区，他们赞同夫妻在结婚生子前应该有经济保障，并认为孩子最好由均在外工作的双亲来抚养。

**语法重点**

**✅平行结构**

**be more likely to do sth. 表示“更有可能去做某事”，其中to do的部分是五个to do的词组平行并列，通过逗号和并列连词and连接起来的，如下：**

**1 to prioritize personal fulfillment in their work,**

**2 to believe they will advance their careers most by regularly changing jobs,**

**3 to favor communities with more public services and a faster pace of life,**

**4 to agree that couples should be financially secure before getting married or having children,**

**5 and to maintain that children are best served by two parents working outside the home**

**✅ 从句**

主句：the survey found（放在最后）

**1 宾语从句：除了主句外，所有余下的部分都是宾语从句 Young people**

**2 定语从句，修饰前面的名词people：who are still getting started in life**

**3 宾语从句，跟在及物动词believe后：they will advance their careers most by regularly changing jobs**

**4 宾语从句，跟在及物动词agree后：that couples should be financially secure before getting married or having children**

**5 宾语从句，跟在及物动词maintain后：that children are best served by two parents working outside the home**
