## 真题

**【段 1 】**

① A century ago, the immigrants from across the Atlantic included settlers and sojourners. ② Along with the many folks looking to make a permanent home in the United States came those who had no intention to stay, and who would make some money and then go home. ③ Between 1908 and 1915, about 7 million people arrived while about 2 million departed. ④ About a quarter of all Italian immigrants, for example, eventually returned to Italy for good. ⑤ They even had an affectionate nickname, “uccelli di passaggio, ” birds of passage.

<div align="center">【每日一题 024 · 2013 英语二 Text 2 第 26 题 · 语义题】</div>

**题目:**

26. “ Birds of passage ” refers to those who _______.

[A] stay in a foreign country temporarily

[B] leave their home countries for good

[C] immigrate across the Atlantic

[D] find permanent jobs overseas


## 生词

✅ immigrant n. 移民（从别国移入） adj. 移民的；迁入的  
✅ immigrate v. 移居（从别国移入）  
✅ immigration n. 外来移民；移居  
✅ settlers n. 定居者  
✅ sojourner n. 旅居者，逗留者  
✅ look to do sth. 寻找做某事的机会  
✅ permanent adj. 永久的，长期的，固定的  
✅ permanently adv. 永久地，长期不变地  
✅ have no intention to do sth. 没打算做某事，无意做某事  
✅ depart v. 离开；出发，启程  
✅ departure n. 离开；出发，启程  
✅ for good 永远地，永久地；一劳永逸地  
✅ affectionate adj. 深情的, 充满深情的  
✅ nickname n. 绰号，小名

## 长难句

**【段 1 】**

② Along with the many folks looking to make a permanent home in the United States came those who had no intention to stay, and who would make some money and then go home.

## 解题方法

**判断语义题：一可以根据题干中的引号，二可以根据原文中的下划线。**

**语义题考查的是文章中的单词、词组或者句子的含义：**

**生词、熟词僻意、指代。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">语义题的解题关键——上下文！</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">根据上下文中的同义词/近义词、逻辑对应/逻辑相反、举例、指代等来判断。</span>**

## 出题点

本题是根据上下文中的指代来猜测词义的。题干考查的内容**Birds of passage位于段1最后一句，该句指出birds of passage是对They的昵称， They指代的是上一句提到的“永久返回意大利的那些意大利移民”，即他们只是短暂地留在美国， A选项stay in a foreign country temporarily （暂时留在国外）与原文相符，因此A选项正确。**

## 易错点

**注意C选项： C选项中的across the Atlantic在段1句①中可以找到，但是横跨大西洋移居的人包括定居者和旅居者两类人，尤其是在看懂了句②这个长难句的基础上能更好地理解这一点，而此处Birds of passage仅指旅居者这一类，因此C选项错误。<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">一定要注意，逢长难句处必出题！！！</span>**
