## 真题

**【段 2 】**

① There are three reasons for the public-sector unions’thriving. ② First, they can shut things down without suffering much in the way of consequences. ③ Second, they are mostly bright and well-educated. ④ A quarter of America’s public-sector workers have a university degree. ⑤ Third, they now dominate left-of-centre politics. ⑥ Some of their ties go back a long way. ⑦ Britain’s Labor Party, as its name implies, has long been associated with trade unionism. ⑧ Its current leader, Ed Miliband, owes his position to votes from public-sector unions.

<div align="center">【每日一题 017 · 2012 英语一 Text 4 第 37 题 · 细节题】</div>

**题目:**

37. Which of the following is true of Paragraph 2?

[A] Public-sector unions are prudent in taking actions.

[B] Education is required for public-sector union membership.

[C] Labor Party has long been fighting against public-sector unions.

[D] Public-sector unions seldom get in trouble for their actions.


## 生词

✅ thrive v. 蓬勃发展，兴旺  
✅ shut things down 使事情停止运转  
✅ consequence n. 后果，结果  
✅ dominate v. 支配，控制；占优势，处于支配地位  
✅ dominant adj. 占优势的，支配的，统治的；显性的  
✅ go back a long way 回溯到很久以前，由来已久  
✅ Labor Party 工党  
✅ as its name implies 顾名思义  
✅ imply v. 意味，暗示  
✅ be associated with... 与……有联系、渊源

## 解题方法

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节题的解题方法：细节比对原文！！！</span>**

**判断细节是否正确用<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">“灵魂三问法”</span>：**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2.全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3.匹配否？</span>**

**（适用于细节题/推断题/观点题）**

**题干没有定位信息时，可根据选项定位到原文。**

## 易错点

**注意同义改写：<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">同义改写是考研阅读中最常用、最经典的出题方式。</span>原文说“公共部门工会可以罢工而无须承担太多后果”， D选项“公共部门工会很少因其行动而陷入麻烦”是对原文的同义改写，因此D选项正确。**
