**【段 4】**

① But it’s not just a gender thing. ② At work, people pretty much know what they’re supposed to be doing: working, making money, doing the tasks they have to do in order to draw an income. ③ The bargain is very pure: Employee puts in hours of physical or mental labor and employee draws out life-sustaining moola.

**题目:**

24. The word “ moola ” (Para. 4) most probably means ________.

[A] skills

[B] energy

[C] earnings

[D] nutrition


## 生词

✅ gender n. 性别  
✅ pretty much 几乎，差不多是  
✅ bargain n. 交易，协议，协定; 便宜货  
✅ put in 投入  
✅ life-sustaining adj. 维持生命的

## 解题方法

**语义题常见的提问形式：**

***The word/phrase/expression/sentence “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) most probably means/refers to/denotes/stands for/suggests _______**

***The word “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) is closest in meaning to _______**

***What does the author mean by “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) ?**

***By “…” (Line…, Paragraph…), the author means/implies ________.**

**判断语义题：一可以根据题干中的引号，二可以根据原文中的下划线。**

**语义题考查的是文章中的单词、词组或者句子的含义：**

**生词、熟词僻意、指代。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">语义题的解题关键——上下文！</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">根据上下文中的同义词/近义词、逻辑对应/逻辑相反、举例、指代、从句、修饰语等来判断。</span>**

## 出题点

**本题是<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">根据上下文中的逻辑对应</span>来猜测词义的。先来看上下文的内容：句②描述人们上班的情形“非常清楚自己应该做什么：工作、赚钱、做他们必须做的任务以赚取收入”；句③阐释交易的纯粹“员工投入数小时的体力或脑力劳动，员工获得维持生活的moola ”。观察上下文可知，<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">句③中的draws out life-sustaining moola对应句②中的draw an income</span>，都是指“获得收入”，因此<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">moola对应income，指的是“收入”</span>， C选项为income的同义改写，因此C选项正确。**
