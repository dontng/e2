英语一2004年Text1第41题-细节题

【段1 】 ① Hunting for a job late last year, lawyer Gant Redmon stumbled across CareerBuilder, a job database on the Internet. ② He searched it with no success but was attracted by the site’s “personal search agent”. ③ It’s an interactive feature that lets visitors key in job criteria such as location, title, and salary, then E-mails them when a matching position is posted in the database. ④ Redmon chose the keywords legal, intellectual property, and Washington, D.C. ⑤ Three weeks later, he got his first notification of an opening. ⑥“I struck gold,” says Redmon, who E-mailed his resume to the employer and won a position as in-house counsel for a company.

41. How did Redmon find his job?

[A] By searching openings in a job database.

[B] By posting a matching position in a database.

[C] By using a special service of a database.

[D] By E-mailing his resume to a database.

**答案见视频下方**

## 再看讲解

**正确选项：C**

**解决“做不对”的问题**

## 解题方法

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节比对原文！</span>所有涉及到考查细节的题目，都要<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">拿着选项仔细地去比对原文</span>，逐词比对，因为考研阅读在考查细节时会考得非常细。绝对不要凭借脑子里的印象去想，非常易错。**

**判断细节是否正确的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">“灵魂三问法”：</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1)有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2)全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3)匹配否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">（如上适用于细节题/推断题/观点题）</span>**

**正确选项可以是对原文的同义改写或高度概括。**

## 出题点

**解决细节题的关键就是把选项逐一跟原文进行比对，运用静姐的“灵魂三问”法，仔仔细细比对每一个单词的内容。特别注意一下B选项的主被动和动作发出者；还要注意对比A和C选项与题干所问是否匹配。**

## 生词

stumble v.意外发现，偶然碰见；跌倒； 结巴地说话

database n.数据库

search agent搜索引擎，搜索代理

interactive adj.交互的；互相作用的，互相影响的

interact v.相互作用，相互影响

interaction n.相互作用，相互影响

key in...键入，输入……

criteria n. （做出判断、决定的）标准，准则（criterion的复数形式）

matching adj.匹配的

intellectual property知识产权

notification n.通知，通知书

resume n.个人简历；摘要
