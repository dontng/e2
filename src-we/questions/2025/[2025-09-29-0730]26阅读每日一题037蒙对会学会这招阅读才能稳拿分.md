英语一2002年Text2第47题-语义题

【段2】 ①As a result, the modern world is increasingly populated by intelligent gizmos whose presence we barely notice but whose universal existence has removed much human labor. ②Our factories hum to the rhythm of robot assembly arms. ③Our banking is done at automated teller terminals that thank us with mechanical politeness for the transaction. ④Our subway trains are controlled by tireless robot-drivers. ⑤And thanks to the continual miniaturization of electronics and micro-mechanics, there are already robot systems that can perform some kinds of brain and bone surgery with submillimeter accuracy—far greater precision than highly skilled physicians can achieve with their hands alone.

47. The word “gizmos” (Para. 2) most probably means

[A] programs.

[B] experts.

[C] devices.

[D] creatures.

**答案见视频下方**

## 再看讲解

**正确选项：C**

**解决“做不对”的问题**

## 解题方法

**判断语义题：一可以根据题干中的引号，二可以根据原文中的下划线。**

**语义题考查的是文章中的单词、词组或者句子的含义：**

**生词、熟词僻意、指代。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">语义题的解题关键——上下文！</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">根据上下文中的同义词/近义词、逻辑对应/逻辑相反、举例、指代、从句、修饰语等来判断。</span>**

## 出题点

**本题是<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">根据上下文中的举例、定语从句</span>来猜测词义的。根据gizmos后面的定语从句和其后的举例robot assembly arms（机器人装配臂）、automated teller terminals（自动柜员终端）和robot-drivers（机器人司机）可以推测出gizmos的词义。**

**解决“看不懂”的问题**

## 生词

populate v. 聚居

presence n. 在场，出席；存在

universal existence普遍的存在

remove human labor解放人类劳动力

hum v. 发出嗡嗡声 n. 嗡嗡声（文中表示“很忙碌”）

rhythm n. 节奏；韵律

assembly n. 装配，安装；聚集

automated teller terminals自动柜员终端

terminal n. 终端，终端设备；航站楼

politeness n. 有礼貌；有礼貌的行为

transaction n. 交易，业务；办理

tireless adj. 不知疲倦的，不觉得劳累的

thanks to…多亏了，由于

continual adj. 持续的，频繁的，多次重复的

electronics n. 电子器件

surgery n. 外科手术

accuracy n. 精确度，准确性

accurate adj. 精确的

precision n. 精确度，准确性
