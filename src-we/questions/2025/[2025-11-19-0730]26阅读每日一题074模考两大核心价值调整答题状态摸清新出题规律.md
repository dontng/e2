## 真题

**【段 1 】**

① The Internet may be changing merely what we remember, not our capacity to do so, suggests Columbia University psychology professor Betsy Sparrow. ⑧ In other words, human memory is not deteriorating but “adapting to new communications technology, ” Sparrow says.

**【段 3 】**

② There is no experimental evidence showing that it interferes with our ability to focus, for instance, wrote psychologists Christopher Chabris and Daniel J. Simons. ③ And surfing the web exercised the brain more than reading did among computer-savvy older adults in a 2008 study involving 24 participants at the Semel Institute for Neuroscience and Human Behavior at the University of California, Los Angeles.

**【段 4 】**

①“There may be costs associated with our increased reliance on the Internet, but I’d have to imagine that overall the benefits are going to outweigh those costs, ” observes psychology professor Benjamin Storm. ②“It seems pretty clear that memory is changing, but is it changing for the better? ③ At this point, we don’t know. ”

<div align="center">【每日一题 074 · 2023 英语二 Text 3 第 35 题 · 观点题】</div>

**题目:**

35. Neither Sparrow nor Storm would agree that _______.

[A] our reliance on the Internet will be costly

[B] the Internet is weakening our memory

[C] memory exercise is a must for our brains

[D] our ability to focus declines with age


## 生词

✅ merely adv. 仅仅，只不过  
✅ capacity n. 能力；容量  
✅ deteriorate v. 退化，恶化  
✅ deterioration n. 退化，恶化  
✅ experimental adj. 实验性的，试验性的  
✅ experiment n. 实验 v. 做实验  
✅ interfere v. 妨碍；干涉，干预，介入  
✅ computer-savvy 精通电脑的  
✅ associated adj. 有关联的，相关的  
✅ outweigh v. 超过，重于，大于

## 解题方法

**观点题就是考查某人发出的观点内容。**

**识别观点题**

**Sb. think/believe/hold/suggest/argue that…**

**According to sb.,…**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">注意提问方式的多样化，例如本题： What does Quinn think of Hardy?</span>**

**观点题的解题方法**

**1.人：作者不用找，其他人要明确找到**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">不止一方观点时，要区分发出观点的人</span>**

**2.观点细节：细节比对原文， “灵魂三问法”**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">本题是比较特别的观点题，注意出题的全新角度</span>：不是传统的提问一个人对事情的观点，而是<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">提问“两个人都不会同意”的观点</span>，因此要先在文章中定位找到这两个人，再根据两人的观点进行反向推测，还得找到不同意的观点的交集，才是正确答案。**
