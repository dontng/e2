## 真题

**【段 1】**

①Stratford-on-Avon, as we all know, has only one industry—William Shakespeare—but there are two distinctly separate and increasingly hostile branches. ②There is the Royal Shakespeare Company (RSC), which presents superb productions of the plays at the Shakespeare Memorial Theatre on the Avon. ③And there are the townsfolk who largely live off the tourists who come, not to see the plays, but to look at Anne Hathaway’s Cottage, Shakespeare’s birthplace and the other sights.

**【段 2 】**

①The worthy residents of Stratford doubt that the theatre adds a penny to their revenue. ②They frankly dislike the RSC’s actors, them with their long hair and beards and sandals and noisiness. ③It’s all deliciously ironic when you consider that Shakespeare, who earns their living, was himself an actor (with a beard) and did his share of noise-making.

<div align="center">【每日一题 039 · 2006 英语一 Text 2 第 26 题 · 细节/推断题】</div>

**题目:**

26. From the first two paragraphs, we learn that

[A] the townsfolk deny the RSC’s contribution to the town’s revenue.

[B] the actors of the RSC imitate Shakespeare on and off stage.

[C] the two branches of the RSC are not on good terms.

[D] the townsfolk earn little from tourism.

✅ v. （使）分离，隔开；分开，分居；区别（于）

## 解题方法

**不用纠结于区分细节题和推断题，因为<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题和细节题的解题方法一样</span>，都是考查对于文章细节内容的理解。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题千万不要推断！！！</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节比对原文，运用“灵魂三问法”：</span>**

**1)有否？**

**2)全否？**

**3)匹配否？**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题的正确答案往往是对原文信息换种方式表达，比如同义改写、正话反说、高度概括。</span>**

## 易错点

**C选项最易错：同学们一看到the two branches... are not on good terms（两个分支关系不好），凭借读完文章的印象就觉得这个选项好像是对的，但其实不是，因为关系不好的不是the two branches of the RSC（皇家莎士比亚剧团的两个分支），而是the two branches of Stratford-on-Avon（Stratford这个地方的两个分支），C选项偷换概念，因此错误。考查细节时，这类非常具有迷惑性的选项是大家最易错的，所以时刻记得运用“灵魂三问法”仔细比对选项和原文。**
