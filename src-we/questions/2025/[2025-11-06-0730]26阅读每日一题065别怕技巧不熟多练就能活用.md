## 真题

**【段 3】**

①The tourist streams are not entirely separate. ②The sightseers who come by bus—and often take in Warwick Castle and Blenheim Palace on the side—don’t usually see the plays, and some of them are even surprised to find a theatre in Stratford. ③However, the playgoers do manage a little sight-seeing along with their playgoing. ④It is the playgoers, the RSC contends, who bring in much of the town’s revenue because they spend the night (some of them four or five nights) pouring cash into the hotels and restaurants. ⑤The sightseers can take in everything and get out of town by nightfall.

**题目:**

27. It can be inferred from Paragraph 3 that

[A] the sightseers cannot visit the Castle and the Palace separately.

[B] the playgoers spend more money than the sightseers.

[C] the sightseers do more shopping than the playgoers.

[D] the playgoers go to no other places in town than the theater.


## 生词

✅ stream n. (人)流，(车)流；溪流  
✅ entirely adv. 完全地，彻底地；全部地，完整地  
✅ entire adj. 全部的，整个的；全体的  
✅ sightseer n. 观光客，游客  
✅ playgoer n. 爱看戏的人，戏迷  
✅ manage v. 设法做到，勉力完成  
✅ along with... 连同……一起，与……一道  
✅ contend v. 声称，主张，认为

## 解题方法

**推断题的判断： be inferred/learned/indicated**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题千万不要推断！！！</span>解题方法同细节题。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节比对原文，运用“灵魂三问法”：</span>**

**1)有否？**

**2)全否？**

**3)匹配否？**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题的正确答案往往是对原文信息换种方式表达，比如同义改写、正话反说、高度概括。</span>**

**反向排除法：当三个选项都有明显错误时，那么剩余的这个选项自然就是正确答案。**

## 出题点

**AC选项都是利用原文中出现过的信息，再加上捏造的错误内容创造的干扰项。**

**D选项是对原文的反向干扰，把原文内容说反了。**

**B选项正确是对于原文的高度概括。**
