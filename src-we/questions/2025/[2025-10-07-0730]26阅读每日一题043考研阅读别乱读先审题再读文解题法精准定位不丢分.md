## 真题

**【段 4】**

①And this process need not be left to the unconscious. ②Cartwright believes one can exercise conscious control over recurring bad dreams. ③As soon as you awaken, identify what is upsetting about the dream. ④Visualize how you would like it to end instead; the next time it occurs, try to wake up just enough to control its course. ⑤With much practice people can learn to, literally, do it in their sleep.

**题目:**

34. Cartwright seems to suggest that

[A] waking up in time is essential to the ridding of bad dreams.

[B] visualizing bad dreams helps bring them under control.

[C] dreams should be left to their natural progression.

[D] dreaming may not entirely belong to the unconscious.


## 生词

✅ exercise v. 练习，使用；运动，锻炼  
✅ recur v. 重现，重复出现，再来  
✅ visualize vt. 设想，想像  
✅ literally adv. 确实地，真正地；照字面地

## 解题方法

**观点题就是考查某人发出的观点内容。**

**识别观点题**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">Sb. think/believe/hold/suggest/argue that…</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">According to sb.,…</span>**

**观点题的解题方法**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.人：作者不用找，其他人要明确找到</span>**

**2.观点细节：细节比对原文， “灵魂三问法”**

**正确答案是对原文的同义改写**

**选项合并分析：两个选项相反，可以放在一起比对原文**

## 出题点

**同义改写： D选项dreaming may not entirely belong to the unconscious （做梦可能不完全属于无意识的），对应原文中的句① this process need not be left to the unconscious（这一过程不一定是无意识的），属于同义改写，因此D选项正确。**

**反向干扰： C选项表达的与D选项和原文正好相反，说“应该让梦顺其自然”，即“梦是无意识的，没有受控制的”，属于反向干扰，因此C选项错误。**
