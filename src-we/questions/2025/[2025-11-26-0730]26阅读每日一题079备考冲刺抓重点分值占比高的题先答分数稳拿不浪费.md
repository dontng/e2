## 真题

**【段 1 】**

① Communities throughout New England have been attempting to regulate short-term rentals since sites like Airbnb took off in the 2010s. ② Now, with record-high home prices and historically low inventory, there’s an increased urgency in such regulation, particularly among those who worry that developers will come in and buy up swaths of housing to flip for a fortune on the short-term rental market.

**【段 2 】**

① In New Hampshire, where the rental vacancy rate has dropped below 1 percent, housing advocates fear unchecked short-term rentals will put further pressure on an already strained market. ② The state Legislature recently voted against a bill that would’ve made it illegal for towns to create legislation restricting short-term rentals.

<div align="center">【每日一题 079 · 2023 英语一 Text 2 第 26 题 · 细节题】</div>

**题目:**

26. Which of the following is true of New England?

[A] Its housing supply is at a very low level.

[B] Its communities are in need of funding.

[C] Its rental vacancy rate is going up slowly.

[D] Its home prices are under strict control.


## 生词

✅ community n. 社区，社会；团体  
✅ attempt v. 尝试，企图；努力  
✅ regulate v. 管理，控制，约束；调节  
✅ regulation n. 章程，规章制度；管理，控制  
✅ take off 突然大受欢迎；（飞机）起飞；脱下；离开  
✅ inventory n. 库存，存货；财产清单  
✅ urgency n. 紧急，迫切  
✅ vacancy rate 空置率  
✅ vacancy n. 空缺，空额  
✅ advocate n. 支持者，拥护者 v. 拥护，支持，提倡  
✅ unchecked adj. 不受限制的，不加约束的，放任的  
✅ put pressure on… 对……施加压力  
✅ strained adj. 紧张的，神色不宁的；勉强的  
✅ vote against… 投票反对……  
✅ illegal adj. 违法的，不合理的  
✅ legislation n. 制定法律，立法；法规，法律  
✅ legislate v. 立法，制定法律  
✅ restrict v. 限制，束缚，阻碍  
✅ restriction n. 限制，约束

## 解题方法

**细节题就是考查对于文章细节内容的理解**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节题的解题方法：细节比对原文！！！</span>**

**判断细节是否正确用<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">“灵魂三问法”</span>：**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2.全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3.匹配否？</span>**

**除了根据题干定位之外，也可以根据选项定位到原文。**

**正确选项是对原文的同义改写、正话反说或高度概括。**

**注意错误选项常用的挖坑陷阱。**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">偷换概念</span>：原文段1句①指出新英格兰的社区一直试图“规范短租”；句②指出“实施此类监管的紧迫性增加”，这里的“此类监管”指的就是上文说的“规范短租”。由此可知新英格兰的社区<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">迫切需要规范短租</span>，而选项说“它（新英格兰）的社区<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">迫切需要资金</span>”，<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">资金≠规范短租</span>，属于偷换概念，因此B选项错误。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">反向干扰+答非所问</span>：原文段2句①说“在新罕布什尔州，出租房屋空置率已<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">下降</span>至1%以下”，而选项说的是“它（新英格兰）的出租房屋空置率正在缓慢<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">上升</span>”，选项与原文意思相悖，属于<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">反向干扰</span>。题干问的是有关<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">New England</span>新英格兰的情况，而原文说的是新英格兰所辖<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">New Hampshire</span>的情况，选项所答与题干所问不符，属于<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">答非所问</span>，因此C选项错误。**
