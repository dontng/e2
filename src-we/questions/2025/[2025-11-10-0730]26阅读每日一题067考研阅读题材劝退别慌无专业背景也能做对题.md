## 真题

**【段 3 】**

①The conflict has been surfacing since 2002, when the corporation bought Vermont’s only nuclear power plant, an aging reactor in Vernon. ②As a condition of receiving state approval for the sale, the company agreed to seek permission from state regulators to operate past 2012. ③In 2006, the state went a step further, requiring that any extension of the plant’s license be subject to the Vermont legislature’s approval. ④Then, too, the company went along.

<div align="center">【每日一题 067 · 2012 英语一 Text 2 第 27 题 · 细节题】</div>

**题目:**

27. By entering into the 2002 agreement, Entergy intended to

[A] obtain protection from Vermont regulators.

[B] seek favor from the federal legislature.

[C] acquire an extension of its business license.

[D] get permission to purchase a power plant.

✅ as a condition of... 作为……的条件  
✅ approval n. 批准，赞成；认可  
✅ approve v. 赞成，批准

✅ extension of the license 许可证延期  
✅ extension n. 延期；延长，伸展  
✅ extend v. 延伸，伸展，扩大

## 解题方法

**细节题就是考查对于文章细节内容的理解**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节题的解题方法：细节比对原文！！！</span>**

**判断细节是否正确用<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">“灵魂三问法”</span>：**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2.全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3.匹配否？</span>**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">在“多点、多类、多方面”的地方易出细节题，注意区分不同点、不同类、不同方面，关键要看题干提问的是什么。</span>例如本题中出现了2002年和2006年两个不同的协议内容，题干提问的是2002年的，所以选项中出自2006年的内容都可以直接排除。**
