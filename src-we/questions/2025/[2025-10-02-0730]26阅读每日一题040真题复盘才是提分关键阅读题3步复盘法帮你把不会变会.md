## 真题

**【段 4】**

①President Trump has underlined fossil fuels—especially coal—as the path to economic growth. ②In a recent speech in Iowa, he dismissed wind power as an unreliable energy source. ③But that message did not play well with many in Iowa, where wind turbines dot the fields and provide 36 percent of the state’s electricity generation—and where tech giants like Microsoft are being attracted by the availability of clean energy to power their data centers.

<div align="center">【每日一题 040 · 2018 英语二 Text 2 第 28 题 · 推断题】</div>

**题目:**

28. It can be learned that in Iowa,.

[A] wind energy has replaced fossil fuels

[B] there is a shortage of clean energy supply

[C] tech giants are investing in clean energy

[D] wind is a widely used energy source


## 生词

✅ underline v. 强调；在……下面画线  
✅ path n. 道路，途径  
✅ dismiss v. 拒绝考虑，否定，驳回；解雇，使离开  
✅ unreliable adj. 不可靠的  
✅ reliable adj. 可靠的，可信赖的  
✅ availability n. 可利用性，可获得性

## 解题方法

**推断题的判断： be inferred/learned/indicated**

**不用纠结于区分细节题和推断题，因为<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题和细节题的解题方法一样</span>，都是考查对于文章细节内容的理解。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题千万不要推断！！！</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节比对原文，运用“灵魂三问法”：</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1)有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2)全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3)匹配否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题的正确答案往往是对原文信息换种方式表达，比如同义改写、正话反说、高度概括。</span>**

## 易错点

**反向排除法：正确选项D是对于原文的高度概括，因为没办法跟原文的信息直接字对字地对应，所以大家通常不敢选。没关系，可以<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">采用排除法</span>，根据静姐的“灵魂三问法”<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">排除掉三个有明显错误的选项，那么剩下的这个选项就是正确答案。</span>**
