## 真题

**【段 1 】**

① Many Americans regard the jury system as a concrete expression of crucial democratic values, including the principles that all citizens who meet minimal qualifications of age and literacy are equally competent to serve on juries; that jurors should be selected randomly from a representative cross section of the community; that no citizen should be denied the right to serve on a jury on account of race, religion, sex, or national origin; that defendants are entitled to trial by their peers; and that verdicts should represent the conscience of the community and not just the letter of the law.

<div align="center">【每日一题 008 · 2010 英语二 Text 4 第 36 题 · 细节/推断题】</div>

**题目:**

36. From the principles of the US jury system, we learn that _______.

[A] both literate and illiterate people can serve on juries

[B] defendants are immune from trial by their peers

[C] no age limit should be imposed for jury service

[D] judgment should consider the opinion of the public


## 生词

✅ regard … as … 把……看作……  
✅ jury n. 陪审团  
✅ concrete adj. 具体的；有形的  
✅ crucial adj. 重要的，决定性的  
✅ democratic adj. 民主的, 民主政治的；平等的  
✅ democracy n. 民主，民主主义；民主政治  
✅ principle n. 原则, 原理; 准则, 规范  
✅ minimal adj. 极小的，最小的  
✅ qualification n. 资格；资历；学历  
✅ literacy n. 读写能力  
✅ competent adj. 有能力的；足以胜任的  
✅ serve on a jury 担任陪审员  
✅ juror n. 陪审员  
✅ randomly adv. 随机地；随意地  
✅ random adj. 随机的，任意的  
✅ representative n. 代表 adj. 典型的；有代表性的  
✅ on account of…因为，由于  
✅ defendant n. 被告  
✅ be entitled to sth.享有某事的资格  
✅ peer n. 同等的人  
✅ verdict n. 裁决，裁定  
✅ conscience n. 良心，良知

## 解题方法

**不用纠结于区分细节题和推断题，因为都是一样的解题方法。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节题/推断题/观点题</span>的解题都可以采用<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节比对原文</span>，运用“<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">灵魂三问法</span>”：**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1)有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2)全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3)匹配否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">长难句的平行结构（并列多点）易出题，考查细节。</span>把每一点前标记数字12345，方便解题。**

## 易错点

**A和C两个选项都是针对于原文句①中第一个that从句出题，注意选项（没有文化水平和年龄的限制）与原文（有文化水平和年龄的最低标准）仔细对应，就会发现与原文不符，错误。**

**D选项虽然乍一看与原文字面意思不完全相同，但在上下文背景中意思相同，即为同义改写，正确。**
