## 真题

**【段 2 】**

① Even before Alan Greenspan’s admission that America’s red-hot economy is cooling, lots of working folks had already seen signs of the slowdown themselves. ② From car dealerships to Gap outlets, sales have been lagging for months as shoppers temper their spending. ③ For retailers, who last year took in 24 percent of their revenue between Thanksgiving and Christmas, the cautious approach is coming at a crucial time. ④ Already, experts say, holiday sales are off 7 percent from last year’s pace. ⑤ But don’t sound any alarms just yet. ⑥ Consumers seem only mildly concerned, not panicked, and many say they remain optimistic about the economy’s long-term prospects even as they do some modest belt-tightening.

**题目:**

52. How do the public feel about the current economic situation?

[A] Optimistic.

[B] Confused.

[C] Carefree.

[D] Panicked.


## 生词

✅ admission n. 承认；准许进入  
✅ admit v. 承认；许可进入  
✅ red-hot economy 过热的经济  
✅ working folks 上班族  
✅ slowdown n. 减速，减缓  
✅ dealership n. 代理权，经销权  
✅ outlet n. 专卖店，经销店；打折店；出口，出路  
✅ lag v. 滞后，落后  
✅ temper v. 使缓和，调节（文中指 “ 减少 ” ）  
✅ retailer n. 零售商  
✅ revenue n. 总收入，收益；税收  
✅ cautious adj. 小心的，谨慎的  
✅ approach n. 靠近, 接近; 方式, 途径 v. 走近，靠近  
✅ at a crucial time 在这关键时刻  
✅ crucial adj. 至关重要的，关键的；决定性的  
✅ sound alarms 拉响警报  
✅ mildly adv. 适度地；温和地，和善地  
✅ panicked adj. 恐慌的  
✅ panic n. 恐慌，惊慌 v. 使恐慌  
✅ remain optimistic about... 关于……保持乐观

## 解题方法

**态度题考查的是某人对某事的态度，绝大多数考查的都是作者的态度。**

**识别态度题：可以通过题干（出现attitude/view/feel等词）判断，更直接的是<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">通过选项（单个单词表示态度）判断</span>。**

**态度题的解题步骤：**

**1)<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">审题：看清楚提问的是“谁”对“什么”的态度。</span>**

**（作者通常不用找，但其他人必须在原文中定位找到。）**

**2)<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">选项分类：积极/消极/中立</span>**

**3)<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">判断态度：根据在原文中定位处前后的表示感情色彩的词</span>，判断态度的方向。**

**认识表示态度的词很重要**

**but转折处最容易出题，注意前后内容的逻辑相反**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">注意but转折处</span>：虽然这段话前面都在讲经济状况如何如何不好，但是<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">But一出现就意味着前后内容是逻辑相反的</span>， But之后开始说： But don’t sound any alarms （不必拉响警报）， only mildly concerned （只是有一些担心）， not panicked （没有恐慌）， remain optimistic （保持乐观）。从这些词可以很明显地看出“乐观的”态度，因此A选项正确。**
