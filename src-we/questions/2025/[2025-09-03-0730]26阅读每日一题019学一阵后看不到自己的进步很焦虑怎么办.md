## 真题

**【段 4 】**

① But don’t bother trying to kill off old habits; once those ruts of procedure are worn into the brain, they’re there to stay. ② Instead, the new habits we deliberately press into ourselves create parallel pathways that can bypass those old roads.

<div align="center">【每日一题 019 · 2009 英语一 Text 1 第 23 题 · 语义题】</div>

**题目:**

23. The word "ruts"(Para. 4) is closest meaning to _______.

[A] tracks

[B] series

[C] characteristics

[D] connections


## 生词

✅ don’t bother doing sth. 别自寻烦恼做某事，别费心做某事  
✅ bother v. 费心；烦扰，打扰  
✅ kill off old habits 扼杀、改掉旧习惯  
✅ kill off... 扼杀、停止、根除 …...  
✅ deliberately adv. 故意地，蓄意地；慎重地，谨慎地  
✅ pathway n. 小路，小径  
✅ bypass v. 绕过，避开

## 解题方法

**语义题常见的提问形式：**

***The word/phrase/expression/sentence “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) most probably means/refers to/denotes/stands for/suggests _______**

***The word “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) is closest in meaning to _______**

***What does the author mean by “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) ?**

***By “…” (Line…, Paragraph…), the author means/implies ________.**

**判断语义题：一可以根据题干中的引号，二可以根据原文中的下划线。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">生词、熟词僻意、指代。</span>**

## 出题点

**本题是<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">根据上下文中的逻辑对应</span>来猜测词义的。<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);"> new habits（新习惯）对应parallel pathways（相应的道路），而old habits（旧习惯）对应old roads（旧路）</span>，由此可见新旧习惯都对应的是“路”，因此旧习惯的ruts的含义也应该与“路”相近，所以选择tracks “路径，踪迹；车辙”，因此 A 选项正确。**
