## 真题

**【段4】**

①Railroads justify rate discrimination against captive shippers on the grounds that in the long run it reduces everyone’s cost. ②If railroads charged all customers the same average rate, they argue, shippers who have the option of switching to trucks or other forms of transportation would do so, leaving remaining customers to shoulder the cost of keeping up the line. ③It’s a theory to which many economists subscribe, but in practice it often leaves railroads in the position of determining which companies will flourish and which will fail. ④“Do we really want railroads to be the arbiters of who wins and who loses in the marketplace?” asks Martin Bercovici, a Washington lawyer who frequently represents shippers.

## 解题方法

**语义题常见的提问形式：**

***The word/phrase/expression/sentence “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) most probably means/refers to/denotes/stands for/suggests _______**

***The word “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) is closest in meaning to ______**

***What does the author mean by “…” (Line…, Paragraph…) ?**

***By “…” (Line…, Paragraph…), the author means/implies ________.**

**语义题考查的是文章中的单词、词组或者句子的含义：**

**生词、熟词僻意、指代。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">语义题的解题关键——上下文！</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">根据上下文中的同义词/近义词、逻辑对应/逻辑相反、举例、指代、从句、修饰语等来判断。</span>**

## 易错点

**it often leaves railroads<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);"> in the position of determining</span> which companies will flourish and which will fail**

**Do we really want railroads to be the<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);"> arbiters</span> of who wins and who loses**

**本题是<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">根据上下文中的逻辑对应</span>来猜测词义的。如上，句③和句④的结构对应，根据对应可以推测出arbiters的词义应该与determining（决定）相关。但是并不是D选项，因为这里决定的不是价格price，因此就找到了determine的同义改写B选项who function as judges （起仲裁者作用的人）。**
