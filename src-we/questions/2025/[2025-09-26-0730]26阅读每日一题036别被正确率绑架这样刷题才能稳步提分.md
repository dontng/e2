别着急，大家最近都会这样，觉得遇到了瓶颈期，努力做题还是会错。

首先，不要纠结于正确率，这个不重要。现阶段的正确率会受很多因素的影响，比如每篇文章的难度不一样；有的题虽然错了，但你仔细看看，你实际上是会的；有的是因为单词不认识，但是背了不会的单词，你还会进一步提升的，所以这个不能说明你的真实水平或最终水平。

其次，考前所有的错题都是在给你机会，了解自己，找到薄弱点，定向解决，就会稳步提升。<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">现阶段要看的不是正确率，而是总结错题的原因，汇总自己易错的点，把不会的、没背下来的、易错的都解决了。</span>

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">“发现问题——分析问题——解决问题”，这才是做题的意义，加油吧！</span>

英语二2016年Text3第32题-写作目的题

【段3】 ①In fact, “becoming more efficient” is part of the problem. ②Thinking of time as a resource to be maximised means you approach it instrumentally, judging any given moment as well spent only in so far as it advances progress toward some goal. ③Immersive reading, by contrast, depends on being willing to risk inefficiency, goallessness, even time-wasting. ④Try to slot it in as a to-do list item and you’ll manage only goal-focused reading—useful, sometimes, but not the most fulfilling kind. ⑤“The future comes at us like empty bottles along an unstoppable and nearly infinite conveyor belt,” writes Gary Eberle in his book Sacred Time, and “we feel a pressure to fill these different-sized bottles (days, hours, minutes) as they pass, for if they get by without being filled, we will have wasted them.” ⑥No mind-set could be worse for losing yourself in a book.

32. The “empty bottles” metaphor illustrates that people feel a pressure to.

[A] update their to-do lists

[B] make passing time fulfilling

[C] carry their plans through

[D] pursue carefree reading

**正确选项：B**

## 解题方法

**写作目的题考查的是文章中的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">举例、引用或比喻等被使用的目的</span>是为了表达什么。**

**写作目的题的常见提问方式：**

***The example of ... is used to show ________.**

***... is used to illustrate _______.**

***The quotation in Paragraph XXX explains that ________.**

***... is quoted to indicate _______**

***The “empty bottles ” metaphor illustrates that people feel a pressure to.**

**写作目的题的解题方法<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">：通常，先找内容，再前后找目的</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">但这道题更简单，不用自己找，题干定位就解决了。</span>**

## 出题点

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">题干的精准定位可以直接找到答案</span>**：首先，根据题干中的empty bottles先定位找到比喻；然后，再根据题干中的feel a pressure to直接精准定位找到答案，即比喻要表达的目的。**

## 生词

maximise v. 最大限度利用，充分利用

approach v. 处理，对待；走近，接近

advance progress toward some goal 朝着某个目标前进

advance v. 前进，发展；促进，推动

immersive adj. 沉浸式的

by contrast相比之下，与之对比

inefficiency n. 效率低，无效率

time-wasting adj. 浪费时间的

slot in安置，安排

to-do list任务清单

fulfilling adj. 使人满足的，令人愉快的；能实现个人抱负的

fulfill v. 履行；实现；使满意

infinite adj. 无限的; 无穷的; 无数的

conveyor belt传送带

mind-set思维模式，思想倾向
