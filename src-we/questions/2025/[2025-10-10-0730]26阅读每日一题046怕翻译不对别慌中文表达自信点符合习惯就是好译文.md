## 真题

**【段 2】**

①This episode crystallizes the irony that although American men tend to talk more than women in public situations, they often talk less at home. ②And this pattern is wreaking havoc with marriage.

**【段 3 】**

①The pattern was observed by political scientist Andrew Hacker in the late 1970s. ②Sociologist Catherine Kohler Riessman reports in her new book Divorce Talk that most of the women she interviewed—but only a few of the men—gave lack of communication as the reason for their divorces. ③Given the current divorce rate of nearly 50 percent, that amounts to millions of cases in the United States every year—a virtual epidemic of failed conversation.

**【段 4 】**

②Instead, they focused on communication: “He doesn’t listen to me.” “He doesn’t talk to me.”

**题目:**

28. All of the following are true EXCEPT.

[A] men tend to talk more in public than women

[B] nearly 50 percent of recent divorces are caused by failed conversation

[C] women attach much importance to communication between couples

[D] a female tends to be more talkative at home than her spouse


## 生词

✅ episode n. 一段情节；插曲；一集  
✅ crystallize v. 使……明确化、具体化  
✅ irony n. 讽刺  
✅ tend to do sth. 往往做某事，倾向于做某事  
✅ sociologist n. 社会学家  
✅ interview v./n. 采访，面谈；面试  
✅ lack of communication 缺乏交流，缺乏沟通  
✅ lack of... 缺乏  
✅ given prep. (表示原因)鉴于；考虑到  
✅ virtual adj. 实际上的，事实上的  
✅ epidemic n. 流行病；流行

## 解题方法

**细节题就是考查对于文章细节内容的理解**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节题的解题方法：细节比对原文！！！</span>**

**判断细节是否正确用<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">“灵魂三问法”</span>：**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2.全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3.匹配否？</span>**

**（适用于细节题/推断题/观点题）**

**正确选项可以是对原文的同义改写、正话反说或高度概括。**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">注意题干中的否定，避免把题答反</span>：例如本题中的EXCEPT，还有很多其他否定词，如not、without、fail to do（没能做到）等。**

## 易错点

**B选项干扰性极强：难点在于区分the current divorce rate of nearly 50 percent（当前约 50% 的离婚率）和 nearly 50 percent of recent divorces（最近离婚中的约 50%）。前后单词的顺序不同以及一字之差（少了rate）意思都会不一样。**

**l D选项是对原文的正话反说：原文说的是“男性在家里说话较少”，这句话反过来说就是D选项的“女性在家里比配偶说话更多”。**
