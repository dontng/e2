## 真题

**【段 3 】**

① But as companies continue their attempts at personalised medicine, the courts will remain rather busy. ② The Myriad case itself is probably not over. ③ Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented; gene patents suppress innovation rather than reward it; and patents’monopolies restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad’s. ④ A growing number seem to agree.

**题目:**

32. Those who are against gene patents believe that _______.

[A] genetic tests are not reliable

[B] only man-made products are patentable

[C] patents on genes depend much on innovation

[D] courts should restrict access to genetic tests


## 生词

✅ personalised medicine 个性化医疗  
✅ be over 完结  
✅ make arguments against... 提出反对 ……的理由  
✅ suppress v. 抑制，制止；镇压, 压制  
✅ innovation n. 创新，革新  
✅ reward v. 奖励，奖赏，酬谢，报答  
✅ monopoly n. 垄断; 垄断者, 垄断企业  
✅ restrict access to … 限制对 …的使用  
✅ restrict v. 限制，约束  
✅ restriction n. 限制，约束  
✅ restrictive adj. 限制的，约束的

## 长难句

**【段 3】**

③ Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented; gene patents suppress innovation rather than reward it; and patents’monopolies restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad’s.

## 解题方法

识别观点题

Sb. think/believe/hold/suggest/argue that…

According to sb., …

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">观点题的解题方法</span>

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.人：找到发出观点的人（作者不用找）</span>

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2.观点细节：细节比对原文，“灵魂三问法”</span>

<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">正确选项的设置常用“同义改写/高度概括/正话反说”</span>

## 易错点

**注意B选项：原文说的是“基因是天然产物，因此它不可申请专利”，这句话反过来说就是“批评者们认为可申请专利的应该是非天然产物，即人造产品”； B选项only man-made products are patentable（只有人造产品可以申请专利），属于对原文的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">正话反说</span>，因此正确。**

**注意C和D选项：选项中的部分内容都可以在原文中找到，但是有个别的信息被偷换了，属于<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">偷换概念</span>，与原文不符，因此要仔细比对原文。**

## 出题点

**逢长难句处必出题！！！长难句的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">平行结构最易出考查细节的题目</span>。例如本篇文章中的第三段句③，根据这个平行结构，改编出了这道题的四个选项。**
