## 真题

**【段 2 】**

① Pat Hardy, who sympathises with the views of the energy sector, is resisting proposed changes to science standards for pre-teen pupils. ②“There are as many scientists working against all the panic of global climate change as there are those who are pushing it, ” she claims. ③“Texas is an energy state and we need to recognise that. ④ You need to remember where your bread is buttered.

**【段 3 】**

① Most scientists and experts sharply dispute Hardy’s views. ② Board members like her “casually dismiss the career work of scholars and scientists as just another misguided opinion, ” says Dan Quinn, senior communications strategist at the Texas Freedom Network, a non-profit group that monitors public education.

<div align="center">【每日一题 073 · 2023 英语一 Text 1 第 22 题 · 观点题】</div>

**题目:**

22. What does Quinn think of Hardy?

[A] She exaggerates the existing panic.

[B] She denies the value of scientific work.

[C] She shows no concern for pre-teens.

[D] She expresses self-contradictory views.


## 生词

✅ sympathise with… 赞同……，支持……  
✅ sympathise v. 赞同，支持；同情  
✅ sector n. 部门，领域，行业  
✅ resist v. 抵制，阻挡；反抗，回击  
✅ resistance n. 抵制，阻挡；反抗；反抗力resistant adj. 抵抗的，有抵抗力的；阻止的  
✅ propose v. 提议，建议  
✅ sharply adv. 猛烈地，严厉地；急剧地  
✅ dispute v. 对……提出质询（或怀疑）；争论，辩论 n. 辩论，争端，纠纷  
✅ casually adv. 随意地，漫不经心地  
✅ dismiss v. 不予考虑，摒弃；去除，消除；解散  
✅ misguided adj. （因理解或判断失误）搞错的  
✅ senior adj. 级别（或地位）高的；高水平的 n. 较年长的人  
✅ non-profit group 非营利组织  
✅ monitor v. 监视，跟踪调查 n. 显示器；监控器

## 解题方法

**观点题就是考查某人发出的观点内容。**

**识别观点题**

**Sb. think/believe/hold/suggest/argue that…**

**According to sb.,…**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">注意提问方式的多样化，例如本题： What does Quinn think of Hardy?</span>**

**观点题的解题方法**

**1.人：作者不用找，其他人要明确找到**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">不止一方观点时，要区分发出观点的人</span>**

**2.观点细节：细节比对原文， “灵魂三问法”**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">本题是比较特别的观点题，注意出题的全新角度</span>：不是传统的提问一个人对事情的观点，而是<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">提问一个人对另一个人的观点</span>。所以在定位到原文的时候要注意，要<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">找到两个人同时所在的段落</span>，才能准确地解题。**
