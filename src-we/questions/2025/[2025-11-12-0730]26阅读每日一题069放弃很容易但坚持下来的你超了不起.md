①“Short-termism”, or the desire for quick profits, has worsened in publicly traded companies, says the Bank of England’s top economist, Andrew Haldane. ②He quotes a giant of classical economics, Alfred Marshall, in describing this financial impatience as acting like “children who pick the plums out of their pudding to eat them at once” rather than putting them aside to be eaten last.

## 解题方法

**写作目的题考查的是文章中的举例、引用或比喻等被使用的目的是为了表达什么。**

**写作目的题的常见提问方式：**

***The example of ... is used to show ________.**

***... is used to illustrate _______.**

***The quotation in Paragraph XXX explains that ________.**

***... is quoted to indicate _______**

***The “empty bottles ” metaphor illustrates that people feel a pressure to.**

***The ants analogy is used to illustrate.**

**写作目的题的解题方法：通常，先找内容，再前后找目的**

## 出题点

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">把引用内容和目的结合在一起理解，综合上下文，更利于解题。</span>先在原文中定位，通过引号可以快速找到引用的内容，然后再往前看，自然就找到了它所服务的写作目的。但要注意，不仅要看定位到的句②的前半句，还要结合上文的句①一起看。**
