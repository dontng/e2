## 真题

**【段 5】**

① There’s no doubt that our peer groups exert enormous influence on our behavior. ② An emerging body of research shows that positive health habits —as well as negative ones —spread through networks of friends via social communication. ③ This is a subtle form of peer pressure: we unconsciously imitate the behavior we see every day.

<div align="center">【每日一题 023 · 2012 英语一 Text 1 第 24 题 · 细节题】</div>

**题目:**

24. Paragraph 5 shows that our imitation of behaviors _______.

[A] is harmful to our networks of friends

[B] will mislead behavioral studies

[C] occurs without our realizing it

[D] can produce negative health habits


## 生词

✅ There’s no doubt that... 毫无疑问 ……  
✅ exert enormous influence on... 对...... 产生巨大影响  
✅ an emerging body of research 大量涌现的新研究  
✅ positive adj. 积极的，乐观的；肯定的，确信的  
✅ negative adj. 消极的，否定的；负的  
✅ social communication 社交  
✅ subtle adj. 不易察觉的；微妙的，精细的  
✅ peer pressure 同伴压力  
✅ unconsciously adv. 无意识地，不知不觉地；失去知觉地  
✅ imitate v. 模仿，效仿；复制

## 解题方法

**题干定位分为两步：初步定位→精准定位**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">题干的精准定位可以直接找到答案</span>**

**题干+正确选项是对原文的<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">同义改写</span>**

**细节题的解题方法：<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节比对原文！！！</span>**

**判断细节是否正确用<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">“灵魂三问法”：</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1.有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2.全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3.匹配否？</span>**

**（适用于细节题/推断题/观点题）**

## 出题点

**同义改写：题干的关键词our imitation of behaviors（我们对行为的模仿）是对原文we imitate the behavior（我们模仿行为）的同义改写；原文unconsciously（无意识地）是对C选项occurs without our realizing it（发生在我们没有意识到的情况下）的同义改写。**
