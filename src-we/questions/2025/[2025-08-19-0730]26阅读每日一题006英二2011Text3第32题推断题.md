## 真题

**【段 3 】**

① Economic condition was only a stimulus for the trend toward efficient living. ② The phrase “less is more ” was actually first popularized by a German, the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, who like other people associated with the Bauhaus, a school of design, emigrated to the United States before World War II and took up posts at American architecture schools. ③ These designers came to exert enormous influence on the course of American architecture, but none more so than Mies.

<div align="center">【每日一题 006 · 2011 英语二 Text 3 第 32 题 · 推断题】</div>

**题目:**

32. Which of the following can be inferred from Paragraph 3 about the Bauhaus?

[A] It was founded by Ludwig Mies van der Rohe.

[B] Its designing concept was affected by World War II.

[C] Most American architects used to be associated with it.

[D] It had a great influence upon American architecture.


## 生词

✅ economic condition 经济状况  
✅ stimulus n. 刺激，刺激物；激励，鼓舞  
✅ efficient living 高效生活  
✅ popularize v. 使受欢迎, 普及, 使流行  
✅ architect n. 建筑师  
✅ architecture n. 建筑学；建筑风格；体系结构  
✅ (be) associated with... 与……有关  
✅ emigrate v. 移民 ( 移出 ), 移居国外  
✅ immigrate v. 移民 ( 移入 ), 使移居入境  
✅ take up posts 就职  
✅ exert enormous influence on... 对……施加巨大影响  
✅ exert v. 运用，发挥；施以影响  
✅ 长难句：  
✅ 【段 3 】② The phrase “ less is more ” was actually first popularized by a German, the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, who like other people associated with the Bauhaus, a school of design, emigrated to the United States before World War II and took up posts at American architecture schools.

## 解题方法

**考研阅读的解题原则：<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">择优录取</span>**

**审题干的两大目标：一是找题干的定位信息（段落/名词/数字/引号）；二是确定题目类型。**

**推断题的判断： be inferred/learned/indicated**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">推断题千万不要推断！！！解题方法同细节题。</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">细节比对原文，运用“灵魂三问法”：</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1)有否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2)全否？</span>**

**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">3)匹配否？</span>**

## 易错点

**注意A选项，“Bauhaus被Ludwig所创建”，不等于原文中所说的“像其他人一样与Bauhaus有关”，<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">有关≠创建，偷换概念</span>。**

**注意C选项，最易错。原文说“他和其他人一样，与Bauhaus有关”，但选项说的是“大多数美国建筑师过去都曾与Bauhaus有关（现在无关了）”，与原文不符。<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">其他人≠大多数美国建筑师</span>，并且也看不出来过去有关但现在无关，<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">偷换概念</span>。**

**D选项从逻辑上来讲，也是有点小瑕疵的，并不是完美的选项，但本着“择优录取”的原则，其他三个选项的错误更大更明显，因此选择D。**
