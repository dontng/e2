## 原句

> Orin Kerr, a law professor, compares the explosion and accessibility of digital information in the 21st century with the establishment of automobile use as a virtual necessity of life in the 20th: The justices had to specify novel rules for the new personal domain of the passenger car then; they must sort out how the Fourth Amendment applies to digital information now.

**思考题:**

冒号之前的句子的核心内容是什么？余下非核心的扩展内容分别表示什么？

## 分析长难句前的准备工作

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">0 找谓语动词：</span></strong> compares / had to specify / must sort / applies

（4个谓语动词，意味着4件事，应该断开为4段）

## 分析句子

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">1 断开：</span></strong>通过冒号/分号和连词how来断开句子。

- 【句1】主句1：Orin Kerr, a law professor, compares the explosion and accessibility of digital information in the 21st century with the establishment of automobile use as a virtual necessity of life in the 20th:
- 【句2】主句2，补充说明句1：The justices had to specify novel rules for the new personal domain of the passenger car then;
- 【句3】并列句，与句2并列：they must sort out
- 【句4】宾语从句：how the Fourth Amendment applies to digital information now.

<strong><span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">2 简化主句1：</span></strong>先去扩展，找核心；然后看懂核心后，再把扩展的细节加回来看。

- 【句1】主句1：<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">Orin Kerr</span><s>, a law professor,</s> <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">compares</span> <s>the</s> <span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">explosion and accessibility</span> <s>of digital information</s> <s>in the 21st century</s> <s>with the establishment of automobile use</s> <s>as a virtual necessity of life in the 20th</s><s>:</s>

  **简化后的核心内容：**<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">Orin Kerr</span>…<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">compares</span>…<span style="color: rgb(239, 18, 18);">explosion and accessibility</span>…

## 参考译文

法学教授Orin Kerr把“21世纪数字信息爆炸及其可获取性”与“20世纪汽车确立为生活必需“相较：当时法官们不得不为小轿车这一新兴私人领域明确新规；现在他们也必须解决第四修正案如何适用于数字信息的问题。
