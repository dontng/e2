## Section I Use of English (1–20, 0.5 pt each = 10 pts)

1. **[D] tricky** — 后文 `since children respond differently to the same style of parenting` 说明界定好父母很难、很棘手。
2. **[B] for example** — `than, for example, a younger one` 以“年龄更小的孩子”作举例对比。
3. **[A] Fortunately** — 与前段“难以界定”形成转折宽慰，“幸运的是，另一种父母更容易描述”。
4. **[C] describe** — 呼应第一段 `defining what it means to be a good parent`，此处为 `easier to describe`。
5. **[A] while** — 让步关系，“尽管每位父母都想有耐心，但这并非易事”。
6. **[B] task** — 固定搭配 `no easy task`（并非易事）。
7. **[A] tolerant** — 呼应上文 `patient parenting`，指疲劳时难以保持“宽容的”风格。
8. **[C] push** — 固定搭配 `push sb. too far`（把某人逼得太紧/太过分）。
9. **[D] inevitable** — `the inevitable happens`（不可避免的事发生了），即下文失去耐心。
10. **[C] harsh** — 失去耐心时大喊大叫或说出“过于严厉/刺耳的”话。
11. **[A] turn back** — 固定搭配 `turn back the clock`（时光倒流）。
12. **[C] However** — 前句说“我们都经历过”，本句转折强调“尽管常见，但仍须牢记后果”。
13. **[D] regret** — 一时疲惫说出的话可能让你“后悔”很久。
14. **[B] affect** — 与 `do damage to your relationship` 并列，负面“影响”孩子的自尊。
15. **[D] cool** — 固定搭配 `lose one's cool`（发脾气、失去冷静）。
16. **[C] importance** — 后文 `one of the most significant of all life's skills` 提示人们越来越意识到榜样的“重要性”。
17. **[D] confronted** — `when confronted by stress`（当面对压力时）。
18. **[B] hard** — 与下句 `A more practical goal` 对应，时刻保持耐心是“困难的”。
19. **[A] trying** — `trying situations`（令人难受/考验人的处境），呼应下句 `stressful moments`。
20. **[B] emerge** — `emerge from stressful moments feeling better`（从压力时刻中走出来，身心感觉更好）。

## Section II Reading Comprehension

### Part A (21–40, 2 pts each = 40 pts)

#### Text 1
21. **[A] pick up social signals from non-living rats**
   - 原文定位（Para. 1）：`tested whether rats can detect social signals from robotic rats`（`pick up` 对应 `detect`，`non-living rats` 对应 `robotic rats`）。
   - 排除：B 为真鼠日常行为非实验目的；C 未提及训练社交特征；D 未提及发出警告信号。
22. **[D] It moved around alone.**
   - 原文定位（Para. 2）：`Meanwhile, the asocial robot simply moved forwards and backwards and side to side.`
   - 排除：A、B、C 均为 `social robot rat` 的行为。
23. **[B] expected it to do the same in return**
   - 原文定位（Para. 3）：`wanting the robot to return the favour when they get trapped, says Quinn`（`do the same in return` 对应 `return the favour`）。
   - 排除：A（练习逃生）、C（展示智力）、D（有趣游戏）均无依据。
24. **[C] respond more to actions than to looks**
   - 原文定位（Para. 5）：`We'd assumed we'd have to give it a moving head and tail, facial features, and put a scent on it... but that wasn't necessary`，说明外形与气味不重要，大鼠回应的是互动行为。
   - 排除：A 与原文相反；B 未比较气味与体型；D 与 `befriend the social robot` 相反。
25. **[D] are more sensitive to social cues than expected**
   - 原文定位（Para. 5–6）：`surprising given its minimal design` 及 `how sensitive rats are to social cues, even when they come from basic robots`。
   - 排除：A 非重点；B 未与其他动物比较活跃度；C 原文说 `Similarly`，与儿童行为相似而非不同。

#### Text 2
26. **[C] Increased business opportunities for top firms.**
   - 原文定位（Para. 2）：`The best model for understanding the growth of CEO pay is that of limited CEO talent in a world where business opportunities for the top firms are growing rapidly.`
   - 排除：A（公司数量）、B（整体经济普涨）、D（主要经济体合作）均未提及。
27. **[D] operate more globalized companies**
   - 原文定位（Para. 3）：`large American companies are much more globalized than ever before, with supply chains spread across a larger number of countries. To lead in that system requires knowledge...`
   - 排除：A 未提及；B 原文指公司自身成为带研发的科技公司，非 CEO 个人出资研发；C 原文是公司本身成为科技公司，非与外部建立联系。
28. **[B] strict corporate governance**
   - 原文定位（Para. 4）：`corporate governance has become a lot tighter and more rigorous since the 1970s. Yet it is principally during this period of stronger governance that CEO pay has been high and rising.`
   - 排除：A、C、D 原文均未提及。
29. **[D] increase corporate value**
   - 原文定位（Para. 4）：`those practices build up corporate value not just for the CEO.`
   - 排除：A、C 未以此作为合理性依据；B 原文说最高薪酬给的是 `outside candidates` 而非内部人。
30. **[A] CEOs Are Not Overpaid**
   - 主旨定位：全文反驳“CEO 拿高薪是盘剥他人”的常见误解，从人才稀缺、业务难度、严格治理和提升公司价值角度论证高薪合理。
   - 排除：B 过于泛化且未体现作者立场；C 仅涵盖第3段；D 偏离核心论点。

#### Text 3
31. **[D] Its fate is yet to be decided.**
   - 原文定位（Para. 1）：`But with legal battles ahead, the zone's future looks uncertain at best.`
   - 排除：A 原文明确说 `despite its success in improving air quality`；B 法官推翻的是“停止罚款的决定”，即支持限行区；C 原文未提需要更强执法。
32. **[D] They put the burden on individual motorists.**
   - 原文定位（Para. 3）：`they inevitably put the costs of cleaning the air on to individual drivers—who must pay fees or buy better vehicles—rather than on to the car manufacturers...`
   - 排除：A 与原文相反；B 原文肯定其带来一定空气改善；C 原文说的是 `politically controversial`，而非过温和。
33. **[A] arouse strong resistance**
   - 原文定位（Para. 3）：`it is sure to spark intense opposition from the far larger number of motorists who will then be affected.`
   - 排除：B 原文仅称会成为大选重要议题，未说确保胜选；C、D 未提及。
34. **[D] National governments.**
   - 原文定位（Para. 5）：`They are acting because national governments—Britain's and others across Europe—have failed to do so.`
   - 排除：A 未提及；B、C 是目前代为出手但能力有限的地方官员，而非本应统筹解决的主体。
35. **[B] should be forced to follow regulations**
   - 原文定位（Para. 5）：`require auto companies to bring their vehicles into compliance` 及 `We're doing everything but insist that manufacturers clean up their cars.`
   - 排除：A、C 为无依据预测；D 原文强调执行现有法规，非单纯公众监督。

#### Text 4
36. **[D] are drawing growing public attention**
   - 原文定位（Para. 1）：`Now that members of Generation Z are graduating college this spring... the attention has been rising steadily in recent weeks.`
   - 排除：A、B 未提及（第2段反而是焦虑）；C `inhabit those empty office cubicles` 仅描述进入职场，非偏好。
37. **[C] what a tough economic situation is like**（注：原 OCR 文本第 37 题误印为 `36.`）
   - 原文定位（Para. 2）：`Gen Zs know what an economic train wreck looks like. They were impressionable kids during the crash of 2008...`
   - 排除：A、B 未提及；D 是公众想知道的问题，非 Z 世代自身认知焦点。
38. **[D] relieve**（注：原 OCR 文本第 38 题误印为 `37.`）
   - 原文定位（Para. 2）：`The booming economy seems to have done little to assuage this underlying generational sense of anxious urgency`，意为“缓解、减轻”焦虑感。
   - 排除：A（加深）、B（界定）、C（维持）均不符语境。
39. **[B] have a clear idea about their future jobs**（注：原 OCR 文本第 39 题误印为 `38.`）
   - 原文定位（Para. 3）：`88 percent of graduating seniors this year chose their major with a job in mind`，且对雇主特质与职业目标排序清晰。
   - 排除：A 职业培训次于稳定就业；C 未提及；D 工作与生活平衡是第一目标，未说难以实现。
40. **[B] less adventurous**
   - 原文定位（Para. 4）：`Millennials wanted more flexibility in their lives... Generation Zs are looking for more certainty and stability... and they are quite risk averse.`（厌恶风险即更不爱冒险）。
   - 排除：A 与第2段 `clear-eyed, economic pragmatists` 相反；C、D 未提及。

## Section III Translation (46, 15 pts)

### 参考译文

人的一生几乎不可能不经历某种失败。但失败的美妙之处在于，如何看待它完全取决于我们自己。

我们可以选择将失败视为“世界末日”，也可以把它看作它本就常常是的那种极佳的学习经历。每次在某件事上失败时，我们都可以选择去寻找我们应当从中汲取的教训。这些教训至关重要；它们正是我们成长的方式，也是我们避免重蹈覆辙的途径。只有当我们任由失败阻挡自己时，它才会让我们停下脚步。

失败还能让我们认识到关于自己的一些事——若非如此，我们原本永远不会了解。例如，失败能帮助你发现自己是一个多么坚强的人；在某件事上遭遇失败，也能帮你看清谁是你最真正的朋友，或者帮你找到意想不到的成功动力。

### 严格自评（14.0 / 15.0）
- 准确完整还原三段全部信息，无漏译、错译；`otherwise`、`keep from making that same mistake again`、`truest friends` 等关键结构处理自然贴切。

## Section IV Writing

### Part A (47, 10 pts)

Dear Friends,

I am writing to share the details of our upcoming tour to the Forbidden City this Saturday. As the imperial palace of the Ming and Qing dynasties, it boasts magnificent ancient architecture and a rich collection of historical relics, offering a vivid glimpse into traditional Chinese culture.

To ensure a smooth and enjoyable visit, please keep a few tips in mind. First, remember to bring your passport for identity verification at the entrance. Second, wear comfortable walking shoes, as the complex is vast and requires plenty of walking. Finally, since it can get quite sunny, bringing a bottle of water and sun protection is advisable.

We will meet at the south gate at 8:30 a.m. Looking forward to seeing you there!

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

### Part B (48, 15 pts)

The pie chart illustrates the findings of a survey regarding the purposes of mobile phone reading among students at a certain university. Notably, learning knowledge accounts for the largest proportion at 59.5%, far exceeding all other categories. Following that, killing time makes up 21.3%, while obtaining information represents 17.0%. The remaining 2.2% falls under other purposes.

Several factors contribute to this striking phenomenon. First and foremost, with the rapid advancement of digital technology and educational apps, smartphones have become portable libraries, enabling undergraduates to access academic resources and online courses anytime and anywhere. Furthermore, the fast-paced campus life makes fragmented time inevitable, so it is natural that over one-fifth of respondents use mobile reading for relaxation, while others rely on it to stay informed about current affairs.

In summary, mobile reading has evolved into a mainstream and predominantly constructive habit among college students. While it is encouraging that nearly three-fifths read to acquire knowledge, students should also be mindful of balancing screen time and selecting high-quality content so as to maximize the benefits of digital reading.

### 严格自评（13.0 / 15.0）
- 准确解读饼图全部四项数据（学习知识 59.5%、消磨时间 21.3%、获取信息 17.0%、其他 2.2%），原因分析合理、结尾辩证，约 174 词，符合高分档标准。
