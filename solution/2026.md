## Section I Use of English (1–20, 0.5 pt each = 10 pts)

1. **C. proud** — 与前面的 `strive for self-respect` 和 `see ourselves in a positive light` 构成并列，表示“为自己是谁感到自豪”。
2. **B. natural** — 承接上文“损害自尊的评价会令人不适”，想避开它们“是很自然的（only natural）”。
3. **A. whether** — 引导主语从句，表示“你的自尊是否（whether）真的在受损，很大程度上是主观的”。
4. **C. perception** — 对应下段 `interpreting these situations`，指“你对他人言行的理解/看法（perception）”。
5. **A. poor** — 老板挑销售报告的毛病，你觉得这意味着她认为你是“不称职的/糟糕的（poor）”员工。
6. **D. planned** — 与 `cancel` 搭配，取消“原定计划的（planned）”酒吧聚会。
7. **B. term** — 后接冒号引出心理学术语 `personalisation`，故用 `term`（术语）。
8. **D. reality** — `But the reality is that...`（但事实是……），与上一段的负面主观臆测形成转折。
9. **C. mistakes** — 对应上文 `picks holes in your sales report` 和下文 `point these out so that you do better next time`，指报告里犯了一些“错误（mistakes）”。
10. **A. Similarly** — 并列举第二个例子（朋友取消聚会），与老板的例子构成类比，“同样地（Similarly）”。
11. **B. changed** — 固定搭配 `changed their minds`（改变了主意）。
12. **D. examples** — `In both examples`，回指前文提到的“老板”和“朋友”两个例子。
13. **A. process** — 指心理学中另一种正在起作用的认知“过程（process）”，即“读心术（mind reading）”。
14. **D. asking** — 如果不直接“问（asking）”别人，就不可能真正知道他们怎么想。
15. **C. engaging in** — `avoid engaging in personalization and mind reading`（避免陷入/进行个人化和读心术）。
16. **A. happened** — `what's happened`（= what has happened），指“对所发生事情的负面假设”。
17. **D. fundamental** — 与 `deep` 并列，指不至于说出关于你本质的、根本性的（fundamental）评价。
18. **B. feedback** — 老板花时间指出报告问题，即给你“反馈（feedback）”。
19. **C. improve** — 对应上文 `so that you do better next time`，表示帮助你“进步/改进（improve）”。
20. **B. strong** — 积极重释：也许正因为你们的友谊足够“牢固（strong）”，他们才敢于临时取消约定。

## Section II Reading Comprehension

### Part A (21–40, 2 pts each = 40 pts)

#### Text 1 (English Public Libraries)

21. **D. Look into their current situation.**
    - **文本依据**：第2段 `when the culture department commissioned me to conduct an independent review of English public libraries, published yesterday. Over nine months, as I visited libraries up and down the country...`（受文化部委托对英格兰公共图书馆进行独立调查）。
    - **排除项**：A（记录传统）、B（制定扩张计划）、C（收集读者意见）均非受托核心任务。

22. **C. perform diversified functions**
    - **文本依据**：第3、4段指出图书馆除书籍外还提供 `a multitude of other services`（就业指导、语言课、数字支持、商业与知识产权中心、基础健康检查、心理健康小组、编程俱乐部、免费借足球等），即“发挥多样化的功能”。
    - **排除项**：A（藏书丰富非3、4段核心发现）、B（未提及访客记录）、D（服务对象涵盖企业主、男性、儿童等，并非主要吸引年轻读者）。

23. **A. be given greater attention**
    - **文本依据**：第5段 `Yet still our libraries are often overlooked and underappreciated. There is an overall decline in visits...`（图书馆常被忽视和低估），由此推出它们应当得到更多关注。
    - **排除项**：B（访客本就在下降，无需限流）、C（原文仅对比没有其他公私机构能做到这一点，未建议与私企合作）、D（未提及设备升级）。

24. **A. the crisis faced by libraries**
    - **文本依据**：第6段列出2010年以来关闭图书馆的统计数据（全英近800家，英格兰官方记录230家，并称 `230 libraries too many`），直接体现图书馆面临的危机。
    - **排除项**：B（进步）、C（贡献）、D（自身缺陷）均与关停数据不符。

25. **C. Improving user convenience.**
    - **文本依据**：末段建议 `enable members to use their card in any library in the country, and automatic memberships for children`（全国一卡通用及儿童自动成为会员），核心是提升使用便利性。
    - **排除项**：A（未提公众反馈）、B（末段未提筹资）、D（儿童自动会员不等同于终身会员）。

#### Text 2 (AI in the Workplace)

26. **B. give them emotional support**
    - **文本依据**：第1段 `talk to AI like a friend... Our data shows that engaging with AI like this can leave us feeling heard and less isolated`（让人感到被倾听、不再孤单），即提供情感支持。
    - **排除项**：A（促进职业发展）、C（保持动力）、D（改善职场沟通）在第一段均无依据。

27. **A. pose a threat to businesses**
    - **文本依据**：第2段 `For businesses, the implications are worrying. Consider Microsoft Copilot for example... any sensitive information could potentially be exposed to the world.`（对企业而言影响令人担忧，敏感信息可能暴露），说明AI的使用可能对企业构成威胁。
    - **排除项**：B（产生多余数据未提）、C（促进合作未提）、D（“分享给第三方”是数据泄露风险，而非鼓励信息共享）。

28. **C. its significant role in their work**
    - **文本依据**：第3段 `around 63% of them report that using AI increases their productivity, and some even feel AI offers more help than their human colleagues.`（提升生产力、比人类同事帮助更大），说明AI在工作中发挥了重要作用。
    - **排除项**：A（易于获取）、B（想保持消息灵通）、D（与他人竞争）均未在第3段提及。

29. **D. adjust their management to AI development**
    - **文本依据**：第5段 `By instilling best practices in AI engagement and creating policies that evolve with the technology, businesses can positively shift the AI landscape.`（制定随技术演进的政策），即随AI发展调整管理。
    - **排除项**：A（数据来源透明度）、B（优先AI内容质量）、C（将员工观点纳入规则）均非第5段内容。

30. **A. have a limited understanding of it**
    - **文本依据**：末段（第6段）指出 `as long as we’re equipped with the knowledge and tools to make sure AI remains a friend. And this is where the challenge lies. There are so many products out there, being promoted with huge advertising and marketing budgets, that it’s easy to fall victim.` 挑战在于人们缺乏相应的知识与工具（结合前文第1、4段提及许多人不清楚AI隐私与版权归属），即对AI了解有限，从而容易在铺天盖地的营销中成为受害者。
    - **排除项**：B（低估经济成本未提）、C（过度强调其力量未提）、D（过度接触AI属于偷换“产品营销多”的概念）。

#### Text 3 (Italy's High-Speed Rail Network)

31. **B. is a popular option for travelling**
    - **文本依据**：第1段 `trains have become the preferred means of travel across Italy for locals and tourists alike... In many cases, it is the best option`，说明高铁是受欢迎的出行选择。
    - **排除项**：A（被游客视为过时与原文相反）、C（本地人也首选高铁）、D（第2段指出因大量维修工程导致严重延误，并非良好维护的典范）。

32. **A. the common occurrence of train delays**
    - **文本依据**：第3段先说 `Disruptions have become increasingly frequent`，随后引用Ferrovie的数据 `23% of all high-speed trains it operated were late in 2023`，以证明列车延误很常见。
    - **排除项**：B（铁路网价值）、C（媒体广泛报道不是引用23%数据的目的）、D（与23%晚点率相反）。

33. **C. Its limited capacity.**
    - **文本依据**：第5段 `But lack of capacity is another problem... there’s no spare capacity`，明确指出运力不足是造成中断的原因之一。
    - **排除项**：A（原文是 `the situation becomes highly complex`，非时刻表复杂）、B（原文有240亿和1240亿欧元巨额投资计划）、D（未提及规模过大）。

34. **D. puts the high-speed network in trouble**
    - **文本依据**：第5段解释 `mixed system` 指高铁必须在部分路段或大城市使用普通轨道，`If there’s congestion or, for example, a local train breaks down, the entire high-speed network is affected.`（一旦拥堵或慢车故障，整个高铁网络都会受影响）。
    - **排除项**：A（提供更多路线选择）、B（需要额外运营支出）、C（提升舒适度）均未提及。

35. **B. shortening high-speed train intervals**
    - **文本依据**：末段 `It will allow for a reduction of the distance between high-speed trains travelling on the same line at any given time`（缩短同一线路上同时运行的高铁列车之间的距离/间隔）。
    - **排除项**：A（原文是将高铁线与普通线分离，非重建普通线）、C（建地下铁路连接线，非建更多车站）、D（未提及提升安全性）。

#### Text 4 (Chicago Street Festivals)

36. **D. was an appealing cultural gathering**
    - **文本依据**：第1段 `one of its most beloved street festivals... blossomed into a massive cultural event, welcoming tens of thousands of people each year`，说明它是一个很有吸引力的文化盛会。
    - **排除项**：A（原文是停办，非被取代）、B（未提国际知名度）、C（原文是海德公园社区领袖举办、从小型社区聚会发展而来，非在多个社区举办）。

37. **C. they have to tackle financial difficulties**
    - **文本依据**：第2段 `rising production costs and declining attendee donations... highlighting a reality that all street festival organizers face right now.` 成本飙升与捐款骤降即财务困难。
    - **排除项**：A（并非不会合理使用捐款）、B与D（安保、移动厕所等是作为成本上升的例子提及，并非无法获取设施或必须加强安保）。

38. **C. contribute to the local economy**
    - **文本依据**：第3段 `they are economic engines that directly benefit the neighborhoods they’re in and the city of Chicago as whole. Street festivals drive foot traffic to local businesses`，直接促进当地经济。
    - **排除项**：A（未提反映城市生活方式）、B（第4段明确指出 `receive no city funding`）、D（音乐节指市政府办的Chicago Jazz Festival，非街头节日以音乐秀闻名）。

39. **B. reduce the number of performances**
    - **文本依据**：第5段 `We are eliminating a stage, booking fewer performers and making additional cuts to reduce our costs`，减少舞台和演出人员即减少演出数量。
    - **排除项**：A（原文是local artists，非业余艺人）、C（与原文 `striving to keep... true to Wicker Park's unique spirit` 相反）、D（原文是small business vendors，非大企业）。

40. **B. the generosity of attendees**
    - **文本依据**：末段 `they exist because of community support. A thriving summer festival season doesn’t happen by accident; it happens when we all chip in.`（`chip in` 意为出钱/捐款支持），即依赖参与者的慷慨解囊。
    - **排除项**：A（活动多样性）、C（社区声誉）、D（支出管理）均非末段强调的决定因素。

### Part B New Type (41–45, 2 pts each = 10 pts)

41. **E. The supply of seeds is a tough problem that has to be dealt with.**
    - **匹配依据**：第1、2段提及 `developing supplies of seeds and seedlings`，随后Ramni Jamnadass指出 `"Where’s the planting material going to come from? That’s one big bottleneck"`（种植材料从哪来是一个大瓶颈），对应种子供应是个棘手难题。

42. **A. People must pay attention to the fact that some kinds of trees do not produce seeds annually.**
    - **匹配依据**：第3段Christopher Kettle指出许多树种是“大年结实”树种（`which don’t produce seeds every year`），并强调 `People need to be ready. "Otherwise, you miss the boat..."`，对应必须注意有些树并非每年结籽。

43. **G. Some creatures in the forest that multiply fast can become a great threat to forest restoration.**
    - **匹配依据**：第4段John Stanturf提到 `"Insects that today are a minor problem may become a major problem if they can produce three or four generations in a year"`（一年繁殖三四代的昆虫可能变成大问题），对应快速繁殖的森林生物会成为重大威胁。

44. **C. The purpose of restoration efforts may be different from the real needs of the people involved.**
    - **匹配依据**：第6段Pedro Brancalion指出全球恢复资金多用于应对气候变化，但巴西农民关心的是水（`No, I am concerned about water`），说明恢复工作的目标与当地人实际需求存在差异。

45. **D. Human factors, as well as trees planted, should be emphasized in forest restoration.**
    - **匹配依据**：第7段Robin Chazdon指出 `Restoration is about more than what gets planted in the ground... "Yes, it’s about forests, but it’s really about people. They are the agents of restoration."`，对应在森林恢复中除了种树，更应重视人的因素。

## Section III Translation (46, 15 pts)

穿戴对心理的影响指的是我们所穿的衣物如何影响我们的思想、感受和行为。服装不仅仅是用来遮体的，它在塑造我们的自我认知以及与他人的互动方面也发挥着重要作用。

这种影响的一个方面是自我表达。我们选择的衣物能够反映出我们的个性、情绪和身份。无论我们选择大胆、色彩鲜艳的服装，还是偏爱更为低调的风格，我们的着装选择都在传递关于我们是谁以及我们希望如何被他人看待的信息。

此外，服装还能影响我们的自信程度。当我们穿上让自己感到舒适和自信的衣服时，这会对我们的自尊心和整体情绪产生积极影响。

再者，文化和社会因素在塑造我们的着装选择方面也起着重要作用。不同的文化对于着装有着各自的规范和期望，这会影响人们所穿衣物的类型以及赋予这些衣物的意义。

## Section IV Writing

### Part A (47, 10 pts)

Dear Jack,

Thank you so much for sharing the wonderful video of your family’s trip in China! I just finished watching it, and it truly brightened my day. The breathtaking scenery, vibrant local culture, and your family’s joyful smiles made the video a real pleasure to watch. It looks like you all had an unforgettable time exploring different cities and tasting delicious Chinese food.

I would love to hear more about your journey. Which place impressed you the most, and did you encounter any interesting stories or cultural surprises along the way? Looking forward to catching up with you soon!

Best regards,
Li Ming

### Part B (48, 15 pts)

The bar chart illustrates the results of a survey on public perceptions of children’s outdoor activities. As shown in the chart, satisfying curiosity and improving observation skills are the two most widely recognized benefits, accounting for 54.6% and 54.5% respectively. By contrast, strengthening physical fitness and enhancing parent-child relationships represent smaller shares, at 37.2% and 33.2%.

This distribution reflects a growing awareness of the cognitive value of outdoor experiences. Unlike structured indoor classrooms or digital screens, the natural world offers rich, dynamic stimuli that spark children’s innate curiosity and train them to notice subtle details. At the same time, outdoor play naturally promotes physical exercise and creates shared moments that bring parents and children closer together.

In my view, these findings underscore the vital role of outdoor activities in children’s holistic development. In an era dominated by electronic devices, parents and educators should actively create opportunities for children to step outside, explore nature, and grow both intellectually and physically.
