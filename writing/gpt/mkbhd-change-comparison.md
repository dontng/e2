# MKBHD：大作文完整句子语料库

这份材料分成两层。第一层是从 12 期 MKBHD 官方视频字幕中核验出的完整原句，每条都能按时间戳回看；第二层是依据这些表达机制写成的完整训练句，用历年真题内容练书面表达。原句适合听力、跟读和复试口语，训练句适合大作文，不能把两层混成所谓“高级模板”。

## 第二层：36 个完整真题迁移句

以下句子不是 MKBHD 原话，而是把上面的变化、比较、因果和限定机制迁移到 2010—2025 年英语二真题。训练时先判断数据关系，再选择句子；不允许反过来拿句子硬套数据。

### A. 上升、下降和增速

1. `The number of mobile-phone subscriptions in developing countries rose sharply throughout the period.`
2. `The urban population increased steadily, while the rural population followed the opposite trend.`
3. `Museum visits grew at a faster rate than the number of museums themselves.`
4. `Both national and rural delivery volumes increased, but the latter recorded the more rapid growth.`
5. `Public health literacy improved markedly over the nine-year period.`
6. `The increase was modest at first and became much more pronounced later.`

### B. 比较、差距和反转

1. `Domestic brands gained market share, whereas Japanese brands experienced a noticeable decline.`
2. `Employees over fifty reported the highest level of job satisfaction among the three age groups.`
3. `The proportion of students taking part-time jobs rose with each year of study.`
4. `The gap between the urban and rural populations narrowed and eventually reversed.`
5. `Employment remained the leading destination, despite a clear rise in further study.`
6. `The largest difference between the two groups can be seen in their preferred career paths.`

### C. 构成、排名和占比

1. `New Year's gifts accounted for the largest share of Spring Festival spending.`
2. `Enjoying the scenery ranked first among the purposes of travel.`
3. `Food quality was by far the most important consideration when respondents chose a restaurant.`
4. `Acquiring information represented the most common purpose of mobile reading.`
5. `Walking was the most popular form of exercise, followed by running and gym training.`
6. `Watching television occupied a larger share of older people's leisure time than any other activity.`

### D. 不变项、例外和限定

1. `By contrast, the proportion choosing further study remained broadly stable.`
2. `All age groups expressed some degree of satisfaction, except that middle-aged employees were less positive.`
3. `The overall trend was upward, although the pace of growth varied across the period.`
4. `The two groups moved in the same direction, but not to the same extent.`
5. `For most respondents, practical considerations appeared to outweigh social ones.`
6. `The chart reveals a clear preference, but it does not establish a single cause.`

### E. 原因、结果和机制

1. `The expansion of digital infrastructure may help explain the rapid growth of rural deliveries.`
2. `Greater health awareness is likely to have encouraged more people to exercise regularly.`
3. `The convenience of mobile devices may account for the popularity of reading for information.`
4. `As museums became more accessible, they attracted a growing number of visitors.`
5. `The preference for walking may reflect its low cost and lack of equipment requirements.`
6. `Several factors, rather than price alone, appear to shape people's choice of restaurant.`

### F. 优势、代价和评价

1. `Online exercise offers greater flexibility, but it provides fewer opportunities for face-to-face interaction.`
2. `Mobile reading makes information easier to obtain, although it may also encourage fragmented attention.`
3. `Part-time work provides practical experience, but excessive working hours may interfere with study.`
4. `Convenience appears to be the principal advantage of delivery services, while environmental pressure remains a concern.`
5. `Labor courses teach practical skills and may also strengthen students' sense of responsibility.`
6. `Outdoor activities benefit children's physical health while giving them more direct contact with nature.`

## 三种训练，分别服务作文和复试

第一种是跟读：一次选同一栏的三个 MKBHD 原句，回到时间戳听三遍，再关字幕复述。目标是掌握自然重音和比较关系，不要求逐词背诵。第二种是改写：把口语程度词换成书面语，并明确比较维度，例如把 `way better` 改成 `substantially higher in terms of...`。第三种是真题迁移：随机抽一张图，写总览句、关键比较句和可能原因句各一句，再用同样逻辑口述 45 秒。

让 AI 只检查四件事：比较基准是否一致，百分比和百分点是否混淆，程度副词是否超过数据证据，描述与原因是否分开。判断是否真正学会也很简单：面对一张新图，你先看到的是“上升、差距、例外、原因边界”等关系，而不是先想自己背过哪套句子。
