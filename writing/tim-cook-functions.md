# Tim Cook：小作文完整句子语料库

这份材料收录 Tim Cook 公开账号中的完整句子。为便于阅读，少量 `@handle`、话题标签和符号被还原为普通名称，句法关系不作改写。先读整句怎样完成交际动作，再抽取搭配；不要只背一个短语，也不要把整条推文原封不动塞进作文。

## 1. 慰问、关心与援助

| 原句 | 值得学习的地方 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|
| “Deeply saddened by the tragic events in Manchester. Our thoughts are with everyone affected.” | `saddened by` 交代事件，`thoughts are with` 把重心放到受影响者身上。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/866813724601876481) |
| “Our hearts go out to all those in Myanmar and Thailand affected by the devastating earthquake.” | 关心对象后接过去分词短语，信息紧凑而不冷漠。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1906409440128930185) |
| “Our thoughts are with our Bay Area friends and neighbors affected by wildfires. Please stay safe!” | 从慰问自然转到明确祝愿。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/917778240021909504) |
| “Our thoughts are with all those affected by Hurricane Matthew. Support relief efforts by donating to the Red Cross.” | 感受之后给出可执行行动。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/785527246408646656) |
| “Deeply saddened by the news of the shooting at the Gilroy Garlic Festival, a beloved community tradition in Northern California.” | 同位语补出事件对当地人的意义。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1155685355615469568) |
| “I pray for President Trump's rapid recovery. My thoughts are with him, the other victims and the Trump family.” | `rapid recovery` 是简短而完整的康复祝愿。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1812291847378600366) |
| “My thoughts are with the victims' families in South Carolina. Let us honor their lives by eradicating racism.” | `honor ... by doing` 把纪念落实为行动。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/612703567082688513) |
| “Our thoughts are with his family and all who loved him. Giovanni was a great man, and we are forever in his debt.” | 慰问对象具体，随后说明人物为何值得纪念。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1164190339294351360) |
| “Amid a devastating rise of COVID cases in India, our thoughts are with the medical workers, our Apple family and everyone there.” | `amid + 名词短语` 先交代背景，再列出关心对象。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1386780398609309698) |

这些句子可迁移到慰问、延期、取消访问等题目，但 2018 年道歉信仍必须明确承担责任并提出新安排；同情不能代替道歉。

## 2. 感谢、接待与共同经历

| 原句 | 值得学习的地方 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|
| “Thank you to the Apple Ginza team for the warm welcome back to Tokyo—and for all you do for our customers!” | `for A—and for B` 同时感谢眼前接待和长期贡献。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1602552493082554369) |
| “Thank you to Gallaudet University for having me today! I was awed by the spirit of community and grateful to celebrate with you in person.” | 感谢之后补一个具体印象，不让感谢悬空。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1525257856404135936) |
| “Thank you to Singapore and the teams for a fantastic visit!” | `a fantastic visit` 简洁概括共同经历。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1781252786111606869) |
| “Great visit to Apple World Trade Center! Already looking forward to my next trip to New York City.” | 回顾本次经历，同时表达再次见面的愿望。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1535070669935095809) |
| “We're looking forward to opening a new Academy in Bali to support even more developers. Thank you, Indonesia, for a great visit!” | 未来行动说明这次访问会产生什么后续。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1780576171173851225) |
| “Thank you to our customers—and the incredible teams who support them!” | 感谢主要对象，也承认幕后贡献者。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1707389354644025436) |
| “Loved spending time with the team at Apple Highland Village while I was in Houston, Texas. Thanks for a fantastic visit!” | `spend time with` 比空泛的 `visit` 更有人际温度。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/2087953141736071443) |
| “April 1st marks 50 years of Apple. Thank you to everyone who's been a part of our journey.” | 先说明纪念节点，再感谢共同参与者。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/2032079194923155547) |

这一组最直接对应 2010 年感谢接待、2016 年感谢朋友的祝贺、2017 年接受邀请、2021—2022 年会议与活动邀请。2016 年还要求提供翻译建议，感谢不能替代该项任务。值得背的是“感谢什么—留下什么印象—下一步是什么”的关系，而不是某个公司或城市名。

## 3. 祝贺、肯定与鼓励

| 原句 | 值得学习的地方 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|
| “Proud of this team and the work they do to leave our world better than we found it. Congratulations and thank you!” | 祝贺前先指出工作及其影响。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1062829608037306368) |
| “Teachers, we are inspired by the work you do every day, making a difference in the lives of future leaders and creators.” | `make a difference in` 说明贡献落在谁身上。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/993858068814249984) |
| “App Store developers have now earned over $50 billion! Congratulations on your success and such inspired creativity.” | 用成果支撑祝贺，而不是只写 `Congratulations!`。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/760929629226041345) |
| “Congratulations to the team behind The Boy, the Mole, the Fox and the Horse on their Academy Award!” | `congratulate someone on something` 的自然实例。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1635103856744091648) |
| “Team CODA created a profoundly beautiful movie, a story of hope and heart that celebrates our differences.” | 先概括作品，再用同位语说明价值。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1508308240836165634) |
| “Congratulations to the US women's national team! Awesome team and what a win! You make us all proud!” | 从结果、团队到影响，形成递进。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1147995028431626240) |
| “Congratulations to the Thai women's national volleyball team for winning gold at the Southeast Asian Games!” | `for doing` 明确祝贺原因。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1205384742784393222) |
| “Congratulations to this amazing team at the new Apple Store Puerta del Sol in Madrid.” | 祝贺对象明确，适合团队或活动场合。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/480939552748478465) |
| “Congratulations to Jen and the entire team at The Morning Show!” | `someone and the entire team` 同时照顾个人与集体。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1219218237189185537) |
| “Congratulations to Aaron Levie and the Box team on their IPO and on creating a great company!” | 两个 `on` 并列“眼前成果”和“长期建设”。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/558645473288806401) |
| “I've seen it firsthand. Congratulations to the Apple Design Team for winning this rare award.” | `firsthand` 表明肯定来自亲身了解。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1794144491609477564) |

这一组对应 2011 年祝贺录取、2013 年鼓励同学、2015 年招募志愿者。迁移时至少补出“成果来自什么”或“成果会带来什么”之一，否则仍是空话。

## 4. 回忆、纪念与影响

| 原句 | 值得学习的地方 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|
| “He showed us all the power of community. He'll be missed dearly.” | 先写人物带来的认识，再表达怀念。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1245534768495644672) |
| “Steve showed us that the future isn't something you wait for—it's something you build.” | 用一个认识概括人物影响，胜过堆形容词。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1842565404469711210) |
| “We are deeply saddened by the passing of Bill Atkinson. He was a true visionary whose creativity and groundbreaking work will forever inspire us.” | `whose` 从句具体说明何种品质会继续产生影响。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1931509329125158978) |
| “His life's work shaped our history, and his legacy inspires us to continue the march for racial equity and justice.” | `shape—legacy—inspire` 构成过去、现在和未来的时间链。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1284340274022375424) |
| “Jimmy Buffett was a great entertainer who helped us hear the music of life. He'll be sorely missed.” | 定语从句用影响解释评价，随后简短收束。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1698015266456653994) |
| “Our thoughts are with her family, her loved ones and fans everywhere.” | 慰问对象从家人扩展到所有受影响者。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1030096700428275713) |
| “Today, Steve was awarded the Medal of Freedom, America's highest civilian honor. We will continue building on his legacy.” | 同位语解释荣誉，下一句落到延续行动。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1545128746415316992) |

这一组更适合复试人物题，也能帮助小作文介绍一本书、一次展览或一个历史景点：不要只说 `meaningful`，要说它让人理解了什么、留下了什么影响。

## 5. 邀请、期待与共同目标

| 原句 | 值得学习的地方 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|
| “Looking forward to this morning's event. Hope you can join us.” | 一句表达期待，一句发出邀请，短而完整。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/522753554587471872) |
| “Happy birthday, Apple! Celebrating 40 years and looking forward to many more decades of innovation.” | 回顾节点后自然转向未来。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/716086686342455300) |
| “We're looking forward to continuing to grow and invest across Japan.” | `look forward to doing` 后接持续行动。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1603322309368250368) |
| “Anything is possible when people come together with a shared vision.” | 用共同目标说明参与的价值。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1153708666584891392) |
| “Looking forward to working with Governor Reynolds and the City of Waukee on Apple's newest data center.” | `work with ... on ...` 同时交代合作对象与事项。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/900806597395578880) |
| “Good morning, New York! We're looking forward to an exciting day at the Brooklyn Academy of Music. See you in a few hours!” | 地点、活动和见面时间都进入邀请语境。 | [原文](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/1057238584456105984) |

这一组对应 2013、2015、2021、2022、2024、2025 年的倡议、招募、会议、调研和邀请。共同价值只写一句，随后必须给出题干要求的时间、活动、任务或反馈方式。

## 训练方式：每次只处理四个完整句子

1. 圈出每句的对象：谁感谢谁、谁受到影响、谁采取行动。
2. 标出事实和态度的边界，避免用漂亮情感句替代题干信息。
3. 合上原文，为同一场景复述一句；允许换词，但要保留交际动作。
4. 随机抽一题小作文，把其中一句迁移到开头或结尾，再补齐两个任务要点。

判断是否学会：换掉人名、城市和事件以后，你仍能根据关系和场合重新写出一句，而不是只能回忆原推文。
