# Day 75 Drill · 2026-07-09

## 目标

介词短语合读。看到 `to`、`of`、`at`、`from`、`into`、`in which` 时，先判断它们带走的信息块和整体方向，再决定中文怎么落地。重点不是认识每个小词，而是避免把本来成块的信息拆散。

## 训练材料

1. Critics argue that high prices restrict **access to basic medical tests**.

   理解方向：`access to basic medical tests` 是“获得基础医学检测的机会”。`access to` 后面接的是资源，不是动作；不能翻成“进行测试”。

2. Students **graduating into a weak job market** often accept positions below their qualifications.

   理解方向：`graduating into ...` 是“毕业时进入某种环境”。这里不是毕业之后的普通时间背景，而是一出来就撞进疲软就业市场。

3. Local officials were concerned **at the prospect of young doctors leaving for larger cities**.

   理解方向：`at the prospect of ...` 是“对……可能发生感到担忧”。`young doctors leaving for larger cities` 是担忧对象，重点在“年轻医生可能流向大城市”。

4. The article traces the movement of skilled workers **from rural hospitals to private clinics and to foreign universities**.

   理解方向：`from A to B and to C` 是路径。先画线：农村医院 → 私立诊所 → 外国大学；不要把后面的地点拆成散乱背景。

5. The company built a system **in which a single patent could block several competing tests**.

   理解方向：`in which` 回指 `system`。整句是“在这个系统里，一项专利可能阻挡几种竞争性检测”，不是悬空的“在其中”。

6. The debate centered on the **departure of the best-trained nurses to richer countries**.

   理解方向：`departure of A to B` 是“A 离开并流向 B”。`the best-trained nurses` 是离开的人，`to richer countries` 是去向。

7. Researchers found little evidence **of a direct link between screen time and lower test scores**.

   理解方向：`evidence of a direct link` 是“存在直接联系的证据”。`between A and B` 把联系的两端补完整：屏幕时间和更低考试分数。

8. The policy shifted responsibility **from national agencies to local schools**.

   理解方向：`from A to B` 是责任转移路径。不是两个机构并列出现，而是责任从国家机构转到地方学校。

9. Parents objected **to the use of student data for commercial advertising**.

   理解方向：`objected to ...` 是“反对……”。后面的 `the use of student data for commercial advertising` 是完整反对对象：把学生数据用于商业广告。

10. The report warned of barriers **to treatment for patients with rare diseases**.

   理解方向：`barriers to treatment` 是“获得治疗的障碍”。`for patients with rare diseases` 补出受影响对象：罕见病患者。
