# Day 78 Structure Drill · 2026-07-13

## 目标

训练一个反射：看到 `抽象名词 + that + 完整句子` 时，先把 `that` 读成“这个名词的内容是……”，同时保留主句谓语槽位。不要一看到后面的内容就把开头名词提前封口成“如下”。

## 训练材料

1. The principle **that local officials must follow federal rules** is widely accepted.

   理解方向：`that local officials must follow federal rules` 是 `principle` 的内容。先保留槽位“这个 principle 怎么样”，再接 `is widely accepted`：这一原则被广泛接受。

2. The claim **that online comments reflect public opinion** remains difficult to prove.

   理解方向：`that` 后面是完整句子，说明 `claim` 的内容。不要把前半句译成“主张如下”；主句落点是 `remains difficult to prove`。

3. The assumption **that children learn best in silence** has shaped classroom design for decades.

   理解方向：`assumption` 后的 `that` 是内容从句。先问“这个 assumption 最后怎么样”，答案是 `has shaped classroom design`。

4. The fact **that a policy sounds simple** does not mean that it will be easy to carry out.

   理解方向：第一个 `that` 说明 `fact` 的内容；整块 `The fact that...` 作主语。真正谓语是 `does not mean`，不要在 `simple` 后提前收句。

5. The belief **that talent alone guarantees success** can make people ignore training and timing.

   理解方向：`belief` 的内容是“仅靠天赋就能保证成功”。主句谓语是 `can make`，读到 `belief that...` 时要留住“这个 belief 会怎样”的槽位。
