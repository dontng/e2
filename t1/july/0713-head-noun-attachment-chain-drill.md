# Day 78 Head Noun Chain Drill · 2026-07-13

## 目标

训练一个反射：一个头名词后面连续出现 `that / which / -ing / -ed / 介词短语 / 逗号补充` 时，先判断每个解释块挂在哪个名词上，再决定中文顺序。不要把多个后置内容一路顺译到崩溃，也不要在主句谓语出现前放弃主线。

## 训练材料

1. The idea **that schools should reward effort**, **which many parents support**, has changed classroom policy.

   理解方向：`that` 说明 `idea` 的内容，`which` 回指这个 idea。先合成“学校应奖励努力这一观点”，再接主句谓语 `has changed`。

2. The claim **that social media reflects public opinion**, **often repeated in political debates**, remains uncertain.

   理解方向：`that` 是 `claim` 的内容，`often repeated...` 继续修饰这个 claim。主句落点是 `remains uncertain`，不是 `reflects`。

3. The assumption **that children learn best alone**, **based on a narrow reading of early studies**, has been questioned.

   理解方向：`that` 说明 `assumption`，`based on...` 给出这一 assumption 的依据。最后 `has been questioned` 才是这个 assumption 的命运。

4. The belief **that talent alone explains success**, **a belief encouraged by popular stories**, can hide the role of practice.

   理解方向：逗号后的 `a belief...` 是同位补充，仍然回到前面的 `belief`。中文可拆句：这种信念常被流行故事强化，并会遮蔽练习的作用。

5. The distinction **between rules that protect fairness and rules that merely preserve tradition** is easy to miss.

   理解方向：头名词是 `distinction`，后面两个 `rules that...` 是它区分的两类对象。主句谓语是 `is easy to miss`。

6. The evidence **that the policy failed in small towns**, **gathered over several years**, forced officials to reconsider it.

   理解方向：`that` 说明 `evidence` 的内容，`gathered...` 修饰 evidence。真正动作是 `forced officials to reconsider it`。

7. The warning **that costs would rise**, **which critics dismissed at the time**, proved accurate.

   理解方向：`that` 是 warning 的内容，`which` 回指 warning 或这条警告内容。主句封口是 `proved accurate`。

8. The view **that language shapes thought**, **shared by many writers but challenged by some scientists**, remains influential.

   理解方向：`that` 给出 view 的内容，`shared... but challenged...` 是对 view 的补充状态。最后 `remains influential` 才落主线。

9. The question **whether courts should defer to agencies**, **central to many regulatory disputes**, has returned to public debate.

   理解方向：`whether...` 是 question 的内容，`central to...` 修饰 question。主句谓语是 `has returned`。

10. The principle **that federal law takes priority**, **which appears simple in theory but difficult in practice**, shapes many disputes.

   理解方向：`that` 是 principle 的内容，`which` 回指这一 principle。最后 `shapes many disputes` 是主句动作。
