## 原句

> In his Case Study House, Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life—few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers—but his belief that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable was widely shared.

## 我的理解和翻译

在他的案例研究屋里，RR可能误判了mechanical革命会如何影响每天生活，少量的美国家庭需要直升机飞行员，而大部分有的是衣服烘干机，但他相信，个人满足是同时有希望的和普遍共享的难以置信

## 批改

```
在他的案例研究屋里，RR可能误判了mechanical革命会如何影响每天生活，
少量的美国家庭需要直升机飞行员[1]，而大部分有的是衣服烘干机，
但他相信，个人满足[2]是同时有希望的[3]和普遍共享的[4]难以置信
```

[1] **`few` 极性反 + `acquired` 词义错 + `helicopters` 理解偏**
三个问题叠在一起。`few` 是负极性词，意思是"几乎没有"，不是"少量"——这是考研翻译的经典陷阱，`few` ≠ `a few`。`acquired` 不是"需要"，是"获得/购置"。`helicopters` 是直升机，不是"直升机飞行员"。整句的逻辑是：当时 Rapson 预测机械革命会彻底改变家庭生活，比如家家都会有直升机——结果没发生（few families acquired helicopters）。破折号里这两句是举例说明他预测出了偏差。

[2] **`self-sufficiency` 词义偏**
`self` = 自己，`sufficiency` = 足够/满足需求，合起来是"自给自足"，不是"个人满足"。概念是经济或生活方式上不依赖外部——这是当时 Rapson 设计理念的核心。

[3] **`desirable` 极性/词义错**
`desirable` 来自 `desire`（渴望），意思是"令人向往的、值得追求的"，不是"有希望的"。`hopeful` 才是"有希望的"。

[4] **后半段主干读错，导致整句崩溃**
`but his belief that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable was widely shared` 这句结构是：`his belief` 是主语，`that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable` 是 belief 的同位语从句（说明这个信念的内容），`was widely shared` 是谓语。学生把 `desirable and inevitable` 误读为和 `widely shared` 并列，主干就断了。读到 `his belief that...` 时，要预期后面还有一个谓语是说"这个 belief 本身"的。

---

**参考译文：**

在他的案例研究屋中，拉尔夫·拉普森或许误判了机械革命将如何影响日常生活——极少有美国家庭购置了直升机，尽管大多数家庭最终都添置了烘干机——但他关于"自给自足既令人向往、又不可避免"的信念却得到了广泛认同。

## Vocab

**`few`** *(adj./pron.)*

`few` 字面来自古英语 *fēawe*（稀少），**不含任何"有一些"的意思**。和 `a few`（有几个）是两个不同的词，`few` 自带否定。

- Few survivors were found after the avalanche swept through the valley, and rescue teams began scaling back operations by nightfall.
- Few economists predicted that inflation would persist for so long after the rate hikes began, leaving policymakers with few credible options.
- A few students stayed after class to ask questions, but few of them actually read the assigned chapters beforehand.

这次错在把 `few` 当正面量词"少量"，但 `few` = 几乎没有，是负极性词；`a few` 才是"有几个"——这一个字母的差别在翻译题里直接反转句义。

---

**`acquire`** *(v.)*

词根：拉丁语 *acquirere* = *ad-*（向）+ *quaerere*（寻求）。主动找过去、拿到手——不是被动收到，是自己去争取或购置的。

- She acquired a working knowledge of Mandarin during her three years posted in Shanghai, mostly through daily immersion rather than formal study.
- As incomes rose in the postwar decades, more families acquired televisions, refrigerators, and eventually second cars, reshaping the rhythm of domestic life.
- The company acquired the startup for its patents, not its talent; to acquire influence in a market sometimes means buying the capability you cannot build fast enough.

这次错在把 acquired 读成"需要"；acquire 是主动获得/购置，强调"拿到手"的过程，不含 need 的含义。

---

**`self-sufficiency`** *(n.)*

`self`（自身）+ `sufficiency`（足够）。词根 `sufficere` = *sub-*（从下）+ *facere*（做）= 从底部支撑起来，自己供给自己。

- The appeal of self-sufficiency drove many urban professionals to buy rural land and grow their own food, even when the economics made no sense.
- Rapson's design philosophy treated self-sufficiency not as a fringe ideal but as the logical endpoint of technological progress—a household that needed nothing from outside.
- There is a difference between self-sufficiency and self-reliance: self-sufficiency is about material resources, while self-reliance is about psychological independence.

这次错在把 self-sufficiency 译成"个人满足"——sufficiency 是"供给充足/足够"，不是情感上的满足；整个词是经济或生活方式上的自给自足，不依赖外部。

---

**`desirable`** *(adj.)*

词根：拉丁语 *desiderare*（渴望、期盼），来自 *de-* + *sidus*（星辰）——古人仰望星空、期盼征兆，后引申为强烈渴望。

- A corner office with a city view was considered the most desirable perk at the firm, worth more to most partners than a modest salary increase.
- Self-sufficiency was deemed not merely practical but socially desirable in an era when dependence on corporations was increasingly viewed with suspicion.
- The neighborhood became desirable only after the new rail line opened; previously, its affordability was its sole appeal rather than anything inherently desirable about the area.

这次错在把 desirable 译成"有希望的"——hopeful 才是"有希望"；desirable 来自 desire，意思是"值得追求的/令人向往的"，是主观价值判断。

---

**`inevitable`** *(adj.)*

词根：拉丁语 *inevitabilis* = *in-*（不）+ *evitare*（避开），`evitare` 来自 *ex-*（出）+ *vita*（生命/道路）= 无路可走。

- The merger was widely seen as inevitable once the smaller firm began losing market share; the question was never whether but when.
- Rapson believed the mechanization of the home was not a distant possibility but an inevitable transformation that would reshape how Americans lived within a generation.
- Climate scientists distinguish between what is already inevitable due to past emissions and what remains avoidable—conflating the two leads to either false panic or false comfort.

这次 inevitable 本身没译错，但和 desirable 并列出现时两个词义差异要清楚：desirable 是"值得要"，inevitable 是"必然来"——Rapson 认为自给自足既是理想又是必然。

## Phrases

**`may have + past participle`** *(情态完成式)*

结构：`may/might + have + V-ed`，表达对**已发生的事**进行不确定的推断。`may` 表可能性，`have + V-ed` 把时间锁定到过去。整体读作：当时那件事，现在回头看，很可能是……（但不确定）。

- The original architects may have underestimated the building's load-bearing requirements, which would explain why structural repairs were needed so soon after completion.
- Rapson may have mispredicted the direction of domestic technology, but his core intuition—that the American home would be fundamentally transformed—proved correct.
- She may have misread the room entirely, or she may have known exactly what she was doing; either way, the result was the same kind of calculated disruption.

这个结构是"对过去的推测"，区别于 `may + V`（对现在/未来的推测）；翻译时译成"或许……了/可能……了"，时态感要带出来。

---

**`both...and...`** *(并列两个并重的成分)*

结构：`both A and B`，强调 A 和 B 都成立，两者并重，不分主次。`both` 锁定"两个都"，否定任何"只是其中一个"的读法。放在形容词前（`both desirable and inevitable`）时，两个形容词地位完全对等，共同修饰同一件事。

- The policy was attacked as both ineffective and unconstitutional, giving opponents two separate grounds on which to challenge it in court.
- His belief that automation was both desirable and inevitable shaped every design decision he made—function and futurity were never in tension for him.
- She found the assignment both challenging and rewarding, which is different from finding something merely difficult or merely satisfying.

这次断在后半段主干没读出来：`his belief that...was both desirable and inevitable` 是 that 从句内容，`was widely shared` 才是主句谓语——看到 `his belief that` 要预期后面还有一个谓语说的是"这个信念本身"。
