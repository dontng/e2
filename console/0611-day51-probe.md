## 原句


> Although the Supreme Court of the United States had prohibited intentional racial discrimination in jury selection as early as the 1880 case of Strauder v. West Virginia, the practice of selecting so-called elite or blue-ribbon juries provided a convenient way around this and other antidiscrimination laws.

## 我的翻译


尽管美国的最高法院（后面不知道咋翻译）

## 评分


**1 / 10**

采分点（5点）：
- △ [Although + Supreme Court of the United States] → 写了"尽管美国的最高法院" · 识别了让步从句开头，但"的"字多余，且后文全部缺失
- ✗ [had prohibited + intentional racial discrimination] → 未翻译 · 主句谓语与核心语义均缺失
- ✗ [as early as + the 1880 case] → 未翻译 · 时间状语"早在"和具体案例名称均未体现
- ✗ [blue-ribbon juries / so-called elite] → 未翻译 · 关键文化词汇缺失
- ✗ [a convenient way around + antidiscrimination laws] → 未翻译 · 主句结论部分全部缺失
